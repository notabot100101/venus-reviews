Best Rabbit Vibrators #

A rabbit is a dual-stimulation vibrator: an insertable shaft curved toward the G-spot and a shorter external arm that vibrates against the clitoris at the same time. It is the format most people picture when they think of a vibrator, and the one with the biggest fit caveat: the distance between the shaft and the arm is fixed, so whether the arm lands where it should depends on anatomy, which is why the better designs give the arm some flex. How to pick within the category #

If you want the arm to stay in contact as you move: the We-Vibe Nova 2. Its flexible external arm is built to bend and keep clitoral contact rather than lifting off, it is rated IPX7 with a clear body-safe materials statement, and the We-Vibe app adds remote control, at 149 EUR.

If you want a tip that moves rather than only vibrates: the LELO Soraya Wave. Its WaveMotion shaft rises and falls in a finger-like motion against the front wall while the arm vibrates, and LELO publishes the fullest spec sheet of the four: 218 x 72 x 46 mm, 185 g, a 110 mm insertable length, a 50 dB maximum, 100% waterproof and a 2-year warranty, at a 259 EUR list price (199 EUR on promotion when checked).

If you want the WaveMotion tip in a smaller, lighter body: the LELO Ina Wave 2. The same rising-and-falling WaveMotion shaft and clitoral arm as the Soraya Wave, driven by two synchronised motors, in a 200 x 65 x 45 mm, 176 g body with a 110 mm insertable length, 12 settings, 100% waterproof and a 2-year warranty, at a 199 EUR list price, 60 EUR below the Soraya Wave; LELO publishes no noise figure for it.

If you want dual stimulation at the lowest price with real documentation: the Fun Factory Miss Bi. A silicone rabbit with a documented 173 x 76 x 38 mm body, 230 g, IPX7, USB-C charging and 12 programmes at 53.95 EUR, though Fun Factory publishes no runtime, charge time or warranty term.

If you want a classic firm rabbit with the widest speed range: the Lovehoney Desire. Lovehoney’s own-brand rabbit in latex- and phthalate-free silicone, submersible, with 8 patterns each adjustable across 12 speeds and a 254 mm body with 127 mm insertable, at 99.95 EUR; Lovehoney publishes no runtime, charge time, weight or warranty term, so it rates below the better-documented rabbits.

The two LELO WaveMotion rabbits are compared head-to-head in Soraya Wave vs Ina Wave 2.

Dual stimulation does not have to mean a rabbit: the LELO Enigma and Enigma Cruise pair sonic external stimulation with a G-spot arm, the LELO Ida Wave rotates its internal arm under app control, and the Womanizer Duo 2 pairs air-pulse with a G-spot vibrator. Frequently Asked Questions

What is a rabbit vibrator?

A dual-stimulation vibrator with an insertable shaft aimed at the G-spot and a shorter external arm that rests on the clitoris, so both are stimulated at once. The catch is fit: the distance between shaft and arm is fixed, so whether the arm lands where it should depends on anatomy.

Which rabbit vibrator is the best overall?

Three share the top rabbit rating of 4.0: the We-Vibe Nova 2 at 149 EUR, with a flexible arm built to keep contact and app control, and the two LELO WaveMotion rabbits, the Soraya Wave at 259 EUR list (199 EUR on promotion when checked) and the smaller, lighter Ina Wave 2 at 199 EUR, whose tips move in a finger-like motion and whose spec sheets are the most complete. The Fun Factory Miss Bi at 53.95 EUR is the value pick.

Has Venus tested these rabbit vibrators?

No. Every review is based on the manufacturer's official product page and published specifications, with the date we checked each one stated on the page. Where a value is not published we say so rather than guessing.
