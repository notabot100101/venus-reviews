Yes. Grid trading is not risk-free. We have stop-loss limits, but markets can move fast. Only invest what you can afford to lose.

Depends on volatility. 10–40 trades per month is typical. You see every trade in your daily report.

Our system has failsafes. If anything goes wrong, it auto-cancels all open orders. You stay safe.
