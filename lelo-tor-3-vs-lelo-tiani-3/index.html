LELO Tor 3 vs LELO Tiani 3 #

These are LELO’s two couples’ toys, and they take different approaches to the same goal of shared stimulation during sex. The Tor 3 is a vibrating ring: simple, worn at the base, fully waterproof. The Tiani 3 is a worn wearable: a C-shape held internally and externally, with a motion remote for control at range. The choice is about format and features, not price, since they sit within 8 EUR of each other.

Both reviews are built from LELO’s official specifications, not physical testing. Prices and specs were verified on the dates stated in each review.

What each one does better #

The Tor 3 is the simpler, more robust choice. A ring worn at the base has fewer placement variables than a worn internal-external device, and it is the only one of the two LELO documents as 100% waterproof, so it is the pick for shower use and the easiest to clean. Control is through two physical buttons plus the LELO app for custom patterns.

The Tiani 3 is the more feature-rich choice. Its SenseMotion remote is motion-sensitive and works over a documented 12 m range, so a partner can steer intensity from across the room with a flick of the wrist, and at a documented 50 dB it is the quieter of the two. The trade-offs are that it is a worn wearable, so fit matters more, LELO does not document a waterproof rating, and the remote itself runs on two AAA batteries rather than recharging.

Which to buy #

Choose the Tor 3 if you want the simplest format, full waterproofing and the easiest fit, and you are happy controlling it by button and app. Choose the Tiani 3 if you want a worn wearable with hands-off control at range and the quietest operation, and you can treat it as splash-clean rather than submersible.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Tor 3 and the Tiani 3?

Both are LELO couples' toys, but the format differs. The Tor 3 is a vibrating ring worn at the base during sex, mechanically simple and documented as 100% waterproof. The Tiani 3 is a worn C-shape held internally and externally, with a motion-sensitive SenseMotion remote and a 12 m range, but no documented waterproof rating.

Which is better value?

Both rate 4.0 and sit close on price: the Tiani 3 at 151.99 EUR and the Tor 3 at 159.00 EUR. The Tiani 3 adds the motion remote and is quieter; the Tor 3 adds full waterproofing and a simpler fit. Neither is clearly cheaper for what it does.

Which is quieter?

The Tiani 3, at a documented 50 dB maximum, against the Tor 3's 60 dB. Both figures come from LELO's own specification blocks.
