Frequently Asked Questions

What does a premium vibrator buy you over a budget one?

Not safety: every documented-material product on our budget page is body-safe. The premium tier buys quieter motors, app and remote control, documented waterproofing, longer warranties and specialist formats like dual or air-pulse stimulation. If you only need the core sensation, the budget tier is enough.

Which is the best premium pick overall?

The LELO Enigma at 199 EUR, our top premium pick, combines external sonic and internal G-spot stimulation in one 100% waterproof body with LELO's 2-year plus 10-year warranty structure. For couples, the We-Vibe Chorus at 209 EUR is the equivalent flagship.

Is a premium price always worth it?

Only when you want what it buys. A premium format you will actually use, like air-pulse or dual stimulation, justifies the price; paying more for a basic single-motor device does not. Match the pick to the use case rather than the price tag.
