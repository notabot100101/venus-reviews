Privacy Policy

How Venus Reviews handles analytics, contact-form messages, outbound retailer links, and adult privacy.

Privacy Policy #

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Venus Reviews is an editorial review and buying-guide site for adults. We do not operate a shop, process payments, ship products, or manage retailer accounts. This policy explains what information may be handled when you read the site, use the contact form, or click an outbound retailer link.

Information We Collect #

We keep collection intentionally limited:

  • Basic technical information that a web server receives when pages are requested, such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, referrer, and timestamp
  • Privacy-friendly aggregate analytics through Plausible, used to understand which pages are useful without building personal profiles
  • Information you choose to send through the contact form, such as your email address, subject, priority, and message
  • Outbound-link click events used to understand whether retailer links are working and useful

We do not ask for sensitive personal details, health information, payment details, shipping addresses, account passwords, or order numbers. If you buy from a retailer, that retailer handles the checkout, account, payment, shipping, returns, and support relationship under its own privacy policy.

Contact Form #

The contact form is processed by Formspree. When you submit it, the details you enter are sent to the Venus Reviews team so we can respond. Do not include intimate personal details, medical information, payment data, or retailer account credentials in the form.

Analytics And Cookies #

Venus Reviews uses Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused analytics service designed to work without tracking cookies. We use aggregate page and outbound-link statistics to maintain the site and improve reviews. We do not use analytics to identify individual readers.

If approved affiliate links are added later, the destination retailer or affiliate network may set its own tracking cookies after you click through. Those cookies are controlled by the destination service, not by Venus Reviews. Active affiliate links are disclosed on the page where they appear.

Affiliate And Retailer Links #

Some pages discuss retailers or products. Outbound retailer links appear only when there is a real destination URL and the link is appropriate for the page. If a link is an approved affiliate link, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Editorial conclusions are not based on commission rates.

How We Use Information #

We use limited site and contact information to:

  • Keep the site secure and functioning
  • Respond to messages and correction requests
  • Improve page structure, reviews, and buying guides
  • Check whether outbound links are working
  • Maintain affiliate disclosure and compliance records

We do not sell personal information.

Data Sharing #

We share information only where needed to operate the site:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that serve the website
  • Plausible for aggregate analytics
  • Formspree for contact-form handling
  • Retailers or affiliate networks only after you choose to click an outbound link
  • Legal or security recipients if required to protect the site or comply with a lawful obligation

Retention #

Contact-form messages are kept only as long as needed to respond, resolve a correction, maintain compliance records, or handle abuse. Aggregate analytics may be retained for trend reporting because it does not identify individual readers.

Adult Privacy #

Because this site covers sexual wellness products, privacy matters. Venus Reviews avoids account systems, comment sections, personal profiles, and unnecessary data collection. You can read the site without creating an account.

Your Choices #

You can block analytics scripts in your browser, avoid the contact form, or contact a retailer directly instead of using outbound links. If you want a contact-form message deleted or corrected, send a follow-up through the contact page with enough context for us to identify it.

Contact #

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
