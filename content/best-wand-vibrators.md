## Best Wand Vibrators

A wand vibrator puts the motor in a rounded head on the end of a handle, so the vibration spreads over a wide contact area and reaches deeper than a small bullet. It is the broad-contact end of the external category: the pick for people who find pinpoint devices too focused, or who want a handle long enough to reach comfortably. Classic wands plug into the mains; all three wands we have reviewed are cordless and rechargeable, which removes the cable at the cost of a finite runtime. ### How to pick within the category

**If you want the fullest spec sheet:** the [LELO Smart Wand 2 Medium](/products/lelo-smart-wand-2-medium/). LELO publishes everything except a noise figure: body-safe silicone over ABS, 210 x 85 x 45 mm, 155 g, 10 settings, 100% waterproof, up to two hours of use from a two-hour charge, a 2-year warranty and a 10-year quality guarantee on top. It documents only a three-button interface, with no app or remote.

**If you want app and partner control:** the [We-Vibe Wand 2](/products/we-vibe-wand-2/). A flexible, ridged head, more than ten intensity levels, Smart Silence that only runs the motor on skin contact, IPX7, a documented 120-minute runtime from a 150-minute charge, a remote in the box and the We-Vibe app. We-Vibe publishes no dimensions, weight or noise figure for it.

**If you want the wand format for the least money:** the [Fun Factory VIM](/products/fun-factory-vim/). A German-made weighted-motor wand in body-safe silicone, IPX7 and USB-C rechargeable at 66.95 EUR, less than half the price of the other two. Fun Factory does not publish its runtime, charge time, weight, dimensions or warranty term, and it was out of stock at the manufacturer when its page was last checked.
