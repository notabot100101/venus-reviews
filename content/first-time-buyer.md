## First-Time Buyer Guide

Buying a first pleasure product is mostly about avoiding two mistakes: unsafe materials, and paying premium prices for features you cannot evaluate yet. This page is the short path. The full decision framework lives in the [how to choose guide](/guides/how-to-choose/).

### Decide These Three Things First

1. **Budget.** Entry products run 50–80 EUR; mid-range 80–150 EUR brings better motors and body-safe silicone as standard. As a first buyer you rarely benefit from going above that.
2. **Noise tolerance.** Thin walls or shared housing make this the deciding factor. Under 40 dB is barely audible a few feet away; our [noise and discretion guide](/guides/noise-and-discretion/) maps the ranges.
3. **Material, always.** Medical-grade silicone, ABS plastic, glass or stainless steel. Nothing porous, nothing undisclosed. The [body-safe materials guide](/guides/body-safe-materials/) explains how to verify a listing's claims.

### Before First Use

Clean it, every time, starting before the first use: warm water and mild unscented soap, dried completely, stored clean and separate. The [cleaning and maintenance guide](/guides/cleaning-maintenance/) covers cadence and which cleaners damage which materials. With silicone products, water-based lubricant is the conservative default.
