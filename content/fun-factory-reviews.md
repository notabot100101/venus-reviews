### How to choose a Fun Factory

**For broad, deep massage**, the [VIM](/products/fun-factory-vim/) is the weighted-motor wand: its heavier motor delivers the rumbly, low-frequency sensation wand users look for, over a broad contact head rather than a point.

**For precise contact**, the [Volta](/products/fun-factory-volta/) uses two flexible tips that can cradle or pinpoint, a more exploratory feel than a single flat head, at the lowest 4.0-rated price in the range.

**For a flexible wraparound shape**, the [Manta](/products/fun-factory-manta/) bends around contours and is the most affordable of the four, though its 3.5 rating reflects a more niche shape that suits some users better than others.

**For hands-free internal thrusting**, the [Stronic G](/products/fun-factory-stronic/) is the odd one out: a pulsator that thrusts by itself rather than vibrating, built for people whose G-spot responds to motion rather than buzz. The [Stronic Real](/products/fun-factory-stronic-real/) is its realistic-shaped sibling: the same self-thrusting motor with 7 strengths and 3 rhythms, a documented 202 x 40 x 35 mm body at 275 g, IPX7, USB-C and a 2-hour runtime at 67.95 EUR, with no charge time or warranty published. Both are ranked among the insertables on the [best G-spot & internal vibrators](/best-g-spot-vibrators/) page.

**For a lay-on external at the lowest price**, the [Laya III](/products/fun-factory-laya-iii/) is the palm-sized body that vibrates and taps at once: a documented 107 x 50 x 41 mm, 85 g, 7 levels and 3 patterns, IPX7 and USB-C at 32.95 EUR, with no runtime, charge time or warranty published and only Black in stock when checked.

**For couples' foreplay**, the [Be One](/products/fun-factory-be-one/) is the finger-worn vibrator: two motors, 4 speeds and a random rhythm, a button lock, IPX7, USB-C, 28 g and a 60-minute runtime at 33.95 EUR, the cheapest powered device in the catalogue; Fun Factory names the material only as body-friendly and non-porous.

**For a large, girthy G-spot vibrator**, the [Big Boss](/products/fun-factory-big-boss/) is the XL pick: a documented 230 x 76 x 45 mm silicone body at 350 g with 6 speeds and 6 patterns, IPX7 and USB-C at 49.95 EUR, the cheapest Fun Factory in the catalogue; runtime, charge time and warranty are not published, and the Black colour was sold out when checked.

**For dual stimulation**, the [Miss Bi](/products/fun-factory-miss-bi/) is the rabbit: a filling G-spot shaft and a flexible clitoral arm with 12 programmes, IPX7, USB-C and the LOOP handle at 53.95 EUR, the cheapest dual device in the catalogue. Runtime, charge time and warranty are not documented. It is ranked with the other rabbits on the [best rabbit vibrators](/best-rabbit-vibrators/) page.
