### How to choose a Fun Factory

**For broad, deep massage**, the [VIM](/products/fun-factory-vim/) is the weighted-motor wand: its heavier motor delivers the rumbly, low-frequency sensation wand users look for, over a broad contact head rather than a point.

**For precise contact**, the [Volta](/products/fun-factory-volta/) uses two flexible tips that can cradle or pinpoint, a more exploratory feel than a single flat head, at the lowest 4.0-rated price in the range.

**For a flexible wraparound shape**, the [Manta](/products/fun-factory-manta/) bends around contours and is the most affordable of the three, though its 3.5 rating reflects a more niche shape that suits some users better than others.
