## We-Vibe Chorus vs We-Vibe Sync

Both of these are wearable couples toys: worn by one partner during sex so both feel the vibration, controlled by remote or app. The Chorus is We-Vibe's flagship; the Sync is the older, simpler, cheaper version. The choice is about how much control and polish you want.

### What the extra 50 EUR buys

The Chorus is the more complete product. Its Squeeze Remote responds to how hard you grip it, the touch-sense feature adjusts to movement, and the materials carry an explicit phthalate-free, BPA-free and latex-free statement. The trade-off is setup: app pairing and remote anchoring add friction, and like any wearable couples toy, comfort depends on anatomy and position.

The Sync does the same core job, a C-shape worn during sex with remote and app control, for 50 EUR less. It is smaller and simpler, but its materials disclosure is narrower, and when we checked it was out of stock on the manufacturer page, which can be a phase-out signal. Confirm availability before counting on it.

### Which to buy

Choose the **Chorus** if you want the fullest control and the clearest materials documentation, and you will actually use the app and remote. Choose the **Sync** if you want the same wearable couples format for less and can confirm it is still in stock.
