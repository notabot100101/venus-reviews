## LELO Enigma vs LELO Sona 2

These are two of LELO's sonic devices, and both earn our top LELO rating of 4.5, so the choice is not about quality, it is about format. The Enigma does two things at once: external sonic stimulation plus internal G-spot vibration. The Sona 2 does one thing well: focused external sonic stimulation. The price gap, 199 EUR against 109 EUR, is the cost of that second function.

Both reviews are built from LELO's official specifications, not physical testing, on the dates stated in each review.

### What the extra 90 EUR buys

The Enigma is the dual device. Its larger body carries both an external sonic surface and an insertable G-spot arm documented at 140 mm, with a bigger 850 mAh battery to drive both. That is the whole reason for the price: simultaneous external and internal stimulation in one body, which the Sona 2 cannot do. The trade-off is size and commitment, it is a larger, more purposeful shape that LELO itself frames as a device for people who already want dual stimulation, not a first toy.

The Sona 2 is the focused specialist. It is smaller, lighter, and built around a single 23 mm sonic mouth with 12 intensity modes and a documented 60 dB maximum. For external-only sonic stimulation it earns the same 4.5 rating as the Enigma at nearly half the price, which makes it the better buy unless you specifically want the internal arm.

### Which to buy

Choose the **Enigma** if you want external and internal stimulation together and you are confident dual stimulation is what you want. Choose the **Sona 2** if focused external sonic stimulation is the goal, since you get the same rating for 90 EUR less.
