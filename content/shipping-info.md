## Buying Through Retailer Links

Venus Reviews does not operate a store. We publish reviews, comparisons, and retailer context so readers can decide where to buy.

If you click a retailer link and place an order, the sale is between you and that retailer. Shipping prices, delivery estimates, tracking, customs or duties, return rules, warranty help, payment processing, account access, and customer support are all handled by the retailer.

Before checking out, review the retailer's own shipping and returns pages and confirm that the final checkout terms match what you expect for your location.

For more detail, see our [shipping and delivery guidance](/shipping/).
