Dame Fin is a finger-worn external vibrator from Dame, the US brand behind the catalogue's [Pom](/products/dame-pom/), [Arc](/products/dame-arc/), [Com](/products/dame-com/), [Aer](/products/dame-aer/) and [Eva II](/products/dame-eva-ii/). Dame's own Shopify data labels it a "Finger Vibrator," its tagline calls it "Finger Vibrator Designed to Enhance Touch," and it carries badges reading "Like Your Fingers," "Couple Friendly" and "100% Body-safe." At 49.00 USD it is the second-cheapest product in Dame's reviewed range, just above [Dip](/products/dame-dip/) at 44.00 USD.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Dame's official Fin product page and its published specifications, checked on 12 October 2026. Where Dame does not document something, or disagrees with itself, this review says so rather than filling the gap or picking a number.

## Quick Verdict

Fin is the pick for someone who wants a small, inexpensive, finger-worn vibrator for external stimulation, solo or with a partner, rather than an internal or wand-style device. The specification page covers the basics: medical-grade silicone badged 100% body-safe, waterproof construction, USB-C charging with a 1-hour runtime from a 2-hour charge, and a 3-year warranty. The notable caveat is documentary: Dame's own page gives two different intensity-level counts and two different dimension figures for the same product (detailed below). Fin also documents only a single steady pattern, is explicitly external-only per Dame's FAQ, prices in US dollars (49.00 USD), and publishes no usable product weight or decibel figure.

## Format and Who It Is For

Fin is a small, bulbed vibrator built to be worn on the hand rather than held like a conventional vibrator; Dame's own Fin-vs-Pom comparison widget labels its format "External (finger-worn)." Dame's copy describes wearing it "above or below the hand," held "at the tip of your fingers or close to your palm, and with either end of the bulb facing forward." A removable tether loops around the hand for extra security; per Dame's FAQ, "You can use the included tether for more security, or remove it for a quicker way to bring Fin in and out of the action." A single button cycles through its intensity levels. Per Dame's FAQ, Fin is "exclusively for external stimulations of various erogenous zones including the clitoris or penis," not an internal device.

Dame badges Fin "Couple Friendly," and its FAQ and comparison widget both describe partnered use ("Best for first-timers, couples, foreplay enhancement"); buyers comparing worn-during-sex couples' devices can also see the [best couples' toys](/best-couples-toys/) page, though Fin's finger-worn format differs from that hub's wearables and rings. Buyers who want a classic palm-held external should look at the [Pom](/products/dame-pom/); those who want an insertable device should look at [Dip](/products/dame-dip/) or the curved [Arc](/products/dame-arc/); and anyone comparing compact externals generally should see the [best mini and bullet vibrators](/best-mini-vibrators/) page.

## Documented Specifications

Dame's Specifications block lists the material as "Medical Grade Silicone," with a separate "100% Body-safe" badge elsewhere on the page. On intensity levels, Dame's own page is inconsistent: the Specifications block itself states "5 Intensity Levels" - used in this review's spec table, also matched by an on-page product graphic reading "5 Vibration Levels" - but the hero badge above the buy box, and the Fin-vs-Pom comparison widget, both state "3 Intensity Levels" instead. Dame's page does not resolve this itself, so both figures are disclosed here. The Specifications block gives no explicit pattern count; the comparison widget labels Fin "1 Steady Pattern," used here, the same approach taken for the [Dip](/products/dame-dip/) review.

Dimensions carry a similar conflict: the Specifications block states "3\" L (with strap), 1.5\" diameter" (about 76 x 38 mm, used here), while the comparison widget gives "2.75\" L (with strap), 1\" diameter" (about 70 x 25 mm). Power is USB-C rechargeable, run time 1 hour at the highest setting from a 2-hour charge. The box contains the Fin vibrator, a USB-C cable, a storage bag and a user manual; two colors are listed, Citrus and Quartz. Dame publishes no product weight, app, or noise level in decibels; a Shopify variant field lists a "weight" of 235 g for both colors, but this is a shipping figure, not a stated product weight, so it is not used here.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

Fin's body is medical-grade silicone, badged 100% body-safe, non-porous and intended for external intimate use when cleaned correctly. The Specifications block describes it simply as waterproof, without an IP rating. Dame's FAQ recommends mild soap and warm water, or a compatible toy cleaner, and advises against alcohol-, petroleum- or acetone-based cleaners, and against boiling, baking or dishwashing the device. A dot of aloe or water-based lubricant is optional; Dame's guidance is to never use a silicone lubricant on a silicone toy.

## Battery and Warranty

Fin charges over USB-C, with Dame specifying a wall adapter rated at or below 5V and a fully dry device before charging. Run time is 1 hour at the highest setting from a 2-hour charge, matching [Pom's](/products/dame-pom/) battery figures exactly. As with the rest of the reviewed Dame range, the standout figure is the warranty: 3 years, longer than the two-year terms common elsewhere in the category.

## In the Catalogue

### Fin vs. the Pom and the compact externals

Against [Pom](/products/dame-pom/), Dame's own comparison widget pitches Fin as "Best for first-timers, couples, foreplay enhancement" against Pom's "Best for beginners & experienced users wanting flexible external play." Both share medical-grade silicone, USB-C charging, a 2-hour charge time and a 3-year warranty; Pom documents more pattern variety (5 patterns against Fin's 1) and one consistent set of dimensions (83 x 56 x 30 mm) without Fin's conflicts, at a higher US price (103.00 USD against Fin's 49.00 USD).

Against the catalogue's euro-priced compact externals on the [best mini and bullet vibrators](/best-mini-vibrators/) page, the [We-Vibe Tango X](/products/we-vibe-tango-x/) (89.00 EUR) documents 8 intensity levels and 7 modes with IPX7 waterproofing - more than Fin offers either way. Fin's distinguishing feature against that group is format, not spec density: the only finger-worn device among them, at the lowest USD price compared there.

### Fin among the Dame range

Within Dame, Fin is the smallest, most specialized format, built for finger-worn external stimulation; [Dip](/products/dame-dip/) is the affordable internal-and-external vibrator; [Pom](/products/dame-pom/) the flexible, palm-held external; [Arc](/products/dame-arc/) the curved, insertable G-spot vibrator; [Com](/products/dame-com/) the flexible-head wand; [Aer](/products/dame-aer/) the contactless air-pulse stimulator; and [Eva II](/products/dame-eva-ii/) the hands-free wearable. Choosing between them is mostly a question of format, since all six share Dame's medical-grade silicone and multi-year warranty. The full line-up is on the [Dame reviews hub](/dame-reviews/).

## Value Position

At 49.00 USD, Fin is the second-cheapest product in Dame's reviewed range, priced in dollars, not euros, so European buyers should budget for conversion and import duties, or check a local stockist, before ordering directly from Dame. The reason to choose it is specific: a small, documented, medical-grade silicone finger vibrator for external use, with a removable tether and a 3-year warranty, near the lowest entry price in Dame's range - with the caveat that Dame's own page does not give one consistent intensity-level count or dimension figure. Buyers who want a more internally consistent documentation trail should weigh the [Pom](/products/dame-pom/) first; first-time buyers can see the [first-time buyer](/first-time-buyer/) page, and the full catalogue is ranked on the [best-of 2026](/best-vibrators-2026/) page.
