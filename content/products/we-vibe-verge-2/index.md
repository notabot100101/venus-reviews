We-Vibe Verge 2 is a vibrating ring with an extension: the stretchy silicone ring is worn at the base, and a longer vibrating body reaches back along the perineum, so the wearer gets vibration at the base, the testicles and the perineum at once. We-Vibe describes it as a perineum and penis stimulator, controlled by a button or the We-Vibe app. It sits beside the simpler [Pivot 2](/products/we-vibe-pivot-2/) ring and the [LELO Tor 3](/products/lelo-tor-3/) in the catalogue's couples category.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on We-Vibe's official Verge 2 product page and its published specification, feature and in-the-box tabs, checked on 12 October 2026. Where We-Vibe does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Verge 2 is the pick for a wearer who wants a ring that reaches the perineum rather than only the base, with app control, at 99 EUR. The documentation covers safety and ownership: body-safe silicone that is explicitly free of phthalates, BPA and latex, IPX7 waterproofing stated in the specification itself, a lithium-ion polymer battery with a documented runtime of up to 120 minutes from a charge of up to 90 minutes, and a 2-year warranty. The gaps are the same as the Pivot 2's: no dimensions, no inner diameter, no weight, no level or mode count and no noise figure. The format caveat is inherent: a stretch ring is fit-sensitive and a partnered-use device.

## Format and Who It Is For

Verge 2 is worn by one partner at the base, like any vibrating ring, but its vibrating body is elongated so that it rests along the perineum while the ring holds it in place. We-Vibe describes the design as comfortable and elastic, with deep vibrations for the penis, testicles and perineum, and says it stimulates the G-spot, clitoris and penis during partnered sex. Control is the button on the body or the We-Vibe app, which lets a partner adjust the vibration from a phone; no remote is listed.

It suits a wearer who wants perineum stimulation during sex without a separate prostate device, and couples already on the We-Vibe app. It is a partnered-use product, and because the stretch fit is not quantified, buyers who need a specific inner diameter cannot confirm it from the page. For a ring without the extension, the [Pivot 2](/products/we-vibe-pivot-2/) is 10 EUR cheaper; for aimed prostate stimulation, the [Loki Wave 2](/products/lelo-loki-wave-2/) and [Hugo 2](/products/lelo-hugo-2/) are the dedicated devices.

## Documented Specifications

We-Vibe's specification tab files the Verge 2 under rings, wearables and app-enabled toys, as a vibrating perineum and penis stimulator in black, waterproof to IPX7, with a USB-rechargeable lithium-ion polymer battery, a runtime of up to 120 minutes and a charge time of up to 90 minutes. The materials are body-safe silicone, free of phthalates and BPA and made without latex, and the warranty is 2 years.

The feature tab adds the elastic design, the triple-stimulation claim, body-safe materials, USB charging, a whisper-quiet description without a decibel figure, deep vibrations and the We-Vibe app. The in-the-box tab lists the Verge 2, a USB cable with no adapter, a quick-start guide and safety notes. We-Vibe does not publish dimensions, an inner diameter, a weight, a count of intensity levels or modes, or a noise level for the Verge 2.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

The ring and its extension are body-safe silicone with an explicit phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free statement, which matters for a device in close contact with both partners and the perineum. It is rated IPX7, so it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath or shower within the limits of that rating. The junction between ring and extension is the place to rinse carefully. Clean it after every use, dry it fully, and store it away from other silicone toys. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with a silicone ring.

## Battery and Warranty

The documented figures are up to 120 minutes of use from a charge of up to 90 minutes, charged over a USB cable from any USB port or adapter. The warranty is 2 years, and We-Vibe shows its 100-day pleasure guarantee site-wide; confirm the current terms before relying on either.
