Womanizer Liberty 2 is the travel model in the Pleasure Air range: a compact air-pulse stimulator that ships with a magnetic cover and a lock function so it can go in a bag hygienically and without switching itself on. It sits between the entry [Starlet 3](/products/womanizer-starlet-3/) and the flagship [Premium 2](/products/womanizer-premium-2/) and [Next](/products/womanizer-next/), and it is the current generation of the Liberty line; Womanizer's Liberty page now redirects to it.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Womanizer's official Liberty 2 product page and its published specification table, checked on 10 and 11 October 2026. Where Womanizer does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Liberty 2 is the pick for Pleasure Air that travels. For 99 EUR the specification is complete: a body-safe silicone stimulation head on an ABS body, 8 intensity levels, IPX7 waterproofing, a documented 120-minute runtime from a 60-minute charge with the 160 mAh battery capacity published, exact dimensions of 105 x 50 x 40 mm at 90 g, four colours, and the same 5-year warranty as the flagships. The magnetic cover and lock are the reason to choose it over the cheaper Starlet 3. The trade-offs are the ones you would expect below the flagships: 8 levels rather than 14, no Smart Silence or Autopilot, and no published noise figure.

## Format and Who It Is For

Liberty 2 is an external-only air-pulse stimulator. Its mouth seals over the clitoris and pulses air rather than vibrating against it, with an 8-step intensity range controlled on the device. What sets it apart is the magnetic cover: it snaps over the mouth for hygienic transport, and the lock function stops the device switching on in a bag, which is why Womanizer markets it as the travel companion of the range.

It suits people who want the genuine Womanizer mechanism in a compact, protected form for travel or discreet storage, with a proper runtime. Anyone who wants the full feature set, Smart Silence, Autopilot and finer intensity control, should look at the Premium 2 or Next; anyone purely price-driven can start with the Starlet 3.

## Documented Specifications

Womanizer's specification table lists a body of ABS plastic with a stimulation head of body-safe silicone. The device measures 105 x 50 x 40 mm and weighs 90 g, and it comes in Dark Petrol, Violet, Vibrant Rose and Sage. It offers 8 intensity levels and is rated IPX7.

Power is a rechargeable lithium-ion battery of 3.7 V and 160 mAh, one of the few in the catalogue where the capacity is published, giving a documented 120 minutes of runtime from a 60-minute charge. The magnetic cover and lock function are documented features. Womanizer does not publish a noise level, and Smart Silence and Autopilot are not listed for this model.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

The stimulation head, the part that contacts the body, is body-safe silicone, and the body is ABS plastic. The IPX7 rating means the device can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath or shower. Clean the mouth and silicone head carefully, since fluids collect in an air-pulse opening, dry fully, and only then fit the magnetic cover; the cover keeps the clean head covered in transit, which is its whole point. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone.

## Battery and Warranty

A 120-minute runtime from a 60-minute charge is a strong ratio, four times the Starlet 3's runtime and matching the Premium 2, and Womanizer publishes the 160 mAh battery capacity behind it. The warranty is 5 years plus the 100-day pleasure guarantee, the same cover as the flagships. Confirm the current terms of the guarantee before relying on it.
