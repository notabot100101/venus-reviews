Womanizer Next is the brand's flagship external air-pulse stimulator: the model that introduces 3D Pleasure Air, which pulses air at the clitoris from more than one direction, alongside a Climax Control feature, Smart Silence, Afterglow and an Autopilot mode. It sits above the [Womanizer Premium 2](/products/womanizer-premium-2/) in the range and is the most completely specified contactless device in the catalogue.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Womanizer's official Next product page and its published specification table, checked on 10 October 2026. Where Womanizer does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Next is the pick for someone who wants the most advanced air-pulse device available and is willing to pay for it. The documentation is unusually complete: 3D Pleasure Air with Climax Control, 14 intensity levels, Smart Silence that only runs on skin contact, Afterglow and Autopilot, body-safe silicone that is explicitly phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free, IPX7 waterproofing, a 240-minute runtime from a 120-minute charge, and a 5-year warranty. That runtime is the longest of any device we have reviewed. The caveats are price, at 209 EUR the most expensive air-pulse device here, an unstated noise figure, and a published weight of 480 g that is hard to interpret for a handheld and may be packaged weight.

## Format and Who It Is For

Next is an external-only air-pulse stimulator with no insertable part. Its mouth seals over the clitoris and pulses air, and the 3D Pleasure Air system is Womanizer's claim that the pulses arrive from multiple directions rather than straight on, for a fuller sensation. Climax Control lets the user shape the build, Autopilot runs preset intensity sequences hands-off, Smart Silence keeps the device silent and still until it touches skin, and Afterglow drops to a gentle setting after climax with a long press.

It suits people who already know they respond to air-pulse and want the top of the range, and people who value long sessions between charges. Anyone who wants internal stimulation should look at the [Womanizer Duo 2](/products/womanizer-duo-2/) instead, and anyone unsure whether air-pulse suits them should start cheaper.

## Documented Specifications

Womanizer's specification table lists a stimulation head and body of body-safe silicone, described in the feature copy as soft-touch silicone free of phthalates, BPA and latex. The device measures 17.1 x 5.4 x 5.7 cm and is offered in black, dark violet and sage green. The table gives a weight of 480 g; that figure is unusually high for a handheld stimulator and may represent packaged weight, so this review reports it as published rather than treating it as the in-hand weight.

Power is rechargeable, with a documented runtime of 240 minutes from a 120-minute charge. The device offers 14 intensity levels, with Smart Silence, Afterglow, Autopilot and Climax Control all listed as features. Womanizer does not publish a maximum noise level, a battery capacity or an auto-off timer for the Next.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

The contact surfaces are body-safe silicone with an explicit phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free statement, which is a clear materials disclosure. Next is rated IPX7, so it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath or shower. As with all air-pulse devices, the mouth and the stimulation head are the areas to clean most carefully, since fluids collect there; rinse thoroughly and dry fully before storage. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone.

## Battery and Warranty

The 240-minute runtime is the standout number: four hours from a two-hour charge, double what most devices in the catalogue document. Womanizer documents a 5-year warranty plus its 100-day pleasure guarantee, among the longest cover in the catalogue and a genuine point of confidence at this price. Confirm the current terms of the guarantee before relying on it.
