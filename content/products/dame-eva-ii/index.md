Dame Eva II, now presented by Dame as the Eva Wearable Vibrator, is the catalogue's compact wearable option for partner-facing external stimulation. It is not a rabbit, not an insertable G-spot vibrator, and not a broad wand. Its core promise is narrower: a small, winged clitoral vibrator designed to stay in place during partnered sex while leaving both partners' hands free. Dame's Specifications block documents medical-grade, 100% body-safe silicone, 3 intensity levels, a 60-minute runtime from a 4-hour charge, fully measured dimensions and a 3-year warranty.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Dame's official Eva product page, its published Specifications block and its published variant data, checked on 13 October 2026. Where Dame does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Eva suits couples who want external stimulation during partnered sex without one person holding a bullet in place, and who are comfortable with a fit-dependent wearable device rather than a handheld one. Dame documents a 60-minute runtime from a 4-hour charge, a 3-year warranty, medical-grade 100% body-safe silicone, 3 intensity levels, and fully measured dimensions (33 mm body width, 61 mm wings width, 43 mm height, 28 mm depth), at 129.00 USD with both the Papaya and Ice colourways in stock when checked. The trade-off is pattern variety - Dame's own comparison widget documents only a single steady vibration pattern - and the same format dependency that applies to any wearable: if the wings do not sit well for a particular body or position, the best-documented specs matter less.

## Format and Who It Is For

Eva is a wearable external vibrator: small, flexible silicone wings tuck under the labia and hold a vibrating body against the clitoris, designed to stay in place during penetrative sex rather than requiring a hand to hold it. Dame's instructions describe squeezing the wings and tucking them into place, noting that even an imperfect fit can often be held with light fingertip pressure or pressure between bodies during sex. A single control adjusts between 3 intensity levels.

It suits couples who already know clitoral vibration works for them and want to add hands-free stimulation to partnered sex, and solo users who want a compact, discreet external vibrator. It is a less compelling choice for anyone who wants multiple vibration patterns, deep internal stimulation, or a guaranteed one-size-fits-all fit - Dame's own marketing and the product's wearable format both depend on individual anatomy and position.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

Eva's body is medical-grade silicone, badged 100% body-safe, non-porous and appropriate for external intimate use when cleaned correctly. The Specifications block describes it simply as waterproof, without an IP rating, which is the same qualitative claim Dame uses for the Dip page. Clean with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and dry fully before returning Eva to its inductive charging case - Dame's general guidance for its devices advises against alcohol-, petroleum- or acetone-based cleaners and against boiling, baking or dishwashing. Use a water-based lubricant as the default with silicone.

The winged shape creates edges around the wings and the body-to-wing junction that need attention during cleaning, more than a smooth, single-surface external vibrator would. Because Eva is designed to be worn during partnered sex, drying it fully before storage and before its next use is worth the extra step.

## Battery and Warranty

Eva charges through an inductive charging case rather than a port on the device itself; a USB cable charges the case, and the device sits in the case to charge. The documented run time is 1 hour at the highest setting from a 4-hour charge - a shorter runtime and longer charge time than the Dip page's 1.5-hour runtime from a 2-hour charge, though Eva's case-based charging method is not directly comparable to Dip's direct USB-C port. The standout figure is the warranty: Dame documents 3 years for Eva, matching the Dip and Arc pages and longer than the two-year terms common elsewhere in the category.

## In the Catalogue

### Eva vs. the Dame Pom

Dame's own comparison widget sets Eva directly against the [Pom](/products/dame-pom/) palm vibrator: Pom documents 5 intensity levels and 5 vibration patterns against Eva's 3 intensity levels and single steady pattern, a 2-hour charge against Eva's 4 hours, and larger dimensions (3.25 x 2.2 x 1.2 in., about 83 x 56 x 30 mm) against Eva's smaller, wing-dependent footprint. The two serve different purposes: Pom is a handheld external vibrator usable solo or with a partner, while Eva is built specifically to be worn hands-free during penetrative sex. Buyers who want pattern variety and a handheld shape should choose Pom; buyers who specifically want hands-free wear should choose Eva.

### Eva vs. the other wearable and couples toys

Against other partner-facing devices in the catalogue, the [We-Vibe Chorus](/products/we-vibe-chorus/) and [We-Vibe Match](/products/we-vibe-match/) are app-connected, internally wearable couples vibrators rather than externally worn devices, a different mechanism entirely from Eva's winged, hands-free design. Eva's documentation advantage over those products, where comparable, is its published runtime, charge time and 3-year warranty; its trade-off is a single vibration pattern against their app-controlled pattern libraries.

### Eva among the Dame range

Within Dame, Eva is the brand's hands-free wearable, distinct from the handheld [Pom](/products/dame-pom/), the entry-level [Dip](/products/dame-dip/), the curved [Arc](/products/dame-arc/), the flexible-head [Com](/products/dame-com/), and the flexible [Fin](/products/dame-fin/). All of Dame's reviewed devices share medical-grade silicone and a multi-year warranty; choosing between them is a question of format and fit. The full line-up is on the [Dame reviews hub](/dame-reviews/).

## Value Position

At 129.00 USD, Eva is a mid-range purchase within Dame's catalogue, priced in dollars rather than euros, so European buyers should budget for currency conversion and check for import duties before ordering directly from Dame. The documentation now supports that price better than this page previously reflected: a 60-minute runtime, a 4-hour charge time, fully measured dimensions and a 3-year warranty are all confirmed directly from Dame's Specifications block, alongside the already-known medical-grade silicone and 3 intensity levels. The genuine trade-off is pattern variety - a single steady pattern, documented only through Dame's own comparison widget - and the inherent fit-dependency of any wearable design. Buyers who want multiple patterns or a handheld shape should weigh the [Pom](/products/dame-pom/) first; the full catalogue is ranked on the [best-of 2026](/best-vibrators-2026/) page.
