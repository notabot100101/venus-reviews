LELO Ina Wave 2 is the catalogue's second WaveMotion rabbit after the [LELO Soraya Wave](/products/lelo-soraya-wave/). Like the Soraya Wave, it pairs an insertable shaft whose tip rises and falls against the G-spot with a smaller external arm for the clitoris, driven by what LELO describes as two synchronized motors. On documented values it is shorter, narrower and lighter than the Soraya Wave, offers 12 modes to its 8, and carries the same 199 EUR list price. This page covers the "2" model only.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO's official Ina Wave 2 product page and its published specification block, checked on 12 October 2026. Where LELO does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Ina Wave 2 is the pick for someone who wants LELO's moving-tip rabbit in a smaller package than the Soraya Wave at the same price. The specification block is nearly complete, from materials and full dimensions through battery, 12 modes, 100% waterproofing and a two-year warranty backed by a ten-year quality guarantee. The caveats are the 199 EUR price, no published noise figure, no documented app or remote control, and the fixed geometry that makes every rabbit fit-sensitive.

## Format and Who It Is For

Ina Wave 2 is a rabbit: an insertable shaft for the G-spot and a shorter external arm for the clitoris, designed to work together. What distinguishes it from a conventional rabbit is WaveMotion, LELO's name for the mechanism that moves the tip of the shaft rather than only vibrating it. LELO's how-to-use steps give two ways to use it: thrust the shaft manually, or switch on the WaveMotion feature so the tip rises and falls against the G-spot on its own. The page attributes the motion to two synchronized motors and says the device "moves like a subtle wave".

The right buyer wants a come-hither internal motion with clitoral contact, prefers a slightly smaller rabbit than the Soraya Wave, and is happy with five buttons rather than an app. Anyone who needs a documented noise level, app control or an external-only device should look elsewhere in the catalogue.

## Documented Specifications

LELO lists the materials as body-safe silicone and ABS plastic, with a feature tile adding "extra-soft silicone". The overall size is 200 x 65 x 45 mm, the insertable length 110 mm, the diameter 37.09 mm and the weight 176 g; the Soraya Wave's page publishes no diameter.

Power is a Li-ion 520 mAh 3.7 V cell, charged in 2 hours at 5.0 V and 500 mA, for up to 2 hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby. The frequency is 60 Hz, the device switches off automatically after 20 minutes, and the interface is five buttons. The specification block records 12 modes, matched by a "12 settings" tile running "from a teasing murmur to a satisfying pulse"; LELO does not split these into intensity levels and patterns, so this page records 12 modes and nothing finer.

The page publishes no maximum noise level, so that field is left empty rather than borrowed from another LELO product, and no app, Bluetooth or remote control is mentioned anywhere, so control is treated as five buttons only.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

LELO marks Ina Wave 2 as 100% waterproof, "perfect for the bath and shower", so cleaning is straightforward: rinse the whole device under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner, then dry it fully before it goes back in the supplied satin pouch. The silicone is non-porous, and LELO states the product meets the ISO 3533 international standard for sex-toy safety. The junction where the external arm meets the shaft collects lubricant and deserves a careful rinse. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone.

## Battery and Warranty

The 520 mAh cell is smaller than the Soraya Wave's 850 mAh, but the documented result is the same: a two-hour charge for up to two hours of use and up to 90 days of standby, with automatic switch-off after 20 minutes, which is average for the catalogue. LELO documents a two-year warranty from the purchase date with a free replacement if a problem occurs, and a separate quality guarantee covering the product for ten years after the two-year warranty expires. Keep the receipt, since LELO's warranty text asks for it.

## In the Catalogue

### Ina Wave 2 vs. the Soraya Wave

The [LELO Soraya Wave](/products/lelo-soraya-wave/) is the obvious comparison: same brand, same WaveMotion mechanism, same 199 EUR list price, same 110 mm insertable length, same two-tier warranty. The documented differences are size and detail. Ina Wave 2 is 200 x 65 x 45 mm and 176 g against the Soraya Wave's 218 x 72 x 46 mm and 185 g, so it is shorter, narrower and lighter, it publishes a diameter where the Soraya Wave does not, and it offers 12 modes to the Soraya Wave's 8. The Soraya Wave answers with a larger 850 mAh battery, a 120 Hz frequency against 60 Hz and, decisively for some buyers, a documented 50 dB maximum noise level where Ina Wave 2 publishes none. At equal prices, the choice is a smaller body with more modes against a larger body with a stated noise figure.
