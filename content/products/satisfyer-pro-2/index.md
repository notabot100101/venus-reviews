The Satisfyer Pro 2 is the product that made pressure-wave stimulation a mass-market category rather than a luxury one. This review covers the Pro 2 Generation 3; Satisfyer also sells a Generation 2 and a Generation 4 Connect App alongside it, so the generation has to be named rather than assumed. Generation 3 adds a second motor and a Liquid Air attachment to the classic air-pulse cap, letting pressure waves and vibration run together or separately.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Satisfyer's official Pro 2 Generation 3 product page and its published specification table, checked on 13 October 2026. Where Satisfyer does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

The Pro 2 Generation 3 is a reasonable value pick for anyone who wants to try pressure-wave stimulation with the option of adding vibration. Satisfyer's own page documents 11 pressure-wave programs and 12 vibration programs driven by two independently controllable motors, an IPX7 waterproof seamless silicone body over an ABS frame, a whisper mode, and a 15-year warranty - a standout claim at this price. The gaps are real: Satisfyer does not publish a runtime, a charge time, or a noise level in decibels for this model, so battery life and discretion cannot be judged from the page alone.

## Format and Who It Is For

The Pro 2 Generation 3 is an external-only clitoral stimulator with two interchangeable caps: the classic air-pulse cap for Satisfyer's original contactless sensation, and a Liquid Air cap with a thin silicone membrane designed to mimic a pulsing water jet. Two separately controllable motors drive 11 pressure-wave programs and 12 vibration programs, which can be run alone or combined. Satisfyer's own usage instructions mention switching rhythms, or handing control to a partner, through its app.

It suits someone who wants to try both air-pulse and vibration from a single affordable device, or a first-time buyer curious about pressure-wave stimulation who does not want to commit to premium pricing before knowing whether it suits them. It is not the pick for anyone who needs a guaranteed runtime figure before buying, or who wants the quietest possible device - Satisfyer documents a whisper mode but no decibel figure to back it up.

## Documented Specifications

Satisfyer's own specification table gives the dimensions as 164 mm long, 65 mm wide and 46 mm high, and the weight as 203 g. The material is listed as ABS plastic and silicone, with the product copy describing the silicone as super-soft, skin-friendly and medical-grade, with a 100% seamless soft-touch finish. The device is documented as IPX7 waterproof and suitable for women per Satisfyer's own description.

The control set is the headline feature: 11 pressure-wave programs and 12 vibration programs, run by two separately controllable motors, so pressure-wave and vibration can be combined or used alone. A whisper mode is documented for quieter operation. The battery is rechargeable via a USB-C cable, but Satisfyer does not publish a runtime, a charge time, or a noise level in decibels for this model on its own page. Available colours are Red, Black and Violet.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

The seamless silicone-over-ABS construction is a genuine hygiene advantage: a visible seam where the silicone head meets the plastic body is exactly the sort of join that traps residue, and Satisfyer's page specifically describes this generation's body as 100% seamless. Body-safe, medical-grade silicone over the contact surface means the material should be non-porous where it matters and compatible with water-based lubricant.

IPX7 rating means the device can be rinsed under running water with mild soap rather than only wiped, which matters more for an air-pulse toy than for a vibrator, since the stimulation head is a concave cup that sits against the body and needs cleaning inside as well as out. The interchangeable Liquid Air attachment should be removed and cleaned separately from the base unit, and both should be dried fully before storage - a damp cup stored sealed is the one hygiene mistake this design invites.

## Battery and Warranty

Satisfyer documents the battery only as a rechargeable cell charged over USB-C; no runtime or charge time figure appears on the product's own page, which is a genuine gap for anyone planning extended sessions or travel. The standout documented claim is the warranty: 15 years, stated directly on this product's own page rather than inferred from a retailer listing, far longer than the two-year terms typical in this category. Keep proof of purchase, since a 15-year warranty is only useful if ownership can still be evidenced that far out.

If budget is the deciding factor, the Pro 2 gets you into the category at a fraction of the cost; a full comparison is on the [Womanizer Premium 2 vs. Satisfyer Pro 2](/womanizer-premium-2-vs-satisfyer-pro-2/) page.

### Pro 2 Generation 3 among the Satisfyer line

Within Satisfyer's own Pro 2 range, Generation 3 sits between the simpler Generation 2 (69.95 EUR list) and the Generation 4 Connect App (89.95 EUR), which adds dedicated Bluetooth app control as its headline feature. Choosing Generation 3 is a middle path: the dual-motor pressure-wave-plus-vibration combination without paying for the Connect App's full feature set.

## Value Position

At a 79.95 EUR list price - discounted to 55.97 EUR on Satisfyer's own store at the time of this review, a 30% promotion - the Pro 2 Generation 3 is one of the more affordable ways into pressure-wave stimulation with a vibration option layered on top. The 15-year warranty is a genuine differentiator at this price, and the documentation on dimensions, weight, material and program counts is unusually complete, sourced directly from Satisfyer's own page.
