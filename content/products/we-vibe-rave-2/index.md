We-Vibe Rave 2 is an insertable G-spot vibrator with an asymmetrical, slightly twisted shape that We-Vibe describes as adjustable to the body, controlled by buttons on the handle or by the We-Vibe app. It is the brand's dedicated internal device, sitting beside the [Nova 2](/products/we-vibe-nova-2/) rabbit and the LELO curved G-spot vibrators in the catalogue's internal category.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on We-Vibe's official Rave 2 product page and its published specification, feature and in-the-box tabs, checked on 12 October 2026. Where We-Vibe does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Rave 2 is the pick for someone who wants a dedicated G-spot vibrator with app control and a shape that can be angled rather than a fixed rigid curve. The documentation covers what matters for safety and ownership: body-safe silicone that is explicitly free of phthalates, BPA and latex, IPX7 waterproofing, a lithium-ion polymer battery with a documented runtime of up to 120 minutes from a charge of up to 90 minutes, and a 2-year warranty. We-Vibe says the asymmetrical shape stimulates the G-spot and the clitoris at the same time. The gaps are physical: no count of levels or modes, no weight, no noise figure, and the only dimensions on the page are an evident error. At 139 EUR it is a mid-priced internal device with a thin description of the object itself.

## Format and Who It Is For

A G-spot vibrator is an insertable device with a curved or angled tip built to hold pressure against the front vaginal wall. Rave 2's distinguishing feature is its asymmetrical shape, which We-Vibe describes as adjustable for a comfortable fit on any body; in practice that means the device can be rotated to change where the tip presses. Control is by plus and minus buttons on the handle, which cycle through the vibration modes and intensity levels, or by the We-Vibe app, which adds partner control at any distance.

It suits people who already know they respond to internal stimulation and want to fine-tune the angle, and people who want app control on an internal toy. It is a solo-first device that a partner can also control through the app. It is not the pick for someone who wants external stimulation, who should look at the [We-Vibe Tango X](/products/we-vibe-tango-x/) or the [Melt](/products/we-vibe-melt/), nor for someone who wants a dual rabbit, which is the [Nova 2](/products/we-vibe-nova-2/).

## Documented Specifications

We-Vibe's specification tab lists the Rave 2 in Satin Black and Cosmic Pink, with a USB-rechargeable lithium-ion polymer battery, a runtime of up to 120 minutes and a charge time of up to 90 minutes. The materials are body-safe silicone, free of phthalates and BPA and made without latex, and the warranty is 2 years.

The feature tab adds IPX7 waterproofing, the dual G-spot and clitoral stimulation claim, the ergonomic and adjustable shape and the We-Vibe app. The in-the-box tab lists the vibrator, a USB cable for any USB port or adapter, a quick-start guide and a storage pouch. We-Vibe does not publish a count of intensity levels or vibration modes, a weight, or a noise level. The specification tab does print a dimensions line, 250 x 110 x 750 mm, but a 750 mm tall vibrator is impossible, so this review treats the size as not documented rather than repeating a figure that cannot be right.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

The contact surface is body-safe silicone with an explicit phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free statement, which is a clear materials disclosure. Rave 2 is rated IPX7, so it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath or shower within the limits of that rating. An insertable device should be cleaned after every use and dried fully before it goes back into the pouch. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with a silicone toy.

## Battery and Warranty

The documented figures are up to 120 minutes of use from a charge of up to 90 minutes, which is the best charge-to-use ratio among the We-Vibe insertables in the catalogue: the Nova 2 documents 90 minutes of use from a 120-minute charge. Charging is over a USB cable from any USB port or adapter. The warranty is 2 years, and We-Vibe shows its 100-day pleasure guarantee site-wide; confirm the current terms before relying on either.
