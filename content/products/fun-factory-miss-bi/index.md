Fun Factory Miss Bi is a rabbit vibrator: an insertable shaft aimed at the G-spot with a flexible external arm that vibrates against the clitoris at the same time. It is the German brand's dual-stimulation entry, built around the same LOOP handle and USB-C charging as the [Volta](/products/fun-factory-volta/), and at 53.95 EUR it is the least expensive dual-stimulation device in the catalogue.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Fun Factory's official Miss Bi product page, its "Material & Mehr" specification block and its published variant data, checked on 12 October 2026. Where Fun Factory does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Miss Bi is the pick for someone who wants internal and clitoral stimulation at once without paying flagship money. Fun Factory documents the essentials: silicone, ABS and polycarbonate construction, a measured 173 x 76 x 38 mm body at 230 g, IPX7 waterproofing, USB-C charging and 12 vibration programmes, plus a loop-shaped handle that is easier to hold at an angle than a plain shaft. The gaps are typical of Fun Factory's product pages rather than unique to this model: no runtime, no charge time, no speed count and no stated warranty term. The format caveat is the one every rabbit carries. The distance between the shaft and the arm is fixed, so whether the arm lands where it should depends on anatomy, and the deliberately substantial girth will not suit everyone.

## Format and Who It Is For

A rabbit is a dual-stimulation vibrator. The shaft is inserted and curved toward the front vaginal wall, while the shorter external arm rests on the clitoris, so both areas are stimulated at once. Fun Factory describes the Miss Bi shaft as filling, with a substantial girth, and the flexible arm as vibrating against the external area. It is a solo-first toy that can also be used by a partner holding the handle.

Miss Bi suits people who already know they want internal and external stimulation together and prefer a handheld device with physical buttons over an app-driven one. It is a reasonable first dual toy because of the price, but a cautious first choice overall: someone unsure whether they want internal stimulation at all should start with an external device such as the [Fun Factory Volta](/products/fun-factory-volta/) or the small vibrators on the [budget picks](/budget-picks/) page. Anyone who wants to adjust the arm position should look at designs with a flexible or adjustable arm, which this page does not claim for Miss Bi.

## Documented Specifications

Fun Factory's specification block lists silicone, ABS plastic and polycarbonate as the materials, with the feature copy calling the silicone skin-friendly. The body measures 173 mm long, 76 mm wide and 38 mm high and weighs 230 g. Three colours are listed, Purple, Black and Grape, each with its own article number.

The device is rechargeable over USB-C from a single internal battery and offers 12 vibration programmes, with the feature copy also mentioning several speeds without giving a number. The page refers to a single "power motor" and does not state whether the arm has its own motor. Fun Factory does not publish a runtime, a charge time, an insertable length, a noise level or a warranty term for Miss Bi, and there is no app or remote control.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

The contact surface is silicone, which is non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly; the ABS and polycarbonate are the handle and internal structure rather than contact areas. Miss Bi is rated IPX7, so it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath or shower within the limits of that rating. A rabbit has more crevices than a plain shaft, especially where the arm meets the body, so rinse that junction carefully and dry fully before storage. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with a silicone toy.

## Battery and Warranty

Miss Bi charges over USB-C, which is a practical advantage over proprietary magnetic pins: any standard cable works. Fun Factory does not publish how long a charge lasts or how long it takes, so plan on charging before a session rather than relying on a figure. No warranty term is stated on the product page; buyers who want certainty should check Fun Factory's current terms before purchase.
