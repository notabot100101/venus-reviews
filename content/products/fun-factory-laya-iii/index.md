Fun Factory Laya III is the smallest and cheapest external vibrator in the Venus catalogue's Fun Factory line-up: a curved, palm-sized lay-on body meant to rest against the vulva rather than be inserted. Fun Factory's "Größe, Material & Mehr" block documents an 85 g, 107 x 50 x 41 mm body in silicone and ABS plastic, 7 vibration speeds and 3 patterns, IPX7 waterproofing and USB-C charging, with a mechanism the brand calls unique in its line-up: simultaneous vibration and tapping.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Fun Factory's official Laya III product page, its "Material & Mehr" specification block and its published variant data, checked on 12 October 2026. Where Fun Factory does not document something, this review says so rather than guessing at a single figure.

## Quick Verdict

Laya III suits someone who wants focused external clitoral contact in a lay-on, palm-held shape rather than an insertable toy. Fun Factory documents an 85 g, 107 x 50 x 41 mm body in silicone and ABS plastic, 7 vibration speeds and 3 patterns (10 programmes total), and a mechanism unusual in this catalogue: simultaneous vibration and tapping. It is IPX7 waterproof and rechargeable over USB-C, at 32.95 EUR with the Black colourway in stock when checked, the lowest price of any lay-on external Venus has reviewed. Against that, Fun Factory publishes no runtime, charge time, warranty term or noise figure, so buyers who prioritise a stated battery life or a warranty should look to the LELO or We-Vibe lay-on minis instead.

## Format and Who It Is For

Laya III is a lay-on external vibrator: a curved, palm-sized body held against the vulva rather than inserted, aimed at full-contact clitoral and labial stimulation ("Seine sanften Rundungen schmiegen sich perfekt an deine Vulva"). Unlike a bullet or lipstick mini, it has no narrow tip; the whole curved surface contacts skin at once. Fun Factory's distinguishing claim is that Laya III both vibrates and taps simultaneously, reaching several nerve types rather than one. Two buttons control 7 intensity levels and 3 patterns.

At 107 x 50 x 41 mm and 85 g, it is a compact, one-hand device, smaller and lighter than the externals on the [Fun Factory reviews hub](/fun-factory-reviews/) such as the [Volta](/products/fun-factory-volta/) and [Manta](/products/fun-factory-manta/), and far smaller than the [VIM](/products/fun-factory-vim/) wand. Fun Factory's own marketing also positions it for couples play and as a hands-on accessory during oral sex; Venus has not verified these secondary use claims and notes them only as the manufacturer's framing.

## Documented Specifications

Fun Factory's "Größe, Material & Mehr" block lists the following, verified on 12 October 2026:

- Weight: 85 g
- Length: 107 mm
- Width: 50 mm
- Height: 41 mm
- Material: silicone, ABS plastic ("Silikon, ABS Kunststoff"); body copy separately describes the silicone as "seidigem, körperfreundlichem Silikon", silky and body-friendly
- Colour: Raspberry, Black, Sage Green, consistent between the spec block's Farbe row and the variant picker, with article numbers FF000971RB01, FF000971BK01 and FF000971BT01
- Waterproof: yes, IPX7 ("Wasserdicht ja (IPX7)")
- Battery: one rechargeable battery ("1x Akku"), charged over USB-C per the feature list
- Vibration: 7 speeds and 3 patterns ("7 STUFEN + 3 MUSTER"), confirmed in an FAQ accordion ("sieben Vibrationsstufen und drei Programme") and summing to the "10 abwechslungsreiche Vibrationsprogramme" headline figure

Not documented: runtime, charge time, noise level, warranty term, app or remote control. The product description states Laya III "vibriert und klopft gleichzeitig" (vibrates and taps simultaneously) for dual stimulation, a mechanism detail outside the standard specification keys but worth noting for anyone comparing motor types.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

The body is silicone over an ABS control panel, which Fun Factory separately calls silky and body-friendly. Silicone is non-porous and body-safe when used with a compatible, water-based lubricant; avoid silicone-based lubricant on a silicone toy. The IPX7 rating means Laya III can be rinsed clean under running water or used in the bath or shower, which suits its intended couples and skin-contact uses. Dry it fully, particularly around the seam where the silicone body meets the metallic control panel, before storing.

## Battery and Warranty

Laya III runs on a built-in rechargeable battery over USB-C, so a standard cable works, but Fun Factory does not publish a runtime or a charge time for this model on the product page, and no warranty term is stated either, a gap it shares with the [Volta](/products/fun-factory-volta/), [Manta](/products/fun-factory-manta/) and [VIM](/products/fun-factory-vim/) pages. Buyers who need a specific battery life or a warranty window should check Fun Factory's manual or ask the retailer before buying.

## Value Position

At 32.95 EUR direct from the manufacturer, with no discount and Black in stock when checked, Laya III is comfortably the cheapest lay-on external in this review set and qualifies for the [budget picks](/budget-picks/) page. The price buys a fully measured, IPX7, USB-C-rechargeable vibrator with a documented 7-level, 3-pattern motor and a distinctive tapping mechanism, a combination not matched by the pricier LELO or We-Vibe minis. It does not buy a documented runtime, charge time, warranty or noise figure, all of which those competitors publish. It rates 3.5, consistent with Fun Factory's other battery-thin documentation, for a toy whose price and mechanism are its strongest arguments rather than its paperwork.
