LELO Sila Cruise is the Cruise Control version of LELO's wide-mouth sonic clitoral massager: the body-safe silicone shell, wide mouth and eight settings LELO documents for the [LELO Sila](/products/lelo-sila/), the catalogue's gentle sonic pick, plus patented Cruise Control technology that keeps the pulsations constant when the device is pressed hard against the body. LELO positions it as the ideal choice for sex-toy beginners and a slow, gentle build-up rather than maximum intensity.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO's official Sila Cruise product page and its published specification block, checked on 12 October 2026. Where LELO does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Sila Cruise is the pick for someone who wants the Sila's wide-mouth, gentle sonic format and tends to press a stimulator firmly into the body. LELO's specification block is as complete as the Sila's: body-safe silicone over ABS, 80 x 75 x 35 mm, 105 g, a 520 mAh battery giving up to 2 hours of use from a 2-hour charge, automatic switch-off after 20 minutes, eight settings, 100% waterproofing, and a 2-year warranty followed by a 10-year quality guarantee. The caveats: the 139.00 EUR page price is flagged by LELO's own analytics as a 26% promotion with no standing list price stated, no noise ceiling, standby time or app control is published, and the settings count, runtime and battery are unchanged from the Sila.

## Format and Who It Is For

Sila Cruise is an external-only clitoral massager with a wide mouth that LELO describes as spreading the sonic waves with equal intensity across the whole clitoris rather than just its centre, for a slow build-up, without direct contact. Cruise Control is the addition over the Sila: LELO's patented technology that, in the page's words, makes the pulsations constant during use, so that when the device is pressed hard against the body extra power is unleashed. The page describes a + button to switch on and raise the strength and a ( ) button to cycle the eight settings and to switch off.

This format suits people who want gentle sonic stimulation over a broad area and press firmly rather than hovering. It is not for anyone chasing intensity: LELO's settings copy runs from a teasing murmur to a satisfying pulse, and the focused Sona 2 and Sona 3 are the sharper options. There is no insertable element, no G-spot arm and no documented app layer.

## Documented Specifications

LELO's specification block lists the materials as body-safe silicone and ABS plastic, in Pink, Lilac and Aqua. The size is 80 x 75 x 35 mm and the weight is 105 g. The frequency is given as 60 Hz, labelled Rotation Frequency on the page, and the modes count is 8, recorded here as the patterns count with no separate intensity-level count. The device switches off with the ( ) button, and automatically after 20 minutes.

The battery is a Li-ion 520 mAh cell at 3.7 V, charged in 2 hours at 5.0 V / 500 mA, giving up to 2 hours of user time. LELO does not publish a standby time, a maximum noise level, a finish, a mouth width, an IP rating, a button count or any app or Bluetooth connectivity, so those remain not documented. The Sila page, by contrast, states a 90-day standby.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

LELO states the Sila Cruise is 100% waterproof and describes the surface as ultra-smooth, extra-soft premium body-safe silicone. Body-safe silicone is non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly: rinse under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner, then dry fully before storing it in the supplied pouch, paying attention to the wide mouth, where lubricant can collect. LELO states the product meets the ISO 3533 safety standard and ships with a USB-charging cord, satin storage pouch, warranty registration card and instruction manual.

## Battery and Warranty

Sila Cruise is USB-rechargeable, with a 2-hour charge giving up to 2 hours of use, the same figures LELO publishes for the Sila and the Sona 2. The standby time is not stated on this page, so buyers should not assume the 90-day figure LELO publishes for the Sila. LELO documents a 2-year warranty from the purchase date, with a free replacement in that period provided the receipt is kept, and separately states a 10-year quality guarantee once the base warranty expires.

## In the Catalogue

### Sila Cruise vs. the Sila

The [LELO Sila](/products/lelo-sila/) is the catalogue's gentle sonic pick at a 169 EUR list price, and on documented values the two are almost identical: the same silicone and ABS build, dimensions, weight, 520 mAh battery, 2-hour charge, 2-hour user time, 20-minute switch-off, eight settings, waterproofing and colours. What the Sila Cruise adds is documented Cruise Control; what its page drops is the Sila's stated 90-day standby. On the day checked the Sila Cruise page showed 139.00 EUR against 169.00 EUR for the Sila, but LELO's analytics flagged the Sila Cruise figure as a 26% promotion and the Sila's as undiscounted, so the gap may not persist. LELO's page does not say the Sila Cruise replaces the Sila, and LELO's category feed listed both on the day checked.

### Sila Cruise vs. the Sona 2, Sona 3 and the air-pulse field

The [LELO Sona 2](/products/lelo-sona-2/) at 109 EUR is the catalogue's top sonic pick: a focused mouth, twelve settings, 125 g, a documented under-60 dB noise ceiling. The [LELO Sona 3](/products/lelo-sona-3/) at 149 EUR adds app control and SmoothRise transitions with the same noise ceiling. Both document more than the Sila Cruise and both are tuned for intensity rather than gentleness; the [Sona 2 vs. Sila comparison](/lelo-sona-2-vs-lelo-sila/) sets out the focused-versus-wide split. In the air-pulse field, the [Womanizer Premium 2](/products/womanizer-premium-2/) at 179 EUR documents a 4-hour runtime, 14 intensity levels and an IPX7 rating, none of which the Sila Cruise page matches. The external stimulators across brands are ranked on the [best air-pulse stimulators](/best-air-pulse-stimulators/) page.
