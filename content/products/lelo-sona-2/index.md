LELO Sona 2 is LELO's focused SenSonic clitoral stimulator: an external-only device that delivers sonic pulses from a 23 mm mouth rather than vibrating against the skin, with twelve settings on a three-button interface. At a 109 EUR list price it is the cheapest entry in LELO's sonic line and the catalogue's top sonic pick, below the wide-mouth [LELO Sila](/products/lelo-sila/) at 169 EUR, the app-connected [LELO Sona 3](/products/lelo-sona-3/) at 149 EUR and the dual-format [LELO Enigma](/products/lelo-enigma/) at 199 EUR.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO's official Sona 2 product page and its published specification block, checked on 13 October 2026. Where LELO does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Sona 2 is the pick for someone who wants LELO's sonic stimulation in its most focused and most completely documented form. LELO states the material, 112 x 55 x 51 mm dimensions, 125 g weight, 23 mm mouth width, a 520 mAh battery giving up to 2 hours of use from a 2-hour charge, 90 days of standby, an exact 60 dB maximum noise level, twelve modes, 100% waterproofing in a single piece of silicone, and a 2-year warranty followed by a 10-year quality guarantee. The caveats are that 60 dB is audible in a quiet room, that LELO's own instructions anticipate it feeling too intense for some, and that no app control, intensity-level count or colour options are published.

## Format and Who It Is For

Sona 2 is an external clitoral stimulator with a single contoured mouth that is placed on or beside the clitoris. LELO's SenSonic technology emits sonic pulses that the page describes as resonating deep into the internal structure of the clitoris, so the device works without the mouth making conventional vibrating contact. The highlights copy describes a softer, deeper, bigger mouth, and the specification block gives the opening as 23 mm wide. Three buttons step through twelve modes.

LELO's usage instructions are direct about intensity: turn it on, place the mouth on the clitoris or next to it, and slow down if it feels too intense. That makes the Sona 2 a product for people who want focused sonic stimulation and will learn placement, rather than the gentlest first device; the Sila is LELO's wide-mouth answer for the latter. It is external-only, with no insertable element and no app layer, and LELO's bath note recommends water-based lubricant because water negates natural lubrication.

## Documented Specifications

LELO's specification block lists the material as body-safe silicone with ABS plastic. The size is 112 x 55 x 51 mm (4.4 x 2.1 x 2 in), the weight is 125 g (4.4 oz), and the mouth width is 23 mm (0.9 in). The interface is three buttons with twelve modes, automatic switch-off is after 20 minutes, the frequency is 63 Hz, and the maximum noise level is stated as an exact 60 dB.

The battery is a Li-Ion 520 mAh cell at 3.7 V, charged in 2 hours at 5.0 V / 500 mA, giving up to 2 hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby. LELO's features tile states twelve settings, recorded here as the patterns count because LELO publishes no separate intensity-level figure. LELO does not publish colour options, a finish, rated power, an IP rating or app connectivity, so those remain not documented.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

LELO states the Sona 2 is 100% waterproof, suitable for the bath, shower or hot tub, and that it is made from a single piece of silicone that keeps it watertight and, in LELO's words, super easy to clean. The surface is described as extra-soft premium silicone that feels silky to the touch. Body-safe silicone is non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly: rinse under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner, then dry fully before storing it in the supplied pouch. The 23 mm mouth deserves attention, since lubricant collects there. LELO states the product meets the ISO 3533 international safety standard and ships with a USB-charging cord, satin storage pouch, warranty registration card and instruction manual. LELO claims no IP rating, only the 100% waterproof statement.

## Battery and Warranty

Sona 2 is USB-rechargeable, with a 2-hour charge giving up to 2 hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby, and it switches itself off after 20 minutes. LELO documents a 2-year warranty from the purchase date, with a free replacement if a problem occurs in that period provided the receipt is kept, and separately states a 10-year quality guarantee once the base warranty expires. Buyers relying on the extended guarantee should confirm the current registration terms on LELO's warranty page first.
