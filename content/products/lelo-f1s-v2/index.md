LELO F1S V2 is the first male device in the Venus catalogue: an automatic penis stimulator built around LELO's SENSONIC sonic-wave technology rather than a simple stroking motion. It is also the most sensor-heavy product reviewed here, with an app layer that sets it apart from mechanical strokers.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO's official F1S V2 product page and its published specification block, checked on 5 October 2026. Where LELO does not document a detail clearly, this page says so.

## Quick Verdict

F1S V2 is the pick for buyers who want the most technology-forward male device rather than the simplest one. The specifications are strong and fully documented: body-safe silicone over ABS and aluminium alloy, 100% waterproofing, two separate motors (a 60 Hz suction motor and a 90 Hz vibration motor), an 850 mAh battery good for up to two hours, and a two-year warranty. The LELO app adds sensor-based real-time feedback and Cruise Control, which holds intensity through contractions. The caveats are price and complexity: at 199 EUR it is a premium single-purpose device, and the app-and-sensor layer is more than some buyers want.

## Format and Who It Is For

Most automatic male masturbators rely on either suction or a stroking sleeve. F1S V2 is different because its core mechanism is sonic waves transmitted through the sleeve, with a second motor adding conventional vibration. The sensor system reports back through the app in real time, and Cruise Control keeps the intensity steady rather than letting it drop at the moment it matters.

That makes it most relevant for buyers who actively want the data-and-app experience. Someone who wants a simple, get-in-and-go device will find the setup overhead unnecessary.

## Documented Specifications

LELO lists body-safe silicone with ABS plastic and an aluminium alloy, in a matte black and red finish. The device measures 143 x 71 mm with an insertable length of 110.72 mm and an internal diameter of 41 to 48 mm, and weighs 285 g, so it is a substantial object to hold and store.

Power is a Li-Ion 850 mAh 3.7 V cell, charged in two hours and giving up to two hours of use, with 90-day standby and automatic switch-off after 20 minutes. There are two motors, a suction motor at 60 Hz and a vibration motor at 90 Hz, operated through a three-button interface and the app.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

The F1S V2 is marked 100% waterproof, which matters for a device with an internal sleeve that needs thorough cleaning. Rinse under running water with mild soap or a compatible cleaner, pay attention to the sleeve interior, and dry fully before storage. The body-safe silicone contact surfaces are non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone.

## Battery, App and Warranty

F1S V2 charges over USB in about two hours for a matching two-hour runtime, with a long 90-day standby. The app is central rather than optional here: it is where the sensor feedback and Cruise Control live, so a buyer who will not use an app loses part of what they are paying for. LELO documents a two-year warranty from the purchase date; confirm LELO's registration-based extension terms on the warranty page before relying on a longer period.

## Value Position

At a manufacturer price of 199 EUR, F1S V2 is a premium purchase. It is not a value pick and not a simple one. The reason to choose it is specific: a buyer who wants sonic-wave stimulation plus vibration, app feedback and Cruise Control in one documented, fully waterproof device. Anyone who wants a straightforward male toy without the app layer should look for something simpler.
