Dame Pom is a palm-held external vibrator from Dame, the US brand behind the catalogue's [Arc](/products/dame-arc/), [Aer](/products/dame-aer/) and [Eva II](/products/dame-eva-ii/). Where the Arc is insertable and the Aer is an air-pulse device, Pom is a soft, flexible, egg-shaped vibrator with no insertable part at all: a single contoured body meant to sit in the hand or against the body and conform to movement rather than stay rigid.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Dame's official Pom product page and its published specifications, checked on 12 October 2026. Where Dame does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Pom is the pick for someone who wants a simple, flexible external vibrator with a documented size and a long warranty, rather than a toy with app control or elaborate modes. The specification page is genuinely useful here: medical-grade silicone that Dame separately badges as 100% body-safe, specific dimensions of 83 x 56 x 30 mm (3.25 x 2.2 x 1.2 inches), 5 intensity levels and 5 pattern modes on a two-button layout, USB-C charging, a 1-hour runtime at the highest setting from a 2-hour charge, and a 3-year warranty. The caveats are mostly commercial and documentary: Dame prices in US dollars (103.00 USD) rather than euros, the page states waterproofing without a specific IP rating, it claims "whisper quiet" without a decibel figure, and it does not publish a product weight at all.

## Format and Who It Is For

Pom is an external-only vibrator with a soft, pliable, teardrop-shaped body and a small ridge near the top that Dame positions as a point of contact. There is no shaft and nothing is designed to be inserted; the whole device is meant to flex against the body as the user moves, rather than hold a fixed shape the way a harder-bodied vibrator does. Dame's own comparison chart on the page describes it as suited to solo or couples play, and markets it toward both first-time users and more experienced users who want flexibility and ease rather than a specialist shape.

At 83 x 56 x 30 mm, it is compact enough to sit in a closed hand or a small travel bag, which matches Dame's "fit naturally in your hand" framing. It suits buyers who want a straightforward, low-complexity external vibrator and are comfortable with a US-dollar price; buyers who specifically want internal G-spot stimulation should look at the Arc instead, and anyone wanting a contactless, air-pulse sensation should look at the Aer.

## Documented Specifications

Dame's specification block lists the material as "Medical Grade Silicone," and the product page separately carries a "100% Body-safe" badge alongside the main listing. Dimensions are given as 3.25 inches long, 2.2 inches wide and 1.2 inches deep, which converts to approximately 83 x 56 x 30 mm. The device offers 5 intensity levels and 5 pattern modes, controlled through two buttons, one for power and patterns and one for intensity, per Dame's "how to use" copy.

Power is a USB-C rechargeable battery, with Dame stating a run time of 1 hour at the highest settings from a 2-hour charge, and a reminder on the page to use a 5V (or lower) USB wall adapter and to ensure the product is fully dry before charging. The box contains the Pom vibrator itself, a USB-C charging cable, a storage bag and a user manual. Dame does not publish a product weight, an app, or a maximum noise level in decibels for Pom, so buyers who want those figures will not find them on the official page. Dame's Shopify variant data does list a "weight" field of 275 g for both color options, but that field is the shipping or fulfillment weight recorded for the variant, not a stated product weight, so this review does not use it as the device's weight.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

Pom's body is medical-grade silicone that Dame's page additionally badges as 100% body-safe, which is non-porous and appropriate for external intimate use when cleaned correctly. Dame describes the device as waterproof but, as with the Arc and Aer, does not publish a specific IP rating, so the conservative reading is that it can be rinsed under running water or used in the shower or bath, without assuming it is rated for prolonged submersion until Dame documents a number. Clean with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and dry fully before storage, taking care around the USB-C charging port, and use a water-based lubricant as the default with silicone. Store it in the included bag away from other silicone toys to avoid surface interactions.

## Battery and Warranty

Pom charges over USB-C, which is convenient since a standard phone-style cable will work, and Dame specifies a 5V wall adapter. The documented run time is 1 hour at the highest intensity setting from a 2-hour charge, shorter than some devices in the catalogue, though runtime claims at maximum intensity are typically a worst case, and actual sessions at lower settings should run longer. The standout figure is the warranty: Dame documents a 3-year warranty, matching the Arc and Aer and longer than the two-year terms common elsewhere in the category, which is a genuine point of confidence for a 103 USD device.

## In the Catalogue

### Pom vs. the other external vibrators

Against other external-only vibrators in the catalogue, the [Fun Factory Manta](/products/fun-factory-manta/) (48.95 EUR) and [Fun Factory Volta](/products/fun-factory-volta/) (51.95 EUR) are both cheaper flexible or twin-tip external stimulators, and the [We-Vibe Tango X](/products/we-vibe-tango-x/) (89.00 EUR) is a compact bullet with a broader motor rumble. Pom is the most expensive of the four at 103 USD, and its case is Dame's specific published dimensions, a two-mode (level plus pattern) control layout, USB-C charging and a 3-year warranty rather than a lower price. Buyers focused on value should weigh the Manta or Volta first.

### Pom among the Dame range

Within Dame, Pom is the flexible, no-insertable-part external device, the [Arc](/products/dame-arc/) is the insertable G-spot and clitoral vibrator, and the [Aer](/products/dame-aer/) is the contactless air-pulse stimulator, with the [Eva II](/products/dame-eva-ii/) as the brand's hands-free wearable external toy. The four cover different formats within the same materials and warranty standard, so choosing between them is a question of format rather than quality.
