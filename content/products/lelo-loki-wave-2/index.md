LELO Loki Wave 2 is the catalogue's third prostate massager and the first whose documented distinguishing feature is motion rather than vibration alone. LELO's WaveMotion technology drives the sculpted tip of the insertable arm in what the product page calls a finger-like massage, on top of the vibration from two motors. This page covers the "2" model only; LELO still sells the original Loki Wave as a separate, older product.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO's official Loki Wave 2 product page and its published specification block, checked on 12 October 2026. Where LELO does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Loki Wave 2 is the pick for buyers who already know prostate stimulation suits them and want a moving tip rather than a static vibrating one. The specification block is complete: body-safe silicone over ABS, 91 x 42 x 196 mm with a 100 mm insertable length, 196 g, up to two hours of use from a two-hour charge, a noise ceiling under 60 dB, 100% waterproofing and a two-year warranty. LELO documents twelve settings and two motors, one per massage handle. The caveats are the 219 EUR list price, the highest among the catalogue's prostate massagers, the absence of any documented app or remote control, and the fact that an insertable anal device of this size is not a first toy.

## Format and Who It Is For

Loki Wave 2 is an insertable prostate massager with two arms. The insertable arm carries the WaveMotion tip and is documented at 100 mm of insertable length; LELO's copy describes it as surging in a finger-like massage, which is the come-hither motion that distinguishes this device from a vibration-only massager such as the [LELO Hugo](/products/lelo-hugo/). The second arm, which LELO calls the other massage handle and gives its own motor, stays outside the body and rests against the perineum. The handle with the five-button interface also stays outside.

That external arm and handle matter for safety as well as sensation. Anything used anally needs a part that cannot pass fully inside the body, and on Loki Wave 2 the external arm and control handle are that retrieval point; the device should never be inserted beyond the point where they sit against the body. LELO's own how-to-use steps start with a generous amount of its Personal Moisturizer before slow insertion, and water-based lubricant is the conservative default with a silicone surface.

The right buyer is comfortable with anal use, wants the motion mechanism and a complete, fully waterproof spec sheet, and is happy controlling the device by hand, because LELO documents no remote or app for this model.

## Documented Specifications

LELO's specification block lists body-safe silicone and ABS plastic with a smooth finish. The device measures 91 x 42 x 196 mm, with an insertable length of 100 mm, and weighs 196 g. LELO does not state a diameter for the insertable arm.

Power is a lithium 850 mAh 3.7 V cell, charged in up to two hours at 5.0 V and 750 mA over the supplied USB cord, giving up to two hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby. The motor frequency is documented at 122 Hz, the device switches off automatically after 20 minutes, and the maximum noise level is given as under 60 dB. The interface is five buttons on the handle.

The feature copy adds twelve vibration settings, two motors with each motor driving one massage handle, extra-soft silicone and the WaveMotion tip. LELO does not split the twelve settings into intensity levels and patterns, so this page records them as twelve settings and nothing more precise.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

LELO marks Loki Wave 2 as 100% waterproof and describes it as suitable for the bath and shower, which makes cleaning straightforward: rinse the whole device under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner, then dry it fully before it goes back in the satin pouch. For an anal device, clean before and after every use and check the silicone surface for damage first. The silicone is non-porous and LELO states ISO 3533 certification for body-safe materials, which is a useful documentation signal for a product intended for anal contact. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default unless LELO's instructions say otherwise.

## Battery and Warranty

The two-hour charge matches the two-hour runtime, and the 90-day standby suits occasional use. The device switches itself off after 20 minutes. LELO documents a two-year warranty from the purchase date, with a free replacement if a problem occurs in that time, followed by a ten-year quality guarantee after the two-year warranty expires. Keep the receipt, since LELO's warranty text asks for it.
