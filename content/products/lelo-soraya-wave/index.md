LELO Soraya Wave is the catalogue's second rabbit vibrator and LELO's own dual-stimulation flagship: an insertable shaft built around LELO's WaveMotion technology for the G-spot, paired with a separate external arm for the clitoris, designed so both can run together. It sits alongside the [We-Vibe Nova 2](/products/we-vibe-nova-2/) as the catalogue's other dual-stimulation option, but where the Nova 2's advantage is a flexible arm that follows movement, Soraya Wave's defining feature is a moving internal tip rather than a flexible external one.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO's official Soraya Wave product page and its published specification block, checked on 12 October 2026. Where LELO does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Soraya Wave is the pick for someone who specifically wants a moving internal sensation alongside external clitoral stimulation, rather than a flexible-arm rabbit. LELO's documentation is unusually thorough: body-safe silicone over ABS, full dimensions of 218 x 72 x 46 mm, a stated 185 g weight, a 110 mm insertable length, a 50 dB maximum noise level, 100% waterproofing, 8 pleasure settings, and a two-tier warranty running 2 years as standard with a further 10-year quality guarantee after that. The WaveMotion mechanism itself is clearly described: the sculpted tip rises and falls against the G-spot, said to feel like a come-hither finger motion, while the external arm vibrates independently and both can be run together or separately. The caveats are a 199 EUR list price, no stated app or Bluetooth control, no distinct vibration-pattern count beyond the 8 settings, and a large format that, like any rabbit, is fit-sensitive.

## Format and Who It Is For

Soraya Wave is a rabbit: an insertable shaft for the G-spot plus an external arm for the clitoris, intended for simultaneous use. What sets it apart from a conventional rabbit is WaveMotion, LELO's name for the mechanism that makes the shaft's tip physically rise and fall inside the body rather than only vibrate in place, which LELO describes as reaching the G-spot "like a finger massage." The external arm is a separate vibrating zone, and LELO's own instructions describe running the bigger arm, the smaller arm, or both together, so the two stimulation points are independently controllable rather than locked to a single combined intensity.

This format suits people who want a moving, come-hither internal sensation combined with clitoral contact, and who are comfortable with a larger, two-part insertable shape. It is not a compact or travel-first device, and like all rabbits, correct alignment depends on individual anatomy, so fit cannot be guaranteed from specifications alone. Anyone who wants external-only stimulation, a smaller footprint, or app-based remote control should look elsewhere in the catalogue.

## Documented Specifications

LELO's specification block lists the material as body-safe silicone with ABS plastic internally. The overall size is 218 x 72 x 46 mm, with a separately stated insertable length of 110 mm for the internal shaft, and a product weight of 185 g. The motor frequency is given as 120 Hz, with a documented maximum noise level of 50 dB, among the quieter figures in the catalogue for a rabbit of this size.

Power comes from a Li-Ion cell rated 850 mA at 3.7 V, charged in 2 hours at 5.0 V / 750 mA, giving a documented 2-hour user time and up to 90 days of standby. The device switches off automatically after 20 minutes of inactivity. LELO's feature copy states 8 pleasure settings, described as running "from a teasing murmur to a satisfying pulse," recorded here as the levels figure; the page does not separately state a distinct number of vibration patterns, so that field is left undocumented rather than assumed equal to the settings count. LELO also does not state whether Soraya Wave has app or Bluetooth connectivity, so this review treats that as not documented rather than assuming either way.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

LELO states Soraya Wave is 100% waterproof, suitable for the bath or shower, which also makes cleaning straightforward: rinse thoroughly under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner, then dry fully before storage. The body-safe silicone covering the shaft and external arm is non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly, and LELO states the product meets the ISO 3533 safety standard. Because the device has two closely-set stimulation zones, the seam where the external arm meets the shaft is worth rinsing carefully, since lubricant can collect there. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone.

## Battery and Warranty

Soraya Wave is USB-rechargeable, with a 2-hour charge giving a documented 2-hour user time and up to 90 days of standby between charges, figures that are average for the catalogue rather than a standout. LELO documents a 2-year warranty from the purchase date covering defects, and separately states a further 10-year "quality guarantee" that applies once the base 2-year warranty expires, for a combined 12 years of stated cover, among the longest total warranty window in the catalogue. Buyers who want to rely on the extended guarantee should confirm the current registration terms on LELO's warranty page before purchase.
