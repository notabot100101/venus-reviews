Dame Aer is the air-pulse stimulator from Dame, the US brand behind the catalogue's [Eva II](/products/dame-eva-ii/) and [Arc](/products/dame-arc/). Where those two cover hands-free external and insertable G-spot use, the Aer is Dame's take on the contactless format: a compact device whose flexible silicone mouth seals over the clitoris and delivers rhythmic pulses of air, in the same category as the Womanizer and LELO sonic devices.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Dame's official Aer product page and its published specification block, checked on 10 October 2026. Where Dame does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Aer is the pick for someone who wants compact air-pulse from a brand with a strong body-safe reputation and a long warranty. The specifications are solid: medical-grade, 100% body-safe silicone, a flexible mouth built to seal over a range of anatomies, 5 intensity levels and 5 pattern modes, USB-C charging, up to 1.5 hours of runtime at the highest setting from a two-hour charge, and a 3-year warranty. The caveats are commercial and documentary rather than technical: Dame prices in US dollars (125.00 USD), it calls the Aer "whisper quiet" without publishing a decibel figure, it states waterproofing without a specific IP rating, and the 1.5-hour runtime trails the two- to four-hour figures the category leaders document.

## Format and Who It Is For

Aer is an external-only air-pulse stimulator. Its soft, flexible mouth creates a seal around the clitoris and pulses air in rhythm rather than vibrating against it, the same contactless-feeling sensation Womanizer and Satisfyer built the category on. Dame's distinction is the mouth itself, designed to flex so the seal works across different shapes, and the device's small size, about 118 mm tall and 37 mm wide.

It suits people who want air-pulse in a compact, easy-to-hold body, and people who already trust Dame's materials. Anyone wanting internal stimulation should look at the Arc or the dual-format devices, and anyone unsure whether air-pulse suits them should start with a cheaper device.

## Documented Specifications

Dame's specification block lists medical-grade silicone described as 100% body-safe. The device measures 1.95 inches (about 50 mm) from mouth to back, 4.66 inches (about 118 mm) tall and 1.46 inches (about 37 mm) wide, with a flexible mouth of 0.5 by 0.8 inches. It offers 5 intensity levels and 5 pattern modes.

Power is a USB-C rechargeable battery giving up to 1.5 hours of runtime at the highest setting from a 2-hour charge; Dame advises a 5V wall adapter. The box includes the Aer, a USB-C cable, a storage bag and a manual. Dame does not publish a weight, a battery capacity, a decibel figure, or a specific waterproof rating, so buyers who want those numbers will not find them on the official page.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

The contact surfaces are medical-grade silicone that Dame states is 100% body-safe, non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly. Dame describes the device as waterproof without a specific IP rating, so the conservative reading is that it can be rinsed under running water but should not be assumed fully submersible until Dame documents the rating. The mouth is the area to clean most carefully, since fluids collect in an air-pulse opening; rinse thoroughly and dry fully before storage, taking care around the USB-C port. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone.

## Battery and Warranty

Aer charges over USB-C in two hours for up to 1.5 hours of use at the highest setting, which is adequate for a compact device but shorter than the 2 to 4 hours the Womanizer and LELO devices document. The standout is the warranty: Dame's 3-year term is longer than most of the catalogue, matching the Arc and a point of confidence at this price.
