Fun Factory VIM is a full-size wand massager, Fun Factory's take on the mains-era "magic wand" format rebuilt as a cordless, rechargeable, body-safe device. Its defining engineering detail is Weighted Rumble: two weights sit beside the motor inside the vibrating head, adding mass that Fun Factory says pushes the vibration toward a deeper, lower-frequency register rather than the buzzy high end typical of smaller motors. The brand's "Größe, Material & Mehr" block documents a 437 g, 315 x 61 x 61 mm body in body-friendly silicone, 5 vibration speeds and 3 patterns (8 programmes total), and USB-C charging.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Fun Factory's official VIM product page, its published specification block and its published variant data, checked on 13 October 2026. Where Fun Factory does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

VIM suits someone who wants a full-size wand for broad external massage with a reasonably complete spec sheet behind it: 437 g, 315 x 61 x 61 mm, body-friendly silicone, 5 speeds and 3 patterns (8 programmes total), USB-C charging, at 66.95 EUR with the Orange and Nightblue colourways in stock when checked. Fun Factory's own feature badges call it waterproof to IPX7, but its cleaning FAQ separately states the wand is water-resistant rather than waterproof and should not be submerged in the bath or shower - a contradiction worth knowing before buying. Runtime, charge time, warranty term and a measured noise figure are not published.

## Format and Who It Is For

VIM is a wand massager: a slim, flexible-necked handle topped by a rounded vibrating head, built for broad-surface external contact rather than insertion or pinpoint stimulation. Fun Factory frames it for use on the clitoris, perineum, shoulders or other muscle groups, and for "blended orgasm" play alongside penetration or a separate dildo. The handle is deliberately slim and light relative to the vibrating head, so the weight concentrates where the stimulation happens rather than tiring the hand holding it.

Two buttons step through 5 vibration speeds and 3 named programmes (Deep Orgasm, Intense Orgasm and Edging), 8 settings in total. It suits buyers who specifically want the wand format and prefer a deep, rumbly vibration character over a buzzy one. It is not the right choice for anyone who needs a compact or travel-sized toy, or who wants confirmed runtime, charge time or warranty figures before buying, and anyone who wants to use a wand in the bath or shower should read the waterproofing note below first.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

The contact surface is body-friendly silicone over a plastic handle that houses the controls. Silicone is non-porous and suitable for external use when cleaned correctly; water-based lubricant is the conservative choice. Waterproofing is where Fun Factory's own page disagrees with itself: a feature bullet and a product badge both state "Wasserdicht (IPX7)," but a FAQ answer on the same page, under "Wie lässt sich VIM reinigen?", says the wand "ist wasserresistent und leicht zu reinigen... Achtung: wasserresistent heisst nicht wasserdicht!" (is water-resistant and easy to clean - caution: water-resistant does not mean waterproof), and specifically tells buyers not to take it into the bath or shower or clean it under running water. This review records the IPX7 claim in the specification field above together with the care note's caveat, and flags the contradiction here: anyone who wants bath or shower use, or wants to rinse VIM under a tap, should not rely on the IPX7 badge without confirming directly with Fun Factory first. The manufacturer's own recommended cleaning method is its spray-and-wipe cleaner, or a damp cloth.

## Battery and Warranty

VIM runs on a built-in rechargeable battery charged over USB-C, so a standard cable works. That is where the documentation stops: no runtime or charge time is published for this model, and no warranty term appears on the page either, a gap shared by other Fun Factory pages Venus has reviewed. Buyers who want a specific battery life or a warranty window should check Fun Factory's manual or ask the retailer before buying.

## Value Position

At 66.95 EUR direct from the manufacturer, with no discount and the Orange and Nightblue colourways in stock when checked, VIM is the least expensive wand Venus has reviewed, undercutting the premium wands on the [best wand vibrators](/best-wand-vibrators/) page by a wide margin, and it qualifies for the [budget picks](/budget-picks/) page. The price buys a fully measured, weighted-motor wand in body-friendly silicone with a documented 5-speed, 3-pattern motor. It does not buy a documented runtime, charge time or warranty, and the manufacturer's own waterproofing claim is directly contradicted by its own cleaning FAQ, which is worth confirming before any wet use. It rates 4.0, reflecting strong size and motor documentation offset by the undocumented battery figures and the waterproofing inconsistency.
