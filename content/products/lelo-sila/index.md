LELO Sila is the gentle entry in LELO's sonic line: an external-only clitoral stimulator that LELO's page title calls the gentle clitoral vibrator, built around a wide mouth documented as spreading its sonic waves with equal intensity across the whole clitoris for a slow build-up. At a 169 EUR list price it sits above the focused [LELO Sona 2](/products/lelo-sona-2/) at 109 EUR and the app-connected [LELO Sona 3](/products/lelo-sona-3/) at 149 EUR, and below the dual-format [LELO Enigma](/products/lelo-enigma/) at 199 EUR; the [LELO Sila Cruise](/products/lelo-sila-cruise/) adds Cruise Control to the same body.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO's official Sila product page and its published specification block, checked on 13 October 2026. Where LELO does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Sila is the pick for someone who wants LELO's sonic stimulation at the gentle end, with a wide mouth and a slow build-up as the design brief. LELO's documentation is good: body-safe silicone over ABS in three colours, 80 x 75 x 35 mm, 105 g, eight modes, a 520 mAh battery giving up to 2 hours of use from a 2-hour charge, 90 days of standby, 100% waterproofing, and a 2-year warranty followed by a 10-year quality guarantee. The caveats are a 169 EUR list price, 60 EUR above the Sona 2, four fewer settings, and a page that publishes a whisper-quiet tile but no decibel figure, frequency or mouth width.

## Format and Who It Is For

Sila is an external clitoral stimulator with a single wide mouth. LELO's sonic waves work without direct contact, and the highlights copy is explicit that the Sila is tuned gently: the wide mouth engages the entire erogenous area, with the vibrations spread at equal intensity across the whole clitoris rather than concentrated in its centre, which LELO says leads to a sensual, slow build-up. LELO's intro copy frames it as a device for self-love exploration and teasing yourself to climax slowly. Three buttons control it: plus to switch on and increase strength, and a mode button to step through the eight vibrations.

This format suits people who have found focused external stimulation too sharp, or who want a gentler first sonic device with a larger contact area. It is not the intensity pick: LELO positions the Sila as gentle, and anyone who wants the most intense sonic setting has the Sona 2 at a lower price. It is external-only, with no insertable element and no app layer, and the 80 x 75 x 35 mm body is short and broad.

## Documented Specifications

LELO's specification block lists the material as body-safe silicone with ABS plastic, in Pink, Lilac and Aqua. The size is 80 x 75 x 35 mm (3.1 x 2.9 x 1.4 in) and the weight is 105 g (0.23 lb). The page lists eight modes and a three-button interface, with automatic switch-off after 20 minutes.

The battery is a Li-ion 520 mAh cell at 3.7 V, charged in 2 hours at 5.0 V / 500 mA, giving up to 2 hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby. The eight modes are recorded here as the patterns count because LELO publishes no separate intensity-level figure. LELO does not publish a noise level in decibels, a frequency, a mouth width, a finish, rated power, an IP rating or app connectivity, so those remain not documented.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

LELO states the Sila is 100% waterproof, suitable for the bath and shower, and describes the surface as extra-soft premium silicone that feels warm to the touch. Body-safe silicone is non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly: rinse under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner, then dry fully before storing it in the supplied pouch. The wide mouth is the main cleaning area, since lubricant collects there. LELO states the product meets the ISO 3533 international safety standard and ships with a USB-charging cord, satin storage pouch, warranty registration card and instruction manual. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone; LELO claims no IP rating, only the 100% waterproof statement.

## Battery and Warranty

Sila is USB-rechargeable, with a 2-hour charge giving up to 2 hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby, and it switches itself off after 20 minutes. LELO documents a 2-year warranty from the purchase date, with a free replacement if a problem occurs in that period provided the receipt is kept, and separately states a 10-year quality guarantee once the base warranty expires. Buyers relying on the extended guarantee should confirm the current registration terms on LELO's warranty page first.

Its page omits the standby time the Sila page states. The Cruise listed at 139 EUR when its review was checked, a figure LELO's own page flagged as promotional that day, so the gap should be re-read at purchase time. On documented features, the Cruise offers more for a buyer who presses firmly; the Sila is the plain version.
