We-Vibe Pivot 2 is a vibrating couples' ring: a stretchy silicone ring worn at the base during partnered sex, with a vibrating body positioned to reach both partners, controlled by a button on the ring or by the We-Vibe app. It is the catalogue's second ring after the [LELO Tor 3](/products/lelo-tor-3/), and at 89 EUR the cheapest couples' device we have reviewed.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on We-Vibe's official Pivot 2 product page and its published specification, feature and in-the-box tabs, checked on 12 October 2026. Where We-Vibe does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Pivot 2 is the pick for a couple who want shared vibration in the simplest wearable format with app control, at the lowest price in the category. The documentation covers safety and ownership: body-safe silicone that is explicitly free of phthalates, BPA and latex, IPX7 waterproofing, a lithium-ion polymer battery with a documented runtime of up to 120 minutes from a charge of up to 90 minutes, and a 2-year warranty. The gaps are physical: We-Vibe publishes no dimensions, inner diameter, weight, level count or noise figure for it, and ring fit is the one thing a buyer most needs to judge. The format caveat is inherent: a ring is a partnered-use device and fit-sensitive by nature.

## Format and Who It Is For

A vibrating ring is worn by one partner at the base, so the vibrating body sits where both partners benefit during penetrative sex. We-Vibe describes the Pivot 2 as soft, stretchy and smooth, easy to put on and take off, with an ergonomic shape and deep, powerful vibrations. Control is by the button on the ring or by the We-Vibe app, which lets a partner adjust the vibration from a phone, in the room or at a distance. No remote is listed.

It suits couples who want a simple shared-vibration toy and already use, or want, the We-Vibe app. It is squarely a partnered-use product, not a solo toy, and because the ring must stretch to fit, buyers who need a specific inner diameter cannot confirm it from the page. Couples who want a worn C-shaped device instead should look at the [We-Vibe Chorus](/products/we-vibe-chorus/) or [Match](/products/we-vibe-match/).

## Documented Specifications

We-Vibe's specification tab files the Pivot 2 under rings and app-enabled toys, in the colour Slate, with a USB-rechargeable lithium-ion polymer battery, a runtime of up to 120 minutes and a charge time of up to 90 minutes. The materials are body-safe silicone, free of phthalates and BPA and made without latex, and the warranty is 2 years.

The feature tab adds the soft, stretchy construction, IPX7 waterproofing, USB charging, deep vibrations, the ergonomic shape and the We-Vibe app. The in-the-box tab lists the ring, a USB cable with no adapter, a quick-start guide and safety notes. We-Vibe's specification and feature tabs do not publish dimensions, an inner diameter, a weight, a count of intensity levels or modes, or a noise level for the Pivot 2. Two of the page's marketing graphics do state four intensities with six patterns and magnetic charging, but those figures appear only in images, not in the specification, so this review reports them as graphic claims rather than documented values.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

The ring is body-safe silicone with an explicit phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free statement, which matters for a device worn in close contact by both partners. It is rated IPX7, so it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath or shower within the limits of that rating. Clean it after every use, dry it fully, and store it away from other silicone toys. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with a silicone ring.

## Battery and Warranty

The documented figures are up to 120 minutes of use from a charge of up to 90 minutes, charged over a USB cable from any USB port or adapter. The warranty is 2 years, and We-Vibe shows its 100-day pleasure guarantee site-wide; confirm the current terms before relying on either.

## In the Catalogue

### Pivot 2 vs. the LELO Tor 3

The [LELO Tor 3](/products/lelo-tor-3/) is the catalogue's other vibrating ring, at 159 EUR. LELO publishes what We-Vibe does not: 46.7 x 22.2 x 60 mm with a 29 mm inner diameter, a 130 Hz motor, a 60 dB maximum and 100% waterproofing, plus LELO app control. Pivot 2 answers with a 70 EUR lower price, IPX7 and the We-Vibe app. If documented fit matters, the Tor 3 is the safer buy; if price does, the Pivot 2.

The [We-Vibe Chorus](/products/we-vibe-chorus/), [Match](/products/we-vibe-match/) and [LELO Tiani 3](/products/lelo-tiani-3/) are worn C-shaped devices with an internal arm; a ring is the simpler, external alternative with fewer positioning variables.
