LELO Smart Wand 2 Medium is a cordless wand massager: the format with a broad, rounded head on a long handle, where the motor sits in the head and the device is swept over the body rather than held at a single point. LELO positions it as both a muscle massager and an external stimulator, naming the clitoral area among the places its ten massage patterns can be used. It is the smaller of LELO's two Smart Wand 2 sizes; the page also lists a Smart Wand 2 Large, a separate product not covered here.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO's official Smart Wand 2 Medium product page and its published specification block, checked on 12 October 2026. Where LELO does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Smart Wand 2 Medium is the pick for someone who wants the wand format with a premium, fully documented specification sheet. LELO lists body-safe silicone over ABS plastic, 100% waterproofing, a 60 Hz motor, 10 vibration settings, up to two hours of cordless use from a two-hour charge, 90 days of standby, a 155 g weight at 210 mm long, a two-year warranty and a ten-year quality guarantee on top. The gaps are specific: LELO publishes no maximum noise level, documents only a three-button interface with no mention of app or remote control, and the 169 EUR list price is more than double the catalogue's other wand, the Fun Factory VIM, on manufacturer price.

## Format and Who It Is For

A wand is the opposite end of the external-stimulation spectrum from a bullet. Where the [We-Vibe Tango X](/products/we-vibe-tango-x/) concentrates its motor into a small tip for pinpoint stimulation, a wand puts the motor into a broad rounded head and spreads the vibration over a wide contact area, with a long handle that keeps the hand away from the point of contact. That makes it a two-purpose device, massager first and stimulator second, and a physically large one: at 210 x 85 x 45 mm this is a bag device rather than a pocket one, although LELO describes the medium size as convenient for use on the go.

It suits people who respond to broad, diffuse vibration rather than a precise point, people who want one device for both massage and sexual use, and couples who want something easy to use over a partner's body. Anyone who wants internal stimulation should look at the [LELO Mona 2](/products/lelo-mona/) or [Enigma](/products/lelo-enigma/) instead, and anyone who prefers focused external sensation should start with the [Sona 2](/products/lelo-sona-2/) or the Tango X.

## Documented Specifications

LELO's specification block lists body-safe silicone and ABS plastic as the materials, with the feature copy describing the silicone as extra-soft and warm to the touch. The wand measures 210 x 85 x 45 mm and weighs 155 g. The motor runs at 60 Hz, the device switches off automatically after 20 minutes, and LELO claims 30% more concentrated power than its first-edition wand; that claim is noted as stated, since no power or noise measurement accompanies it.

Power is a Li-ion 3.7 V cell rated at 520 mAh, charged in two hours at 5.0 V over the supplied USB charging cord, for up to two hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby. Control is a three-button interface. LELO gives one figure for the settings, 10, and uses it for both vibration settings and massage patterns; it publishes no separate count of intensity steps, so this page records 10 patterns and leaves the level count undocumented.

LELO does not publish a maximum noise level, says nothing about app or remote control, and does not state whether the head flexes. The box contains the wand, a USB charging cord, a satin storage pouch, a warranty registration card and a manual. LELO's catalogue data lists Black, Deep Rose and Ocean Blue colourways; the product page prices the Black variant.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

LELO marks the Smart Wand 2 Medium as 100% waterproof and describes it as suited to the bath and shower, so cleaning is straightforward: rinse under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and dry fully before storage. The body-safe silicone is non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly, and LELO states that its range meets the ISO 3533 standard for sex-toy safety. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone.

## Battery and Warranty

The wand is cordless and rechargeable over USB, with the two-hour charge matching its documented two-hour user time, and a 90-day standby that suits occasional use. Two hours is average for the catalogue rather than a standout; the Sona 2 and Mona 2 document the same figure. LELO documents a two-year warranty from the purchase date and a ten-year quality guarantee that begins when the warranty expires; the guarantee's terms are LELO's own, so confirm the current conditions on its warranty page before relying on it.

## In the Catalogue

### Smart Wand 2 Medium vs. the Fun Factory VIM

The [Fun Factory VIM](/products/fun-factory-vim/) is the catalogue's other wand: a German-made, weighted-motor wand in body-friendly silicone, USB-C rechargeable, with an IPX7 badge that Fun Factory's own care note contradicts, at a 66.95 EUR manufacturer price. On the values both pages document: silicone contact surfaces on both, with LELO also specifying ABS plastic; a 100% waterproof statement from LELO against an IPX7 rating from Fun Factory, two different ways of describing a device that can go in water; and a price gap of roughly 100 EUR. The VIM page documents 437 g and 315 x 61 x 61 mm against LELO's 155 g and 210 x 85 x 45 mm, but no runtime, charge time, noise or warranty, so those cannot be contrasted; LELO documents all of them except noise. The VIM leads on price; the Smart Wand 2 Medium is the better-documented buy.

### Smart Wand 2 Medium vs. the small external vibrators

The [We-Vibe Tango X](/products/we-vibe-tango-x/) is the format contrast: a bullet with 8 documented intensity levels, IPX7 waterproofing and an 89 EUR price, for pinpoint stimulation. The Smart Wand 2 Medium is the broad-surface alternative at 169 EUR, with a documented weight that the Tango X page does not state; both pages document a 2-hour runtime. Which to choose is a question of sensation first: precise versus diffuse.

### Smart Wand 2 Medium vs. the We-Vibe Wand 2

The Smart Wand 2 Medium is the better-documented physical object; the Wand 2 is the better-connected one.
