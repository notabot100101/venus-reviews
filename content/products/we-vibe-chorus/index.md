We-Vibe Chorus is a wearable couples vibrator built for use during intercourse: an adjustable shape that sits inside the vagina, a Squeeze Remote for hands-on control, and Touch-sense technology that reads movement through three app-selected modes. It asks more of a couple than a simple external vibrator does - fit, pairing, and remote placement all matter - but it documents real specifics on battery life, materials, waterproofing and warranty that make it possible to evaluate before buying.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on We-Vibe's official Chorus product page and its published specification table, checked on 13 October 2026. Where We-Vibe does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Chorus suits couples who want a wearable, remote- and app-controlled vibrator for use during penetrative sex, and who are willing to spend a few minutes on fit and pairing. We-Vibe documents a USB-rechargeable lithium-ion battery with a 90-minute runtime and a 120-minute charge, body-safe silicone made without phthalates, BPA or latex, IPX7 waterproofing for the toy body, and a 2-year warranty. The spec table also lists ten named vibration types, with further bonus modes available only through the We-Vibe app. The open questions are intensity-level count, weight and noise level, none of which We-Vibe publishes - and the remote, unlike the toy, is only splashproof.

## Format and Who It Is For

Chorus is worn during penetrative sex rather than used as a standalone toy. We-Vibe describes an adjustable fit that can be shaped to the body and holds its position once set, so the device sits against the G-spot while simultaneously reaching the clitoris and, per We-Vibe, the penis during intercourse - what the brand calls three-way pleasure. Control comes from a Squeeze Remote, where pressing harder increases intensity, and from Touch-sense, which uses sensors under the We-Vibe logo to translate movement into vibration across three modes selected in the We-Vibe app.

It suits couples who already want an in-use, hands-adjustable couples toy and don't mind pairing an app and keeping a remote within reach. It is not the pick for solo use, for anyone who wants a toy that works immediately with no setup, or for anyone who needs the remote itself to be fully waterproof - We-Vibe is explicit that only the toy carries the IPX7 rating.

## Documented Specifications

We-Vibe's specification tab lists a USB-rechargeable lithium-ion battery with a 90-minute runtime and a 120-minute charge time. The material is body-safe silicone, made without phthalates and BPA, and produced without latex. Dimensions are given per component: the massager itself at 78 x 33 x 43.5 mm, the remote at 37 x 96 mm, and the storage box at 91 x 57 x 104 mm. Warranty is listed as two years.

The features tab adds the Squeeze Remote, Touch-sense (three modes, selected in the We-Vibe app), IPX7 waterproofing for the toy body only - the remote is explicitly described as splashproof rather than waterproof - and We-Vibe app control for use at a distance. A separate "Vibrationsarten" list names ten settings: Low, Medium, High, Pulse, Wave, Ultra, Echo, Tide, Surf and Peak, and the page notes further bonus modes available only through the app. We-Vibe does not publish a separate intensity-level count, a weight, or a noise level in decibels for Chorus.

The box is listed as including a charging and storage case (a full charge in about 120 minutes), a discreet storage pouch, a quick-start guide, an illustrated multilingual instruction manual, a 2 mL sample of We-Vibe Lube by pjur, and - for the Crave Coral and Cosmic Blue colourways only - a Positions Playbook.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

The body-safe silicone is documented with an explicit exclusion list - no phthalates, no BPA, no latex - which is a clearer materials statement than many couples toys in this category publish. IPX7 waterproofing covers the toy itself, which matters for a wearable device with curves that can collect lubricant during use.

The important distinction is the remote: We-Vibe states plainly that it is splashproof, not waterproof, so it should be wiped rather than rinsed or submerged. Dry the toy fully before returning it to the storage pouch or charging case, since a damp silicone body stored in a closed space is the one hygiene mistake this format invites. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default for a silicone toy unless We-Vibe's own instructions for a specific lubricant say otherwise.

## Battery and Warranty

We-Vibe documents a 90-minute runtime from a 120-minute charge, using a USB-rechargeable lithium-ion battery that can be charged directly or via the included case. That is a shorter runtime-to-charge ratio than some of the catalogue's solo devices, which is a reasonable trade-off for a couples product running two independently addressed motors alongside an app and remote control layer. The warranty is two years; keep the receipt and buy from a traceable retailer, since app-connected products can need firmware or support follow-up that a warranty claim alone won't resolve.
