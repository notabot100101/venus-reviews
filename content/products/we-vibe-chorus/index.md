## Documented Specifications

Per We-Vibe's live Chorus product page at `https://www.we-vibe.com/us/chorus`, Chorus is described as a remote-controlled couples toy. The page highlights adjustable fit, a Squeeze Remote, Touch-sense, Waterproof IPX7, and AnkorLink technology. We-Vibe says Touch-sense uses touch-sensitive receptors beneath the We-Vibe logo and offers three Touch-sense modes through the We-Vibe app. The page also says AnkorLink creates a stable connection between the toy and the We-Vibe app, with the Squeeze Remote acting as the anchor that needs to stay nearby when using the app.

The visible specification tab lists a USB rechargeable lithium-ion battery, 90 min runtime, and 120 min charge time. Materials are listed as ABS and body-safe silicone, produced without phthalates, BPA, or latex. Dimensions are listed as H: 44 mm, B: 33 mm, L: 78 mm, with the remote listed as 37 x 96 mm. Warranty is listed as two years.

The product page says Chorus is waterproof to IPX7, while the remote control is only splashproof, not fully waterproof. The in-the-box tab documents USB charging compatibility with any USB port or standard USB power adapter, a charging case that fully charges the device in approximately 120 minutes, a discreet storage pouch, quick start guide, illustrated multilingual instruction manual and safety information, a 2 mL We-Vibe Lube by pjur sample, and a Positions Playbook for some color variants.

Exact motor count, decibel level, app platform requirements, Bluetooth range, and full vibration-mode count were not documented clearly in the visible manufacturer specification table.

## Materials and Cleaning Analysis

Chorus has better-than-average material disclosure for an app-enabled couples toy. We-Vibe lists ABS and body-safe silicone and adds that the product is produced without phthalates, BPA, or latex. That gives buyers a clearer basis for comparison than a generic "silicone feel" claim.

IPX7 waterproofing is helpful for cleaning the toy body, especially because wearable couples vibrators have curves and contact surfaces that can collect lubricant. The important distinction is the remote: We-Vibe explicitly says the remote is splashproof, not fully waterproof. That means cleaning should keep the remote away from soaking or rinsing and should follow manufacturer instructions.

The charging case adds convenience but also another surface to keep clean and dry. Store the product only after the silicone body is dry, and do not put a damp toy into a closed pouch or case. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default unless We-Vibe's instructions for the exact product and lubricant say otherwise.

## Published Feedback Synthesis

Published feedback for Chorus-type products tends to reward the idea when it works: couples like having a wearable vibrator that can be adjusted, controlled remotely, and expanded through app patterns. Positive themes usually include partner control, the novelty of shared adjustment, and stronger flexibility than older fixed-shape couples vibrators.

The negative pattern is just as consistent. Wearable couples toys are fit-sensitive. A device can be well made and still miss the right contact points for a particular body pairing. Some buyers also find app pairing, remote placement, or keeping the Squeeze Remote nearby less spontaneous than expected. Chorus is therefore best framed as a couples system, not a simple object.

Another recurring concern is expectation management around "during sex" positioning. The manufacturer positioning is couples-focused, but comfort, stability, and sensation depend on anatomy, pace, and preference. A shopper who wants a guaranteed low-effort result may be happier with a simpler external vibrator or a remote bullet. A couple that enjoys experimenting with positioning and controls is a better fit.

## Who Should Buy It

Choose Chorus if partnered use is the priority, app control sounds useful, and a Squeeze Remote plus adjustable fit is worth the setup. It is strongest for couples who already enjoy experimenting with positioning and who will actually use app modes rather than treating them as a spec-list bonus.

Skip it if you want a simple solo vibrator, dislike app setup, need the lowest price, or want a device with fully documented noise level and Bluetooth range before buying. Also skip it if remote waterproofing matters; the manufacturer says the toy is IPX7 waterproof, but the remote is only splashproof.

## Ownership Notes
