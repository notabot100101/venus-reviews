Fun Factory Manta is an external vibrator built around flexible, wraparound wings rather than a bullet, a palm vibrator, or a wand head. Fun Factory's German product page frames it primarily as a vibrator for the penis, solo or with a partner, but the "Größe, Material & Mehr" block documents a 287 g, 18 cm device with a 6-speed, 6-rhythm motor, a button lock, IPX7 waterproofing and a runtime documented as a 40-to-120-minute range.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Fun Factory's official Manta product page, its "Größe, Material & Mehr" specification block and its published variant data, checked on 13 October 2026. Where Fun Factory does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Manta suits someone who wants a flexible external vibrator and is specifically interested in wraparound pressure rather than pinpoint or broad-surface contact. Fun Factory documents a 287 g, 18 cm body with flexible wings, a 6-speed, 6-rhythm motor, a battery-level indicator, a button lock for travel, and IPX7 waterproofing, with a runtime published as a range (40 to 120 minutes) rather than a single figure, charged in 6 to 8 hours. The manufacturer names only "body-friendly materials" for the contact surface, not silicone specifically, and does not publish a warranty term or a noise figure. Both Petrol and Black were out of stock at the manufacturer when checked, so there is currently no buyable offer directly from Fun Factory.

## Format and Who It Is For

Manta is an external vibrator with two flexible silicone-feeling wings that wrap around the body rather than pressing against a single point. Fun Factory's feature list describes powerful vibration for solo or partnered use, wings that adapt to body shape, a ribbed internal structure meant to hold lubricant in place, and deep, penetrating vibration. A dedicated Funktionen row in the spec block also lists a button lock (Tastensperre), useful for carrying the device without accidentally triggering it.

It suits buyers curious about wraparound contact who are comfortable learning a less familiar shape, and who do not need a confirmed single-figure runtime or a warranty term before buying. It is a less safe first purchase for anyone who wants a conventional, immediately familiar vibrator, and it is currently out of stock at the manufacturer in both colourways, so buyers who want it now will need to check a retailer.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

Fun Factory describes Manta's contact material only as "body-friendly materials, hypoallergenic and non-porous," without naming silicone specifically. That is a weaker material claim than Fun Factory makes for the [VIM](/products/fun-factory-vim/) and [Volta](/products/fun-factory-volta/) pages, both of which explicitly say "Silikon." Non-porous, body-friendly materials are generally suitable for external use when cleaned correctly, and water-based lubricant is the conservative default given the material is not confirmed as silicone.

The flexible wings and ribbed internal structure increase surface area and are designed to hold lubricant, which also means more careful cleaning is needed. The IPX7 rating supports a thorough rinse under running water with mild soap or a toy cleaner, more than a splashproof-only device would allow. Dry the wings and the ribbed texture fully before storage, and inspect the flexible sections for wear over time.

## Battery and Warranty

Manta runs on a built-in rechargeable battery with a charge-level indicator, using a charging cable that Fun Factory's box-contents line notes ships without a USB adapter - and unlike the VIM and Volta pages, this page does not explicitly label the cable as USB-C. The documented runtime is a range, 40 to 120 minutes, and the charge time is also a range, 6 to 8 hours; Fun Factory does not narrow either to a single figure. No warranty term is published on the page, a gap shared by the [VIM](/products/fun-factory-vim/) and [Volta](/products/fun-factory-volta/) listings. Buyers who want a specific battery life or a warranty window should check the manual or ask the retailer first.
