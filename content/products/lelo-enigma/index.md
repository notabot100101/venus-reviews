LELO Enigma is LELO's dual stimulator: an external mouth that uses the company's SenSonic technology to deliver sonic waves to the clitoris without direct contact, paired with a fully flexible internal arm for the G-spot. It is the catalogue's top-rated dual at a 199 EUR list price, sits 20 EUR below the [LELO Enigma Cruise](/products/lelo-enigma-cruise/) that adds Cruise Control to the same documented body, and is the step up from LELO's external-only sonic devices, the [LELO Sona 2](/products/lelo-sona-2/) and the [LELO Sila](/products/lelo-sila/).

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO's official Enigma product page and its published specification block, checked on 13 October 2026. Where LELO does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Enigma is the pick for someone who already wants blended internal and external stimulation and prefers sonic waves to a vibrating external contact. LELO's documentation is the most complete of any dual in the catalogue: body-safe silicone over ABS, 100 x 50 x 185 mm, a 140 mm insertable length, 196 g, an 850 mAh battery giving up to 2 hours of use from a 2-hour charge, 90 days of standby, eight settings, 100% waterproofing, and a 2-year warranty followed by a 10-year quality guarantee. The caveats are the 199 EUR price, no published noise figure, intensity-level count or app control, and a fit-sensitive dual format that is not a first toy.

## Format and Who It Is For

Enigma has two working ends. The external mouth uses LELO's SenSonic technology: sonic pulses that resonate into the internal structure of the clitoris rather than conventional vibration against the skin, which LELO describes as fast yet gentle stimulation without direct contact. The internal end is a fully flexible arm with what LELO calls ultra-powerful vibrations aimed at the G-spot. LELO's instructions are to position the internal end first, not too deep, adjust the clitoral end, then switch on at the lowest speed.

This format suits people who already want simultaneous internal and external stimulation and prefer a sonic to a vibrating external contact. It is a considered purchase: the 185 mm length, 140 mm insertable arm and 196 g weight are substantial, and two contact points must align with two target areas at once. Buyers who want external-only sonic stimulation should look at the Sona 2 or Sila; buyers who want app control get nothing documented here.

## Documented Specifications

LELO's specification block lists the material as body-safe silicone with ABS plastic, in Deep Rose and Black. The size is 100 x 50 x 185 mm (3.9 x 1.9 x 7.3 in), the insertable length is 140 mm (5.5 in), and the weight is 196 g (0.43 lb). The interface is three buttons, with automatic switch-off after 20 minutes. The frequency is given as 60 Hz / 100 Hz.

The battery is a Li-ion 850 mAh cell at 3.7 V, charged in 2 hours at 5.0 V / 750 mA, giving up to 2 hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby. LELO's features tile states eight settings, recorded here as the patterns count because LELO publishes no separate intensity-level figure. LELO does not publish a maximum noise level, motor count, mouth width, IP rating, finish description or app connectivity, so those remain not documented.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

LELO states the Enigma is 100% waterproof, suitable for the bath and shower, and describes the surface as extra-soft premium silicone that feels silky to the touch. Body-safe silicone is non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly: rinse under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner, then dry fully before storing it in the supplied pouch. The junction between the arms and the inside of the mouth deserve attention, since lubricant collects there. LELO states the product meets the ISO 3533 international safety standard and ships with a USB-charging cord, satin storage pouch, warranty registration card and instruction manual. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone; LELO claims no IP rating, only the 100% waterproof statement.

## Battery and Warranty

Enigma is USB-rechargeable, with a 2-hour charge giving up to 2 hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby, and it switches itself off after 20 minutes. LELO documents a 2-year warranty from the purchase date, with a free replacement if a problem occurs in that period provided the receipt is kept, and separately states a 10-year quality guarantee once the base warranty expires. Buyers relying on the extended guarantee should confirm the current registration terms on LELO's warranty page first.
