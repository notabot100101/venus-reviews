Satisfyer Pro 3+ is a clitoral stimulator that combines Satisfyer's signature air-pulse (Druckwellen) sensation with vibration in a single, conical silicone head, driven by two separately controllable motors so the two mechanisms can be adjusted independently. It is positioned alongside the [Satisfyer Pro 2](/products/satisfyer-pro-2/) as part of the brand's Pro line, with a smaller, lighter body and its own directly accessible official product page.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Satisfyer's official Pro 3+ product page and its published specifications, checked on 12 October 2026. Where Satisfyer does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Pro 3+ is a reasonable pick for someone who wants both sensations - air-pulse and vibration - from a single Satisfyer device without stepping up to a premium brand. The documented basics are solid: 11 pressure-wave intensities and 10 vibration programs across two independent motors, medical-grade silicone over an ABS body, IPX7 waterproofing, a whisper mode, magnetic USB charging and a 15-year warranty, all sourced directly from the product's own official page rather than a retailer listing. The caveats are familiar from the rest of the Satisfyer catalogue: no published runtime, charge time, noise figure or app support, and the device was listed as unavailable on Satisfyer's own store at the time of this review, discounted to 48.97 EUR from a 69.95 EUR list price.

## Format and Who It Is For

Pro 3+ is an external-only clitoral stimulator. Its conical silicone head is shaped to seal around the clitoris and deliver pulsing air, Satisfyer's original category-defining sensation, while a second motor adds vibration at the same time. The two mechanisms are controlled separately, so a user can run air-pulse alone, vibration alone, or both together across the full range of intensity and program combinations. It is designed for solo use and, per Satisfyer, aimed at women.

It suits someone who already knows they respond to Satisfyer's air-pulse sensation and wants the option of adding vibration without buying a second device, or a first-time buyer curious about trying both mechanisms at once; see the [first-time buyer's guide](/first-time-buyer/) for background on how air-pulse compares with vibration generally. Anyone who wants only air-pulse, with no vibration motor to manage, is better served by a single-purpose device.

## Documented Specifications

Satisfyer's own specification table lists a material of ABS plastic and silicone, described in the product copy as super-soft, skin-friendly and medical-grade. The device measures 145 mm long, 48 mm wide and 43 mm high, and weighs 113 g. It is offered in three colours: Rose Gold, Black and Violet.

The control set is the headline feature: 11 pressure-wave intensities and 10 vibration programs, run by two independently controllable motors, which the product copy states adds up to 110 combination possibilities. The page documents a whisper mode for quieter operation and rates the device IPX7 waterproof. Satisfyer does not publish a runtime, a charge time, a maximum noise level in decibels, or app support for this specific model - the "Connect App" links that appear elsewhere on the site point to other Satisfyer products' own app-enabled SKUs, not to a stated feature of the Pro 3+.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

The documented material is medical-grade silicone over an ABS plastic body, with the silicone forming the part that contacts the skin. Satisfyer rates the device IPX7 waterproof, so it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath or shower. The air-pulse opening at the tip is the area to clean most carefully, since fluid can collect inside it; rinse thoroughly and let it dry fully before storage. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone.

## Battery and Warranty

Pro 3+ is rechargeable over an included USB magnetic charging cable, powered by a lithium-ion battery, though Satisfyer does not document the runtime or the charge time on this page. The standout documented claim is the warranty: a 15-year guarantee, stated directly on the product's own page rather than inferred from a retailer listing, which is the same long-horizon cover Satisfyer applies across its Pro line. As always, confirm the warranty's current terms and keep proof of purchase before relying on it.

## In the Catalogue

### vs. the Satisfyer Pro 2

Both the Pro 3+ and the [Satisfyer Pro 2](/products/satisfyer-pro-2/) combine air-pulse and vibration through two separately controllable motors, and both document a 15-year warranty. The published program counts differ slightly - 11 pressure-wave and 10 vibration on the Pro 3+ against 11 pressure-wave and 12 vibration on the Pro 2 - and the Pro 3+ is considerably more compact: 113 g at 145 x 48 x 43 mm against the Pro 2's 203 g at 164 x 65 x 46 mm. Both reviews draw their figures directly from Satisfyer's own product pages, checked in October 2026, and neither page publishes a runtime or a noise figure.

### vs. the Womanizer entry and mid-range models

Against the [Womanizer Starlet 3](/products/womanizer-starlet-3/) at 69 EUR, the comparison is mechanism versus documentation: Starlet 3 offers 6 intensity levels of air-pulse only, but Womanizer publishes an exact 30-minute runtime, a 30-minute charge time and precise dimensions, none of which Satisfyer documents for the Pro 3+. Against the [Womanizer Classic 2](/products/womanizer-classic-2/) at 119 EUR, Classic 2 is pricier but better documented still, with a published 180-minute runtime and a 120-minute charge time, though it is air-pulse only with no vibration motor. A direct older-generation comparison between the brands is on the [Womanizer Premium 2 vs. Satisfyer Pro 2](/womanizer-premium-2-vs-satisfyer-pro-2/) page. The pattern holds for the Pro 3+: Satisfyer answers a thinner spec sheet with a dual mechanism and a long warranty, while Womanizer answers with published battery figures.

### among the air-pulse field

Within the wider contactless category, ranked on the [best air-pulse & sonic stimulators](/best-air-pulse-stimulators/) page, the Pro 3+ sits with the rest of the Satisfyer line - alongside the [Pro 2](/products/satisfyer-pro-2/) and [Curvy Trinity 3](/products/satisfyer-curvy/) - in the lower-documentation, lower-price tier, rather than with the fully specified premium devices like the [LELO Sona 2](/products/lelo-sona-2/) or [Womanizer Premium 2](/products/womanizer-premium-2/). Its distinguishing feature within that tier is the dual air-pulse-plus-vibration mechanism, which neither the Starlet 3 nor the Classic 2 offers.
