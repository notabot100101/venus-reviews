We-Vibe Moxie+ is a wearable panty vibrator: a small, flat external vibrator that sits inside underwear, held in place by a magnetic clip on the outside of the fabric, and controlled by a bundled remote or the We-Vibe app. It is the catalogue's first panty vibrator, and the external-only counterpart to the [Jive](/products/we-vibe-jive/), We-Vibe's internally worn egg.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on We-Vibe's official Moxie+ product page and its published specification, feature and in-the-box tabs, checked on 12 October 2026. Where We-Vibe does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Moxie+ is the pick for someone who wants discreet, hands-free external stimulation that a partner can control, in the room or at a distance. The documentation is good for the category: body-safe silicone that is explicitly free of phthalates, BPA and latex, IPX7 waterproofing, exact dimensions of 85 x 23 x 27 mm, ten modes, a runtime of up to two hours from a 90-minute charge, and a 2-year warranty, with a splash-proof remote, a spare magnetic clip and a storage pouch in the box. The gaps are a missing level count, weight, noise figure and remote range. The caveat is the format itself: a panty vibrator does one specific thing, and how well it stays in place depends on the underwear.

## Format and Who It Is For

A panty vibrator is a flat external vibrator worn inside underwear against the clitoris, with no insertable part. Moxie+ is held by a magnet: the vibrator sits inside the fabric and a small magnetic clip sits outside, so the garment does the holding. We-Vibe describes the shape as ergonomic and the fit as adjustable, and the control is the point of the product: a wireless remote with intensity control in the box, and the We-Vibe app for control from a partner's phone at any distance.

It suits couples who want a partner-controlled toy for use outside the bedroom, and solo users who want hands-free external stimulation. It is not the pick for someone who wants internal stimulation, which is the [Jive](/products/we-vibe-jive/) in this format, or broad, deep vibration, which is the [Wand 2](/products/we-vibe-wand-2/).

## Documented Specifications

We-Vibe's specification tab lists the Moxie+ in Aqua and Satin Black, with a USB-rechargeable lithium-ion battery, a runtime of up to two hours and a charge time of 90 minutes. The materials are body-safe silicone, free of phthalates, BPA and latex. The body measures 85 x 23 x 27 mm, and the warranty is 2 years.

The feature tab adds ten modes, IPX7 waterproofing, a "whisper-quiet" description without a decibel figure, the splash-proof remote, the ergonomic and adjustable shape and the We-Vibe app. The in-the-box tab lists the Moxie+, a spare magnetic clip, a USB cable with no adapter, a quick guide, the wireless remote, a 2 ml sample of We-Vibe Lube by pjur and a silky storage pouch. We-Vibe does not publish a count of intensity levels, a weight, a noise level or the remote's range.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

The contact surface is body-safe silicone with an explicit phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free statement. Moxie+ is rated IPX7, so the vibrator itself can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner; the remote is only splash-proof and should be kept out of water. Dry the vibrator fully before storage, and keep the magnetic clip and its spare with the pouch so they are not lost. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with a silicone toy.

## Battery and Warranty

The documented figures are up to two hours of use from a 90-minute charge, charged over a USB cable from any USB port or adapter. The warranty is 2 years, and We-Vibe shows its 100-day pleasure guarantee site-wide; confirm the current terms before relying on either.

## In the Catalogue

### Moxie+ vs. the Jive

The [We-Vibe Jive 2](/products/we-vibe-jive/) is the other wearable in the range, at the same 129 EUR: an egg worn internally with the same app and remote control and a documented 4-hour runtime. Moxie+ is the external-only alternative with half the documented runtime but exact dimensions, a bundled remote and a magnetic clip that needs no insertion. The choice is internal versus external, not features.

### Moxie+ vs. the worn couples toys

The [Match](/products/we-vibe-match/) and [Chorus](/products/we-vibe-chorus/) are C-shaped devices worn during partnered sex. Moxie+ is a different kind of couples toy: it is worn under clothes, before or instead of sex, with the partner on the remote or the app. All of them are ranked on the [best couples' toys](/best-couples-toys/) page.

### Moxie+ among the We-Vibe range

Moxie+ and the Jive are We-Vibe's two wearables for discreet play. The [Rave 2](/products/we-vibe-rave-2/) is the internal G-spot vibrator, the [Nova 2](/products/we-vibe-nova-2/) the rabbit, the [Tango X](/products/we-vibe-tango-x/) the bullet and the [Melt](/products/we-vibe-melt/) the air-pulse device, all on the same We-Vibe app. The full line-up is on the [We-Vibe reviews hub](/we-vibe-reviews/).

The reason to buy it is specific: a documented, IPX7, body-safe panty vibrator with both a remote and app control from a brand with a 2-year warranty. The reasons to hesitate are the undocumented level count, weight and noise, and a format that only makes sense if discreet, partner-controlled external play is the goal.
