LELO Lily 3 is the catalogue's smallest, lightest external vibrator: a lay-on clitoral device LELO markets as a "silent mini vibrator," built around a flat, palm-sized body rather than an insertable shaft or shaped tip. It reissues the product that launched LELO twenty years ago, now with a documented 10-setting vibration range, an ABS-and-silicone build, and 100% waterproofing, at a 99 EUR list price.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO's official Lily 3 product page and its published specification block, checked on 12 October 2026. Where LELO does not document a value clearly, this page records it as not documented rather than filling the gap.

## Quick Verdict

Lily 3 is a straightforward pick for buyers who want simple, external-only clitoral vibration in the smallest and lightest body in this comparison: 39 x 34 x 74 mm, 45 g, ABS with a silicone soft-touch coating. LELO documents it thoroughly for such a simple device - battery, charging, user time, standby, frequency, noise level, 100% waterproofing and a two-tier warranty are all stated, alongside 10 settings. The trade-offs are a 60 dB noise ceiling on the louder side of the catalogue, no stated app or Bluetooth control, and a 99 EUR price that sits in the middle of the range rather than at a clear budget or premium position.

## Format and Who It Is For

Lily 3 is an external-only, lay-on vibrator: a smooth, rounded body meant to rest against the clitoris rather than being inserted or shaped around a specific target. LELO describes it as "sculpted for your pleasure," fitting the body "like a glove," and positions it as travel-ready: "compact and discreet." The interface is simple - two buttons control power and the 10 settings - with no app, remote or additional control mode documented.

This format suits buyers who want an uncomplicated, pocket-sized external vibrator for solo or partnered use, including first-time buyers who want a low-complexity starting point - see the catalogue's [first-time buyer guide](/first-time-buyer/) for format comparisons. It is not the right pick for anyone who wants internal, dual or app-controlled stimulation, or who is noise-sensitive, since LELO's documented maximum is 60 dB rather than a whisper-quiet figure.

## Documented Specifications

LELO's specification block lists the material as ABS with a silicone soft-touch coating, a finish described simply as "smooth," and overall dimensions of 39 x 34 x 74 mm at a weight of 45 g - the lightest device compared in this review. The motor runs at a documented frequency of 93 Hz, with a maximum noise level stated as 60 dB, an exact figure rather than an upper bound.

Power comes from a Li-Ion cell rated 3.7 V at 300 mAh, charged via USB in up to 2 hours, giving a documented user time of up to 2 hours and standby of up to 90 days between charges. The device switches off automatically after 20 minutes of inactivity. LELO states 10 vibration settings, described as ranging "from a teasing murmur to a satisfying pulse," which this review records as the patterns figure; the page does not separately state a distinct intensity-"levels" count, so that field is left undocumented rather than assumed equal to the settings total. The interface is a simple 2-button layout, and no app, Bluetooth or remote-control feature is mentioned anywhere on the page.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

LELO states Lily 3 is 100% waterproof, certified to the IPX7 standard, which LELO's own copy ties to use "in the bath or the shower." Cleaning follows the usual approach for a waterproof silicone-surfaced toy: rinse thoroughly under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner, then dry fully before storage in the included satin pouch. LELO states the product meets the ISO 3533 international safety standard for sex toys, which the manufacturer describes as covering both device safety and the body-safety of materials in contact with the genitalia. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with a silicone-coated surface.

## Battery and Warranty

Lily 3 is USB-rechargeable, with a documented charge time of up to 2 hours producing up to 2 hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby - figures that are average for the catalogue rather than a standout, and shorter in absolute terms than some larger devices simply because the battery itself is smaller. LELO documents a 2-year warranty from the purchase date, and separately states a further 10-year "quality guarantee" that applies once the base 2-year warranty expires, for a combined 12 years of stated cover. Buyers who want to rely on the extended guarantee should confirm current registration terms on LELO's warranty page before purchase.

## In the Catalogue

### Lily 3 vs. the We-Vibe Tango X

The [We-Vibe Tango X](/products/we-vibe-tango-x/) is the catalogue's other small external vibrator: an 89 EUR bullet rather than Lily 3's flat lay-on body. Tango X states 8 intensity levels across 7 patterns, but We-Vibe's page leaves runtime, charge time, weight and noise undocumented, so a direct battery or noise comparison is not possible. Lily 3 costs 10 EUR more but documents considerably more: a 45 g weight, full dimensions, a 60 dB noise ceiling, and both runtime and charge time. The [Fun Factory Volta](/products/fun-factory-volta/) is a third small external option at 51.95 EUR with 6 patterns and IPX7, but likewise no stated runtime, charge time or noise figure - making Lily 3 the most fully documented of the three on power and acoustic specifications, if not the cheapest.

### Lily 3 vs. the We-Vibe Touch X

The [We-Vibe Touch X](/products/we-vibe-touch-x/) is the closest rival in format and price: a lay-on vibrator at the same 99 EUR with 8 intensity levels and 7 patterns, exact 102 x 43 x 34 mm dimensions, IPX7 and a lock function, but no published weight or noise figure. The Lily 3 answers with a documented 45 g and a 60 dB maximum; the Touch X with the level-and-pattern split and the travel lock.

### Lily 3 vs. the Mia 3

The [LELO Mia 3](/products/lelo-mia-3/) is LELO's other 99 EUR mini: a 22 x 22 x 111 mm lipstick-shaped vibrator at 36 g with 8 settings, a transport lock, 100% waterproofing, a documented under-60 dB ceiling and a 90-minute runtime. The Lily 3 is the lay-on shape with 10 settings and a two-hour runtime; the Mia 3 is the pocketable pinpoint shape. Both carry LELO's 2-year warranty.

### Lily 3 vs. the sonic LELOs

Lily 3 vibrates conventionally; it does not use LELO's sonic-wave technology. The [LELO Sona 2](/products/lelo-sona-2/) and [LELO Sila](/products/lelo-sila/) are LELO's sonic clitoral stimulators, pulsing air and surface contact rather than vibrating a motor against the body. Sona 2 matches Lily 3's 60 dB noise ceiling but costs 109 EUR, weighs 125 g, and offers 12 patterns against Lily 3's 10. Sila is gentler by design, costs 169 EUR, and does not document a noise figure. Buyers who want sonic-wave stimulation should look to Sona 2 or Sila; buyers who want conventional vibration in the smallest, lightest body should stay with Lily 3.

### Lily 3 among the LELO range

Within LELO's catalogue, Lily 3 occupies the brand's original, entry-level external-vibrator position, reissued for LELO's 20th anniversary rather than introducing a new mechanism. It is simpler than the sonic Sona 2 and Sila, and far smaller than shaped or dual-stimulation devices elsewhere in the range.
