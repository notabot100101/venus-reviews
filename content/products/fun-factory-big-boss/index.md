Fun Factory Big Boss is the largest G-spot vibrator in the Venus catalogue, and the name is the whole pitch: a realistic-shaped shaft built for size and power. Unlike the brand's [Stronic G](/products/fun-factory-stronic/) and [Stronic Real](/products/fun-factory-stronic-real/), Big Boss is a true vibrator, not a pulsator. Fun Factory's "Material & Mehr" block documents a 350 g, 230 x 76 x 45 mm full-silicone body over an ABS handle, 6 speeds and 6 patterns, IPX7 waterproofing and USB-C charging.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Fun Factory's official Big Boss product page, its "Material & Mehr" specification block and its published variant data, checked on 12 October 2026. Where Fun Factory does not document something, or where the page contradicts itself, this review says so rather than guessing at a single figure.

## Quick Verdict

Big Boss suits someone who has tried a standard-sized G-spot vibrator and wants more of everything: length, girth, weight, motor. Fun Factory documents a full-silicone 230 x 76 x 45 mm shaft at 350 g, a slight G-spot curve, a ribbed LOOP handle, 6 speeds and 6 patterns (12 programmes total), IPX7 waterproofing and USB-C charging, at 49.95 EUR with Purple in stock when checked. It also carries a rare credential: Stiftung Warentest rated it "gut (1,8)" for safety in issue 11/25. Against that, no runtime, charge time, warranty or noise figure is published, the page contradicts itself on the second colour's name and on girth, and 350 g at 76 mm wide is a large-format choice, not a general-purpose first vibrator.

## Format and Who It Is For

Big Boss is a realistic-shaped insertable vibrator: a defined head, stylised veins, and a slight upward curve Fun Factory aims at the G-spot ("Die leichte Krümmung trifft den G-Punkt punktgenau"). The shaft is cast in what the feature list calls high-quality full silicone ("hochwertigem Vollsilikon"), said to flex with movement. Power comes from vibration alone; there is no thrusting mechanism, which separates it from the brand's Stronic pulsators despite the similar shape. Controls sit on the same ribbed LOOP handle used on the [Miss Bi](/products/fun-factory-miss-bi/) and [Volta](/products/fun-factory-volta/), meant to hold even with wet hands.

The defining fact is size: 230 mm long, 76 mm wide, the widest and heaviest G-spot vibrator Venus has reviewed from Fun Factory. It suits people who want a filling, high-presence toy and find standard sizes too slight, and couples wanting a shared toy, which the page suggests. It is not the place to start for anyone new to internal toys, anyone who prefers a compact device, or anyone who needs a confirmed runtime or warranty.

## Documented Specifications

Fun Factory's "Größe, Material & Mehr" block lists the following, verified on 12 October 2026:

- Weight: 350 g
- Length: 230 mm
- Width: 76 mm
- Height: 45 mm
- Material: silicone, ABS plastic ("Silikon, ABS Kunststoff"); the feature list calls the shaft "hochwertigem Vollsilikon", high-quality full silicone
- Colour: the spec block lists Black and Pink, but the buy box and variant data list Black and Purple, article numbers FF000950BK01 and FF000950PL01 - the page disagrees with itself on the second colour's name
- Waterproof: yes, IPX7 ("Wasserdicht ja (IPX7)")
- Battery: one rechargeable battery ("1x Akku"), charged over USB-C
- Vibration: 6 speeds and 6 patterns ("6 STUFEN + 6 MUSTER"), summed in the feature list as 12 vibration programmes

Marketing copy elsewhere gives a circumference of 4.5 cm in one bullet and a diameter of up to 4.6 cm in body text; the two disagree with each other and with the spec block's 76 mm width, so this review uses the spec block's measured width. Not documented: runtime, charge time, noise level, warranty term, insertable length, app or remote control - the FAQ says charge time "differs by model" and points to the manual instead of a figure.

## Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning

The contact surface is silicone, described in brand copy as body-friendly and, for this product, as full silicone over an ABS handle that houses the controls. Silicone is non-porous and suitable for internal use when cleaned correctly; water-based lubricant is the conservative choice. The IPX7 rating means the device can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a toy cleaner and used in the bath. Pay attention to the seam where silicone meets the ABS handle, and dry fully before storage.

## Battery and Warranty

Big Boss runs on a built-in rechargeable battery over USB-C, so any standard cable works. That is where the documentation stops: no runtime is published, and the FAQ says charge time varies by model without giving a figure. No warranty term appears on the page, a gap shared by the [Stronic G](/products/fun-factory-stronic/), [Stronic Real](/products/fun-factory-stronic-real/) and [Miss Bi](/products/fun-factory-miss-bi/) pages, so buyers who want certainty should check the manual or ask the retailer first.

## In the Catalogue

### Big Boss vs. the LELO G-spot vibrators

The [LELO Mona](/products/lelo-mona/) at 139 EUR documents 128 g, 196.2 x 53.1 x 42 mm, a 120-minute runtime from a 120-minute charge, 6 patterns, a 2-year warranty and a 50 dB noise ceiling. The [LELO Gigi 2](/products/lelo-gigi-2/) at 109 EUR is lighter still at 79 g, 163 x 35 x 42 mm, with a 240-minute runtime. Big Boss is roughly 2.7 times the Mona's weight and nearly double its width, at a third of the price - but LELO documents runtime, charge time and, for the Mona, warranty and noise, none of which Fun Factory publishes.

### Big Boss vs. the Stronic pulsators

Despite the similar silhouette, Big Boss works differently than the [Stronic G](/products/fun-factory-stronic/) (295 g, 209 x 49 x 37 mm, 69.95 EUR) and [Stronic Real](/products/fun-factory-stronic-real/) (275 g, 202 x 40 x 35 mm, 67.95 EUR): those thrust and do not vibrate, while Big Boss vibrates and does not thrust. It is also the larger device at 350 g and 76 mm wide, yet the cheapest at 49.95 EUR. Choose by mechanism first, then by size.

### Big Boss among the Fun Factory range

Big Boss sits alongside the [Miss Bi](/products/fun-factory-miss-bi/) rabbit at 53.95 EUR as a vibrating internal option, distinct from the thrusting Stronic pair and external toys like the [VIM](/products/fun-factory-vim/) wand. It is the largest and least expensive of Fun Factory's G-spot-oriented vibrators Venus has reviewed, and the only one with a published Stiftung Warentest result. ## Value Position

At 49.95 EUR direct from the manufacturer, with no discount and Purple in stock when checked, Big Boss is the least expensive Fun Factory G-spot device here and sits in the sub-90 EUR band on the [budget picks](/budget-picks/) page. The price buys a fully measured, IPX7, USB-C vibrator with a documented 6-speed, 6-pattern motor and an independent Stiftung Warentest safety rating - a combination nothing else at this price matches. It does not buy a documented runtime, charge time or warranty, and the page's inconsistencies on colour and girth are worth confirming first. It rates 3.5, in line with Fun Factory's other battery-thin models, for a toy that is a specific choice for size and power rather than a general-purpose first vibrator. Buyers weighing format first should start with the [catalogue overview](/best-vibrators-2026/).
