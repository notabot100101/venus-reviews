### How to pick within the category

**If you want external and internal stimulation at once:** the [LELO Enigma](/products/lelo-enigma/). It pairs SenSonic external stimulation with internal G-spot vibration in one 100% waterproof body, which is what earns it the top rating. It is a dual device rather than a pure G-spot toy, so it is the pick when you want both. The [LELO Enigma Cruise](/products/lelo-enigma-cruise/) is the same format with Cruise Control at 219 EUR: 100 x 50 x 185 mm, 196 g, a 140 mm insertable length, 8 settings and 100% waterproof, though its page publishes no charge time or noise figure.

**If you want a dedicated, travel-friendly G-spot device:** the [LELO Gigi 2](/products/lelo-gigi-2/). Its flattened tip is a genuine design difference for broad front-wall contact, it is light at 79 g with a documented insertable 95 mm and 100% waterproofing, and it runs up to 4 hours per charge. LELO does not publish a noise figure for it.

**If you want a firmer, more pronounced curve:** the [LELO Mona](/products/lelo-mona/). A longer, firmer body with a purposeful curve for targeted internal pressure, 100% waterproof with a documented 50 dB maximum. It is aimed at people who already like targeted G-spot toys rather than absolute beginners.

**If you want a well-documented, app-controlled rabbit:** the [We-Vibe Nova 2](/products/we-vibe-nova-2/). Its flexible external arm is built to keep clitoral contact as the device moves, it is rated IPX7 with a clear body-safe materials statement, and the We-Vibe app adds remote control. It is the rabbit to pick when documentation and features matter.

**If you want a rabbit whose tip moves rather than only vibrates:** the [LELO Soraya Wave](/products/lelo-soraya-wave/). Its WaveMotion shaft rises and falls in a finger-like motion against the front wall while the external arm vibrates, with a fully documented spec sheet: 218 x 72 x 46 mm, 185 g, a 110 mm insertable length, a 50 dB maximum, 100% waterproof and a 2-year warranty, at 199 EUR. The [LELO Ina Wave 2](/products/lelo-ina-wave-2/) is the smaller, lighter WaveMotion rabbit at the same price: 200 x 65 x 45 mm, 176 g, 12 settings and two synchronised motors, with no noise figure published.

**If you want an ergonomic curve from a reputable brand:** the [Dame Arc](/products/dame-arc/). Its angled handle keeps the wrist comfortable while the bulbed tip presses the front wall, it uses medical-grade silicone with a 3-year warranty, and it works internally or externally. The catch is that Dame prices in US dollars, so European buyers face conversion and import.

**If you want app control and an adjustable angle:** the [We-Vibe Rave 2](/products/we-vibe-rave-2/). An asymmetrical shape that We-Vibe describes as adjustable to the body, IPX7, body-safe silicone and a documented 120-minute runtime from a 90-minute charge, controlled by buttons or the We-Vibe app, at 139 EUR. We-Vibe publishes no level count, weight or noise figure for it.

**If vibration is not what your G-spot responds to:** the [Fun Factory Stronic G](/products/fun-factory-stronic/). It is a pulsator, not a vibrator: it thrusts by itself, hands-free, for people whose G-spot wants pressure and motion rather than buzz. It is one of two thrusting devices in the catalogue; the [Fun Factory Stronic Real](/products/fun-factory-stronic-real/) is the realistic-shaped sibling with 7 strengths and 3 rhythms, a documented 202 x 40 x 35 mm body and a 2-hour runtime at 67.95 EUR. Both are sensation choices rather than spec comparisons.

**If you want dual stimulation at the lowest price:** the [Fun Factory Miss Bi](/products/fun-factory-miss-bi/). A silicone rabbit with a documented 173 x 76 x 38 mm body, 230 g, IPX7, USB-C charging and 12 programmes at 53.95 EUR, though Fun Factory publishes no runtime, charge time or warranty term for it.

**If you want a value rabbit:** the [Lovehoney Desire](/products/lovehoney-desire/). A mainstream dual-motor rabbit at the lowest price here, but several specifications were not documented by the manufacturer when we reviewed it, so treat materials, waterproofing and warranty as items to confirm on the retailer page before buying.
