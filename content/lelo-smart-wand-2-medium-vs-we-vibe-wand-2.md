## LELO Smart Wand 2 Medium vs We-Vibe Wand 2

These are the two premium cordless wands in the catalogue, and they answer different questions. The LELO Smart Wand 2 Medium is the fully measured object: size, weight, settings, waterproofing, runtime and charge time are all published, and control is three buttons. The We-Vibe Wand 2 is the connected one: a remote in the box, the We-Vibe app, and Smart Silence, with a thinner physical spec sheet. They sit 10 EUR apart, so the choice is about documentation versus connectivity, not price.

Both reviews are built from the manufacturers' official specifications, not physical testing. Prices and specs were verified on the dates stated in each review; "not documented" means the manufacturer's page does not publish the value.

### What each one does better

The **Smart Wand 2 Medium** is the better-documented buy. LELO publishes 210 x 85 x 45 mm and 155 g, so you know what you are holding before it arrives, plus a 60 Hz motor, 10 settings, a two-hour runtime from a two-hour charge, a 100% waterproof statement and a two-year warranty backed by LELO's ten-year quality guarantee. What it lacks is any connectivity: three buttons and nothing else, and no published noise figure.

The **We-Vibe Wand 2** is the more controllable one. It ships with a remote, pairs with the We-Vibe app for partner control at any distance, and its Smart Silence keeps the motor off until the head touches skin, which We-Vibe says saves battery as well as noise. It documents IPX7, a 120-minute runtime, more than ten intensity levels and a flexible, ridged head. What it lacks is the object itself: We-Vibe publishes no dimensions, no weight and no noise figure, and its 150-minute charge is the slower of the two.

### Which to buy

Choose the **Smart Wand 2 Medium** if you want a wand whose size, weight and runtime are on paper, with the longest cover, and you do not need an app or remote. Choose the **We-Vibe Wand 2** if partner control, a remote and Smart Silence matter more than knowing the exact dimensions, and you want to save 10 EUR.
