## Best Rabbit Vibrators

A rabbit is a dual-stimulation vibrator: an insertable shaft curved toward the G-spot and a shorter external arm that vibrates against the clitoris at the same time. It is the format most people picture when they think of a vibrator, and the one with the biggest fit caveat: the distance between the shaft and the arm is fixed, so whether the arm lands where it should depends on anatomy, which is why the better designs give the arm some flex. ### How to pick within the category

**If you want the arm to stay in contact as you move:** the [We-Vibe Nova 2](/products/we-vibe-nova-2/). Its flexible external arm is built to bend and keep clitoral contact rather than lifting off, it is rated IPX7 with a clear body-safe materials statement, and the We-Vibe app adds remote control, at 149 EUR.

**If you want a tip that moves rather than only vibrates:** the [LELO Soraya Wave](/products/lelo-soraya-wave/). Its WaveMotion shaft rises and falls in a finger-like motion against the front wall while the arm vibrates, and LELO publishes the fullest spec sheet of the four: 218 x 72 x 46 mm, 185 g, a 110 mm insertable length, a 50 dB maximum, 100% waterproof and a 2-year warranty, at 199 EUR.

**If you want the WaveMotion tip in a smaller, lighter body:** the [LELO Ina Wave 2](/products/lelo-ina-wave-2/). The same rising-and-falling WaveMotion shaft and clitoral arm as the Soraya Wave, driven by two synchronised motors, in a 200 x 65 x 45 mm, 176 g body with a 110 mm insertable length, 12 settings, 100% waterproof and a 2-year warranty, at the same 199 EUR; LELO publishes no noise figure for it.

**If you want dual stimulation at the lowest price with real documentation:** the [Fun Factory Miss Bi](/products/fun-factory-miss-bi/). A silicone rabbit with a documented 173 x 76 x 38 mm body, 230 g, IPX7, USB-C charging and 12 programmes at 53.95 EUR, though Fun Factory publishes no runtime, charge time or warranty term.

**If price is everything:** the [Lovehoney Desire](/products/lovehoney-desire/). The cheapest-rated rabbit here, but several of its specifications were not documented by the manufacturer when reviewed, so confirm materials, waterproofing and warranty on the retailer page before buying.
