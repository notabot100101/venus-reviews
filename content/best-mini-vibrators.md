### How to pick within the category

**If you want the best-rated and cheapest in euros:** the [We-Vibe Tango X](/products/we-vibe-tango-x/). A slim bullet with 8 intensity levels and 7 modes, IPX7 and a 2-year warranty at 89 EUR, the top-rated device here at 4.5. We-Vibe publishes no runtime, weight or noise figure for it, so it wins on rating and price rather than on documentation.

**If you want a lay-on mini with a published noise figure:** the [LELO Lily 3](/products/lelo-lily-3/). A 45 g, 39 x 34 x 74 mm body in ABS with a silicone soft-touch coating, 10 settings, 100% waterproof, a two-hour runtime from a two-hour charge and a documented 60 dB maximum, at 99 EUR.

**If you want the most pocketable shape:** the [LELO Mia 3](/products/lelo-mia-3/). A 36 g lipstick-shaped tube, 22 x 22 x 111 mm, with 8 settings, a transport lock, 100% waterproofing and the same 60 dB maximum, at 99 EUR. Its documented runtime is 90 minutes.

**If you want a lay-on body with a flexible tip and a travel lock:** the [We-Vibe Touch X](/products/we-vibe-touch-x/). 102 x 43 x 34 mm, 8 levels and 7 patterns, IPX7, a two-hour runtime from a 90-minute charge and a lock function, at 99 EUR; We-Vibe publishes no weight or noise figure.

**If you want a palm-held shape from a body-safe brand:** the [Dame Pom](/products/dame-pom/). Medical-grade silicone, 83 x 56 x 30 mm, 5 levels and 5 patterns, USB-C and a 3-year warranty, at 103 USD; Dame states it is waterproof without an IP rating.

**If you want the cheapest entry that also works internally:** the [Dame Dip](/products/dame-dip/). A slim 147 x 33 mm silicone vibrator with 5 levels on a single steady pattern, a 90-minute runtime and a 3-year warranty, at 44 USD, the lowest price in the catalogue. One pattern and USD pricing are the trade-offs.
