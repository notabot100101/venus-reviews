## We-Vibe Tango X vs Satisfyer Pro 2

These two get compared because they sit at a similar price, but they are not really rivals: one vibrates and one pulses air. Deciding between them is about which sensation you want, not which is better made.

### The real choice

The Tango X is a compact bullet that delivers a broad, rumbly vibration rather than the thin buzz of cheap bullets, which is why it rates higher. It is waterproof with a body-safe silicone contact surface and strong value for a premium brand at 89 EUR. We-Vibe documents a 120-minute runtime from a 90-minute charge, a 99.8 x 22.9 x 24.3 mm body, silicone made without phthalates, BPA or latex, and a 2-year warranty; it publishes no weight or noise figure, and the review says so.

The Satisfyer Pro 2 is the inexpensive entry into pressure-wave, a sensation the Tango X cannot produce. Satisfyer publishes no runtime or noise figure for it, but the documented 15-year warranty and a 79.95 EUR list price that is usually discounted make it a low-risk way to test whether air-pulse works for you.

### Which to buy

Choose the **Tango X** if you want precise, rumbly vibration you control directly, and the higher overall rating. Choose the **Satisfyer Pro 2** if you are curious about the contactless air-pulse sensation and want the cheapest honest way to try it.
