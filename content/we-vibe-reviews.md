### How to choose a We-Vibe

**For couples**, the [Chorus](/products/we-vibe-chorus/) is the flagship: a worn C-shape with a Squeeze Remote, touch-sense control and a full app ecosystem. The [Match](/products/we-vibe-match/) is the simpler, cheaper worn alternative at 139 EUR, remote-only rather than app-connected.

**For solo external stimulation**, the [Tango X](/products/we-vibe-tango-x/) is the compact bullet: pinpoint, waterproof and surprisingly rumbly for its size, which is why it shares the top We-Vibe rating at the lowest price. The [Touch X](/products/we-vibe-touch-x/) is the lay-on alternative at 99 EUR: a wider body with a flexible tip, the same 8 levels and 7 patterns, exact 102 x 43 x 34 mm dimensions and a lock function, with no app. It is also weighed against air-pulse in [We-Vibe Tango X vs Satisfyer Pro 2](/we-vibe-tango-x-vs-satisfyer-pro-2/).

**For broad, deep external vibration**, the [Wand 2](/products/we-vibe-wand-2/) is the cordless wand: a flexible, ridged head, more than ten intensity levels, Smart Silence, IPX7, a documented 120-minute runtime and both a remote and app control at 159 EUR. It is the opposite answer to the Tango X, broad contact rather than a pinpoint, and is compared with the other wands on the [best wand vibrators](/best-wand-vibrators/) page.

**For contactless stimulation**, the [Melt](/products/we-vibe-melt/) is We-Vibe's Pleasure Air device: pulsing air and gentle suction rather than vibration, with app and partner control built in. It is ranked against the rest of the category in [best air-pulse & sonic stimulators](/best-air-pulse-stimulators/).

**For internal and dual stimulation**, the [Nova 2](/products/we-vibe-nova-2/) is We-Vibe's rabbit: an internal G-spot shaft plus a flexible external arm built to keep clitoral contact as it moves, with app control. The [Rave 2](/products/we-vibe-rave-2/) is the single-shaft G-spot alternative: an adjustable, asymmetrical shape with the same app and IPX7 rating, a documented 120-minute runtime from a 90-minute charge, at 139 EUR. Both are ranked among the insertables on the [best G-spot & internal vibrators](/best-g-spot-vibrators/) page.

**For prostate stimulation**, the [Vector+](/products/we-vibe-vector/) is We-Vibe's dual prostate-and-perineum massager, controlled by button, remote or app. The [Ditto+](/products/we-vibe-ditto-plus/) is the vibrating plug: worn rather than aimed, IPX7, 120 minutes from a 90-minute charge, with the remote in the box and app control at 129 EUR. It is the catalogue's lower-priced prostate option against the fully documented [LELO Hugo](/products/lelo-hugo/).

**For discreet wearable play**, the [Jive](/products/we-vibe-jive/) is We-Vibe's app- and remote-controlled egg: worn internally and controlled by a partner, in the room or at a distance, with a documented 4-hour runtime and IPX7 waterproofing. It sits alongside the worn couples devices on the [best couples' toys](/best-couples-toys/) page.

**For discreet external wearable play**, the [Moxie+](/products/we-vibe-moxie-plus/) is the panty vibrator: a flat 85 x 23 x 27 mm silicone body held in underwear by a magnetic clip, with ten modes, IPX7, a 2-hour runtime, a splash-proof remote in the box and app control at 129 EUR, the external-only counterpart to the Jive.

**For a worn couples' vibrator without an app**, the [Match](/products/we-vibe-match/) is the remote-only C-shape: fully documented, 11 named modes, 100% waterproof and 139 EUR, the cheapest of the We-Vibe wearables, though it was out of stock on the manufacturer store when checked.
