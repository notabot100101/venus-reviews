## Our Evaluation Philosophy

Venus Reviews is a research and curation site. We compare product specifications, materials, warranty terms, retailer information, and published user feedback so readers can make a more informed shortlist before buying.

**Venus does not conduct physical product trials, lab measurements, timed ownership evaluations, or first-person sensory reviews.** When a recommendation depends on published information rather than direct use, we say so plainly.

## What We Review

Our product pages focus on decision points that can be researched from public or manufacturer-provided information:

- Material disclosures and body-safety claims
- Product dimensions, charging details, water-resistance ratings, and feature lists
- Warranty length, retailer support, and return-policy context when available
- Price positioning across comparable products
- Patterns in published buyer feedback, including recurring praise and complaints
- Fit for common buying priorities such as discretion, travel, beginner comfort, partner use, and cleaning

## Research Sources

We use sources that a careful buyer can verify:

- Manufacturer product pages and documentation
- Retailer listings and published offer details
- Warranty and support pages
- Product manuals where available
- Published buyer reviews and retailer feedback summaries
- Category guides and safety references for materials and care

Affiliate relationships do not change the editorial assessment. Product pages should remain useful even when no current offer is available.

## Evaluation Categories

### 1. Build Quality & Materials

We look for documented materials, body-safety disclosures, construction notes, water-resistance ratings, charging design, and whether the manufacturer provides enough detail for a buyer to judge suitability.

### 2. Performance & Features

We compare published feature sets: modes, controls, charging and battery claims, app or remote functionality, shape, size, flexibility, and category-specific design choices. These are research-based comparisons, not measured performance results.

### 3. Noise & Discretion

We evaluate discretion from manufacturer claims, retailer descriptions, product design, and consistent patterns in published buyer feedback. We avoid presenting decibel numbers unless they are clearly sourced.

### 4. Value & Pricing

We compare feature depth, materials, warranty, retailer availability, and category alternatives. Prices and offers can change, so value language should stay conservative unless the source data is current.

### 5. Cleanability & Maintenance

We review surface texture, waterproof or splashproof claims, charging-port design, shape complexity, storage notes, and manufacturer cleaning guidance.

## Comparative Curation

Each recommendation is placed against similar products where possible:

- Comparable feature sets
- Similar price or category position
- Shared buyer use cases
- Known tradeoffs from published feedback
- Warranty and support differences

This helps readers understand why a product may be a strong fit for one use case and a poor fit for another.

## What We Do Not Claim

We do not claim to:

- Own or physically handle every product
- Run lab measurements
- Publish timed product trials
- Present first-person sensory experience as editorial fact
- Let affiliate commission rates determine editorial conclusions
- Guarantee that published manufacturer or retailer information is complete

## Review Updates

We revisit pages when source information changes or when published buyer feedback reveals a meaningful pattern:

- Material, warranty, or feature documentation changes
- Retailer availability or offer changes
- Safety notices, recalls, or widespread defect reports
- New comparable products that change the recommendation context
- Reader feedback that points to missing or unclear information
