## Websites & Publishing

**What we build:** Hugo static sites deployed to Hostinger via Git, using a multi-agent workflow. Forge builds the site, Pixel generates images, Architect plans the structure, and a human approves every deploy.

**What you get:**
- Custom static site (fast, minimal, mobile-optimized)
- Git-based deployment pipeline
- Brand-compliant visuals
- SEO meta tags and Open Graph
- GDPR-compliant legal pages
- Staging preview before production launch

**Suitable for:** landing pages, business sites, portfolio sites, documentation hubs.

## Email Automation

**What we build:** Agent-driven email classification, triage, draft reply generation, and daily summary workflows. The agent prepares — the human decides what gets sent.

**What you get:**
- Automated message classification and priority sorting
- Draft reply generation with context from your business data
- Daily summary reports
- Lead/contact tracking
- Send gate with human approval on every outgoing message

**Suitable for:** small businesses processing customer inquiries, lead management, support ticket triage.

**What you get:**

- Social media graphics sized per platform
- Quality assurance gate (human review before delivery)
- Reusable prompt and pipeline templates

**Suitable for:** brands, e-commerce, social media campaigns, content marketing.
