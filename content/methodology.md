## Scoring System Explained

Our reviews use a **5-point scale** with quarter-point precision (4.0, 4.25, 4.5, 4.75, 5.0). This granularity allows us to distinguish between products that are "good" versus "exceptional."

### Score Interpretation

| Score | Rating | Meaning |
|-------|--------|---------|
| 4.75-5.0 | ★★★★★ | Exceptional - Best in class |
| 4.25-4.5 | ★★★★☆ | Excellent - Highly recommended |
| 3.75-4.0 | ★★★★☆ | Good - Solid choice with minor caveats |
| 3.25-3.5 | ★★★☆☆ | Average - Works but has limitations |
| Below 3.0 | ★★☆☆☆ | Below average - Consider alternatives |

### Weighted Scoring Breakdown

```
Overall Score = 
  (Build Quality × 0.30) +
  (Performance × 0.30) +
  (Noise/Discretion × 0.20) +
  (Value × 0.15) +
  (Cleanability × 0.05)
```

## Category-Specific Protocols

### Vibrators & Massagers

**Power Testing:**
- Maximum intensity held for 10 minutes (thermal check)
- Minimum intensity usability assessment
- Ramp-up time from off to maximum

**Battery Testing:**
- Continuous use at medium setting
- Standby drain over 48 hours
- Full charge cycle timing

### App-Connected Devices

**Connectivity Testing:**
- Pairing success rate (20 attempts)
- Range testing (1m, 3m, 5m, through walls)
- Connection stability during movement
- App responsiveness (latency measurement)

### Waterproof Claims

**Verification Protocol:**
- Submersion at rated depth for 30 minutes
- Steam exposure testing (shower simulation)
- Drying and functionality verification

## Noise Measurement Standards

### Equipment
- Decibel meter: calibrated annually
- Testing environment: 30 dB ambient baseline
- Measurement distance: 1 meter standard

### Measurement Conditions
1. **Open air**: Product on hard surface
2. **Dampened**: Product on memory foam
3. **Enclosed**: Under light blanket
4. **Contact**: During typical use simulation

### Decibel Reference Scale

| Sound | Decibel Level |
|-------|--------------|
| Whisper | 30 dB |
| Quiet office | 40 dB |
| Normal conversation | 60 dB |
| Vacuum cleaner | 70 dB |

## Material Safety Verification

### Certification Requirements
We verify all body-safe claims through:

- Manufacturer certification documentation
- Independent lab reports (when available)
- Material composition disclosure
- pH testing for silicone products

### Red Flags
Products are immediately flagged if:
- No material certification provided
- Strong chemical odor present
- Surface degradation observed during testing
- Manufacturer refuses material disclosure

## Comparative Testing Matrix

Every product is scored against category benchmarks:

| Metric | Budget Range | Mid-Range | Premium |
|--------|--------------|-----------|---------|
| Price | $25-50 | $75-120 | $150+ |
| Expected Score | 3.5-4.0 | 4.0-4.5 | 4.5-5.0 |
| Battery Life | 60+ min | 90+ min | 120+ min |
| Warranty | 1 year | 2 years | 2-5 years |

## Review Integrity Measures

### Conflict of Interest Prevention
- No manufacturer-paid travel or accommodations
- No pre-publication review by manufacturers
- Affiliate relationships disclosed but never influence scores
- Products purchased anonymously when possible

### Verification Steps
- Multiple testers for flagship products
- Cross-check measurements between reviewers
- Photo/video documentation of testing
- Raw data retention for 2 years

## Update Protocol

### When Reviews Are Updated

**Immediate (within 48 hours):**
- Safety recalls or warnings
- Major firmware updates changing functionality
- Verified widespread defects reported

**Scheduled (quarterly):**
- Price changes affecting value scores
- New competitive products released
- Long-term durability feedback incorporated

**Annual:**
- Full re-testing if product still current
- Complete rewrite if significant changes
- Archive if discontinued

## Limitations & Transparency

### What Our Testing Cannot Capture

- Individual anatomical differences
- Personal preference variations
- Long-term durability beyond 1 year
- Compatibility with all body types

### Statistical Confidence

Our sample size (n=1 per product) means:
- Individual unit variations possible
- Manufacturing defect risk exists
- Reader feedback essential for pattern recognition

## Feedback Integration

### How Reader Reports Affect Reviews

**Confirmed Reports (3+ similar reports):**
- Added to review as "Reader Notes" section
- May trigger re-testing
- Score adjusted if issue verified

**Isolated Reports:**
- Documented but not added to review
- Monitored for pattern development
