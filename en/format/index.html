Hypotheses about the 963ml Format

The following considerations are hypotheses, not validated findings. They serve as a thinking framework for the concept.

Shelf Presence

A 963ml bottle is taller than the 650ml longneck and broader in the shoulder — a different silhouette on shelf. We assume:

  • The changed proportion could stand out visually in the drinks aisle, especially among uniform 330ml and 650ml formats.
  • A crafted premium look (textured glass, minimal label) could distinguish the format from standard bottles.
  • The odd size (963ml rather than 1L) is deliberate: not a standard liter format, but its own category.
Hypothesis: A format that raises questions on shelf — and gets attention as a result.

Shared Occasions

We see potential for use cases beyond individual consumption:

  • Two-person format: A bottle that lasts two people through an average evening — without the pressure of a full sixpack.
  • Bring-along format: For parties, BBQs or picnics where one single bottle per person is too little and a sixpack too heavy.
  • Table format: A bottle that stays on the table, not the fridge.
Hypothesis: Shared occasions create a new consumption situation between single bottle and multipack.

Distribution Assumptions

  • Price per ml could fall between the 330ml single bottle and sixpack (per ml).
  • The format could be distributed through existing on-trade and retail channels.
  • A deposit system analogous to 0.5L bottles would be conceivable.
Hypothesis: Existing distribution channels could accommodate the format without major restructuring.

Note

These format considerations are concept work, not market research. None of these hypotheses have been validated through consumer surveys or trade testing. They are intended for discussion and further development.
