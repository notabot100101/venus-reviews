This is an informal concept study for a Desperados 963ml format — a product proposal that does not (yet) exist. We are sharing this proposal to give a visual sense of a possible product extension.

The Idea

What if Desperados — known for its 330ml single-bottle and 650ml longneck formats — were to introduce a 963ml bottle?

This concept explores the 963ml bottle as a shared format: large enough for two, portable enough for small groups, positioned between the 650ml longneck (more individual) and the classic sixpack (more stock-up oriented).

The idea: a premium bottle for social occasions — barbecue, game night, after-work drinks for two — that goes straight to the table without a trip through the fridge door.
