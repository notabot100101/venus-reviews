LELO Enigma vs LELO Enigma Cruise #

The Enigma is the catalogue’s top-rated dual stimulator: SenSonic sonic waves for the clitoris and a flexible internal arm for the G-spot in one 100% waterproof body. The Enigma Cruise is the same format with one addition, Cruise Control, which keeps a reserve of power and releases it when the device is pressed hard, so the stimulation does not fade under pressure. LELO calls the Enigma the Cruise’s predecessor without saying it is replaced, and both were on sale when checked, 20 EUR apart.

Both reviews are built from the manufacturers’ official specifications, not physical testing. Prices and specs were verified on the dates stated in each review; “not documented” means the manufacturer’s page does not publish the value.

What each one does better #

The Enigma is the cheaper and higher-rated of the two, at 199 EUR and 4.5, and its page documents the two-hour charge time that the Cruise page leaves out. On the figures both pages publish, 8 settings, 196 g, a two-hour runtime, 100% waterproofing and a two-year warranty, they are the same device.

The Enigma Cruise adds Cruise Control, the one functional difference, and publishes its motor frequencies, 60 Hz and 100 Hz, which the Enigma page does not. Everything else on its sheet, the 100 x 50 x 185 mm size, the 140 mm insertable length, the 196 g weight, the 8 settings, the two-hour runtime, is the same as the Enigma’s. It costs 219 EUR, publishes no charge time and, like the Enigma, no noise figure.

Which to buy #

Choose the Enigma unless Cruise Control is specifically what you want: it is 20 EUR cheaper, rated higher, and documents its charge time. Choose the Enigma Cruise only if you press hard during use and want the power held in reserve for that.

The Enigma is compared with LELO’s focused external pick in Enigma vs Sona 2; both duals sit among the insertables on the best G-spot and internal vibrators page and against the rabbits on the best rabbit vibrators page. The full LELO line is on the LELO reviews hub.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Enigma and the Enigma Cruise?

Both pair SenSonic sonic external stimulation with an internal G-spot arm, with 8 settings, 196 g, a two-hour runtime and 100% waterproofing. The Enigma Cruise adds Cruise Control, which holds power in reserve and releases it when the device is pressed hard against the body, and its page publishes the motor frequencies. Both pages give the same 100 x 50 x 185 mm size and 140 mm insertable length. The Cruise costs 20 EUR more, and its page omits the charge time the Enigma page states.

Does the Enigma Cruise replace the Enigma?

LELO calls the Enigma the Cruise's predecessor but does not say it is discontinued; both were listed for sale when checked. The Enigma holds the higher rating in our catalogue, 4.5 against 4.0.

Which is better value?

The Enigma, at 199 EUR against 219 EUR, unless Cruise Control is the feature you want. The documented specifications are otherwise near-identical.
