How to pick within the category #

If you want a tip that moves rather than only vibrates: the LELO Loki Wave 2. Its WaveMotion tip surges in a finger-like motion against the prostate while the external arm vibrates, each driven by its own motor. The spec sheet is complete: body-safe silicone over ABS, 91 x 42 x 196 mm with a 100 mm insertable length, 196 g, two hours of use from a two-hour charge, a noise ceiling under 60 dB, 100% waterproof and a 2-year warranty, at 219 EUR. There is no app or remote.

If you want app control and two motors at a lower LELO price: the LELO Hugo 2. The successor to the Hugo, in stock at 179 EUR: body-safe silicone over ABS, 104 x 107 x 41 mm, 121 g, two motors, ten settings of which six need the LELO app, 100% waterproof, a noise ceiling under 60 dB and a 2-year warranty. Its documented runtime is only one hour, and no remote is listed.

If you want a remote-controlled vibrating massager from the same brand: the LELO Hugo. LELO’s established prostate device with a SenseMotion motion remote: body-safe silicone over ABS, 104 x 107 x 41 mm, 116 g, two motors, six settings, a 50 dB maximum, a 2-hour runtime, 100% waterproof, at 189 EUR. It was out of stock at the manufacturer when last checked, so buy through the retailers listed in the review.

If you want adjustability and app control for less: the We-Vibe Vector+. An adjustable prostate-and-perineum massager controlled by button, remote or the We-Vibe app at 139 EUR, the lowest price here, though We-Vibe publishes a thinner spec sheet than LELO.

If you want a worn plug rather than an aimed massager: the We-Vibe Ditto+. A vibrating silicone plug with a broad base, IPX7, 120 minutes from a 90-minute charge, a remote in the box and app control at 129 EUR; We-Vibe publishes no level count, weight or noise figure for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a prostate massager safe to use?

A flared base or an external arm that stops the device travelling inward, a body-safe non-porous material such as silicone, and plenty of water-based lubricant. The four massagers here have an external perineum arm that doubles as the retrieval handle, the Ditto+ plug has a broad base, and all five document silicone contact surfaces.

Which prostate massager is the best overall?

The LELO Loki Wave 2 at 219 EUR is the most completely documented: a WaveMotion tip that moves in a finger-like motion, two motors, a noise ceiling under 60 dB, 100% waterproof and a 2-year warranty. The LELO Hugo at 189 EUR holds the same 4.0 rating but was out of stock at the manufacturer when last checked.
