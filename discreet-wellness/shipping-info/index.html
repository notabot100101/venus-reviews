Shipping Information

Discreet Shipping - Your Privacy Protected

We understand that intimate wellness products are personal items. That's why every Venus Reviews package is shipped with complete discretion:

  • Plain Packaging: All orders arrive in simple, unmarked boxes with no branding or suggestive imagery
  • Neutral Labels: Shipping labels use generic product descriptions (e.g., "Personal Care Item" or "Wellness Product")
  • Privacy First: Your order information is never shared with third parties
