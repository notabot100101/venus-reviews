Wellness-Framed Buying FAQ

Practical questions to check before choosing a review.

What should I compare first?

Start with intended use, comfort, material claims, cleaning effort, noise, warranty, and privacy. Extra modes matter less if the basics do not match your situation.

Is this health advice?

No. Venus reviews products for comfort, materials, cleaning, discretion, and buying fit. Medical questions should go to a qualified clinician.

Why foreground discretion?

Packaging, sound level, storage, and account privacy affect whether a product is realistic to own and use. They deserve the same visibility as price and rating.
