## 🎯 Interpretation Guide

### What counts as "real growth" vs. noise?

| Signal | Likely real growth | Likely noise / bots |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Source | Organic search rising, direct stable | Sudden spike from unknown referrers |
| Pages | Product & guide pages growing | Only homepage / 404 pages spiking |
| Duration | Avg. session time holding or rising | High visits, near-zero duration |
| Geography | US traffic growing | Traffic from datacenter regions (e.g., Virginia, Frankfurt, Singapore) |
| Consistency | Growth sustained 2+ weeks | One-day spikes with no follow-through |

### Bot filtering tips
- Plausible excludes known bots by default ✅
- If using GA4, check "Reports → Tech → Bot filtering" (still in beta; use with caution)
- Cross-check GSC clicks vs. analytics visits: if analytics shows 10× more "visits" than GSC clicks, suspect bot inflation
