LELO Soraya Wave vs LELO Ina Wave 2 #

These are LELO’s two WaveMotion rabbits: dual-stimulation vibrators whose internal tip rises and falls in a finger-like motion against the G-spot while an external arm vibrates. They share the price, the 199 EUR list, the two-hour runtime, the 110 mm insertable length, the 100% waterproof statement and the two-year warranty with LELO’s ten-year quality guarantee. They differ in size, settings and what LELO chose to publish.

Both reviews are built from the manufacturers’ official specifications, not physical testing. Prices and specs were verified on the dates stated in each review; “not documented” means the manufacturer’s page does not publish the value.

What each one does better #

The Soraya Wave is the better-documented one on noise: LELO publishes a 50 dB maximum, among the quieter figures in the catalogue, where the Ina Wave 2 page publishes nothing. It is the larger device at 218 x 72 x 46 mm and 185 g, with 8 settings.

The Ina Wave 2 is the smaller and lighter one at 200 x 65 x 45 mm and 176 g, with 12 settings against 8, two synchronised motors stated outright and a published 37.09 mm diameter, a figure the Soraya page omits. Its gap is the noise figure, which LELO does not publish for it.

Which to buy #

Choose the Soraya Wave if a documented noise ceiling matters, or if you want the larger body. Choose the Ina Wave 2 if you want the smaller, lighter rabbit with more settings and a published diameter, and you can accept an unstated noise level. On everything else they are the same device in two sizes.

Both are ranked with the We-Vibe Nova 2 and the budget rabbits on the best rabbit vibrators page, and among the insertables on the best G-spot and internal vibrators page. The full LELO line is on the LELO reviews hub.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Soraya Wave and the Ina Wave 2?

Both are LELO rabbits whose internal tip rises and falls with WaveMotion while an external arm vibrates, both 100% waterproof with a two-hour runtime and a 110 mm insertable length, the Ina Wave 2 at 199 EUR and the Soraya Wave at 259 EUR list (199 EUR on promotion when checked). The Soraya Wave is larger at 218 x 72 x 46 mm and 185 g with 8 settings and a documented 50 dB maximum. The Ina Wave 2 is smaller at 200 x 65 x 45 mm and 176 g with 12 settings and two synchronised motors, but LELO publishes no noise figure for it.

Which is better value?

On list price the Ina Wave 2, at 199 EUR against the Soraya Wave's 259 EUR (199 EUR on promotion when checked); both rate 4.0. The Ina Wave 2 gives more settings in a lighter body; the Soraya Wave gives a documented noise ceiling. The choice is size and quietness documentation, not money.

Which is quieter?

Only the Soraya Wave has a published figure, a 50 dB maximum, among the quieter numbers in the catalogue. The Ina Wave 2 page publishes no noise level at all, so it cannot be compared on that point.
