How We Test

The Venus Reviews editorial method for assessing fit, privacy, materials, noise, cleaning, and support.

By Venus Editorial Team Updated July 27, 2026

Venus Reviews evaluates products through a buyer-first editorial process. We prioritize the details that are hardest to judge from a product page: body-safe materials, real-world noise, cleaning effort, privacy, travel fit, controls, charging, returns, and who a product is best suited for.

Review Criteria #

Each review is scored against practical categories:

  • Materials and safety: Body-contact materials, cleaning guidance, seams, water resistance, and product documentation.
  • Fit and ergonomics: Intended use, control placement, grip, size, pressure, and beginner accessibility.
  • Noise and discretion: Relative sound level, packaging privacy, travel storage, and household discretion.
  • Care and durability: Charging, waterproofing claims, cleaning time, storage, warranty, and support clarity.
  • Value: Price compared with build quality, features, warranty, and likely long-term use.

Editorial Standards #

We do not treat star ratings as a substitute for fit. A high-scoring product can still be a poor choice for a specific buyer, so every review includes plain-language notes on who should consider it and who should skip it.

Affiliate relationships do not determine ratings, rankings, or review conclusions. When a page includes affiliate links, that relationship is disclosed and the editorial recommendation remains independent.

Updates #

Reviews are revisited when product specifications, pricing, availability, shipping policies, or support terms change materially.
