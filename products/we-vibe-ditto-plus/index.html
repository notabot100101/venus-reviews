We-Vibe Ditto+ is a vibrating anal plug: a tapered silicone body that is inserted and stays in place, with a wide base that remains outside, controlled by a button on the toy, the remote in the box or the We-Vibe app. It is the catalogue’s first plug, sitting beside the prostate massagers from LELO and We-Vibe as the simpler, wearable end of the anal category.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on We-Vibe’s official Ditto+ product page and its published specification, feature and in-the-box tabs, checked on 12 October 2026. Where We-Vibe does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Ditto+ is the pick for someone who wants a wearable vibrating plug that a partner can control, in the room with the remote or at a distance with the app. The documentation covers safety and ownership well: body-safe silicone that is explicitly free of phthalates, BPA and latex, IPX7 waterproofing, published dimensions of 68.45 x 88.10 mm, a documented 120-minute runtime from a 90-minute charge, a splash-proof remote in the box and a 2-year warranty. The gaps are a missing count of levels and patterns, no weight, no noise figure and no base width. The caveat is the format: an anal plug is a specialist device that needs lubricant, patience and careful cleaning, and is not a first toy.

Format and Who It Is For #

A plug differs from a prostate massager: it is designed to be inserted and worn, with the vibration delivered through the body of the plug rather than aimed by a curved tip and an external arm. We-Vibe describes the Ditto+ shape as ergonomic and its product images show a tapered body ending in a broad flat base, the part that stays outside and serves for retrieval; the page does not state the base width. Control is by a button on the toy, by the bundled We-Vibe remote with intensity control, or by the We-Vibe app, switching between vibration patterns and intensity levels.

It suits people who already know they enjoy anal stimulation and want hands-free, partner-controlled vibration during sex or wear. It is also a couples toy in the We-Vibe sense: the partner holds the remote or the phone. Someone who wants targeted prostate stimulation should look at the LELO Loki Wave 2 or the We-Vibe Vector+, both ranked on the best prostate massagers page; someone new to anal play should start smaller and simpler than a vibrating plug.

Documented Specifications #

We-Vibe’s specification tab lists the Ditto+ in Satin Black and Cosmic Pink, with a USB-rechargeable lithium-ion polymer battery, a runtime of 120 minutes and a charge time of 90 minutes. The materials are body-safe silicone, free of phthalates, BPA and latex. The dimensions are given as 68.45 x 88.10 mm, two figures without labels, recorded here as published. The warranty is 2 years.

The feature tab adds IPX7 waterproofing, the splash-proof remote, the ergonomic shape and the We-Vibe app. The in-the-box tab lists the Ditto+, a USB cable for any USB port or adapter, a quick-start guide, the new We-Vibe remote with intensity control and a linen storage pouch. We-Vibe does not publish a count of intensity levels or patterns, a weight, a noise level, a base width or the remote’s range.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

The contact surface is body-safe silicone with an explicit phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free statement, which matters more for an anal toy than for any other format: the material must be non-porous and must tolerate thorough cleaning. Ditto+ is rated IPX7, so the plug can be washed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner; the remote is only splash-proof and stays out of water. Clean the plug after every use and dry it fully before storage. Use water-based lubricant generously; silicone lubricant is not the safe default with a silicone toy.

Battery and Warranty #

The documented figures are 120 minutes of use from a 90-minute charge, charged over a USB cable from any USB port or adapter. The remote’s own battery and range are not documented. The warranty is 2 years, and We-Vibe shows its 100-day pleasure guarantee site-wide; confirm the current terms before relying on either.

In the Catalogue #

Ditto+ vs. the prostate massagers #

The LELO Loki Wave 2, LELO Hugo and We-Vibe Vector+ are massagers with a curved internal tip and an external perineum arm, built for targeted prostate stimulation. Ditto+ is the plug: worn rather than aimed, simpler in shape, and the only one of the four with a bundled remote and app control at 129 EUR. LELO publishes weights, noise ceilings and full dimensions that We-Vibe does not. The whole anal category sits on the best prostate massagers page.

Ditto+ vs. the other We-Vibe wearables #

The Moxie+ and the Jive are We-Vibe’s other partner-controlled wearables, at the same 129 EUR, with the same remote-and-app control model. The choice between the three is where the toy is worn: in underwear, internally, or anally. All of them are ranked on the best couples’ toys page.

Ditto+ among the We-Vibe range #

Ditto+ is We-Vibe’s anal plug; the Vector+ is its prostate massager. The rest of the range covers couples wearables, the Rave 2 G-spot vibrator, the Wand 2 and the Melt, all on the same app. The full line-up is on the We-Vibe reviews hub.

Value Position #

At 129 EUR, Ditto+ is mid-priced for the catalogue and level with We-Vibe’s other wearables. The reason to buy it is specific: a documented, IPX7, body-safe vibrating plug with remote and app control from a brand with a 2-year warranty. The reasons to hesitate are the undocumented level count, weight, noise and base width, and a format that only makes sense for someone who already enjoys anal play.
