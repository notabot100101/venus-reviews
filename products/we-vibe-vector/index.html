We-Vibe Vector+ is the catalogue’s second prostate massager and the first from We-Vibe: a two-armed silicone device with an insertable arm for the prostate and a flexible outer arm resting against the perineum, controlled from a button on the base, a remote, or the We-Vibe app. It gives the LELO Hugo its first direct comparison in this category.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on We-Vibe’s official product page and its published specification and box-contents blocks, checked on 6 October 2026. We-Vibe’s /vector URL currently carries the Vector+ revision, and that is the product described here. Where We-Vibe does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Vector+ is the pick for someone who wants hands-free, dual prostate and perineum stimulation with the richest control options in the category: a button on the device, a remote, and an app that works in the same room or across a distance. The documented basics are solid: body-safe silicone free of phthalates and BPA, up to two hours of runtime from a 90-minute charge, and a two-year warranty, at a 139 EUR manufacturer list price that undercuts the Hugo. The caveat is the spec sheet. We-Vibe’s page does not publish a waterproof rating, a weight, a noise figure, a battery capacity or a count of vibration modes, so buyers who want those numbers before purchase will not find them on the official page.

Format and Who It Is For #

Vector+ is what We-Vibe calls an anal rabbit vibrator: a single silicone piece with two arms. The insertable arm has a tapered tip, a slim neck and a bulb-shaped head designed to sit against the prostate; the outer arm is flexible and angled back toward the body to vibrate against the perineum. We-Vibe’s instructions describe applying lubricant to the inner arm, inserting it, then rotating the device until it rests comfortably against the prostate and perineum. The vibrations are meant to reach both points at once rather than one or the other.

The three-way control is the format’s real distinction. The base button switches the device on and cycles patterns, the We-Vibe remote does the same from a distance, and the We-Vibe app adds partner control whether the other person is in the same room or on another continent. For solo use, the remote matters more than the app; for partnered or long-distance use, the app is the reason to choose this over a button-only massager.

This is an insertable, anal-use product. It is a reasonable first prostate massager because of the slim neck and tapered tip, but it is not a first toy for someone unsure about internal use at all.

Documented Specifications #

We-Vibe’s specification block lists body-safe silicone, free of phthalates and BPA and manufactured without latex. The listed dimensions are 106 mm x 106 mm x 30 mm. We-Vibe does not break that out into insertable length or diameter, and it does not publish a weight.

Power is a USB-rechargeable lithium-ion battery with up to two hours of runtime and a charge time of up to 90 minutes, over a magnetic USB charging cable that works from any USB port or standard USB power adapter. Battery capacity in mAh is not documented.

Controls are documented as the button on the base, the We-Vibe remote and the We-Vibe app, with the user switching between vibration patterns and intensity levels from any of the three. The number of patterns and levels is not documented on the product page, and neither is the remote’s range or a maximum noise level.

The page offers two colourways, Charcoal Black / Midnight Black and Royal Blue, at the same price. The itemised box contents are the Vector itself, the magnetic USB charging cable, an illustrated multilingual quick-start guide, a 2 ml sample of We-Vibe lube by pjur, and a linen storage pouch. The feature copy separately states that a remote is included, but the box-contents list does not name one; buyers who need the remote should confirm with the retailer before ordering.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

The documented material is body-safe silicone, free of phthalates and BPA and latex-free, which is non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone, and We-Vibe ships a water-based pjur sample in the box.

Waterproofing is the gap. We-Vibe’s product page does not state a waterproof or IPX rating for Vector+ anywhere in its specification block or feature copy, so this review does not claim one. Until a buyer confirms the rating in the user manual We-Vibe links from the page, cleaning should be treated as wipe-down with a toy cleaner or a damp cloth rather than full rinsing under running water. This is a notable contrast with the Hugo, which LELO presents as 100% waterproof, and with We-Vibe’s own Chorus and Melt, both documented as IPX7.

Battery and Warranty #

Vector+ charges magnetically over USB in up to 90 minutes for up to two hours of use. That charge-to-runtime ratio is better than the Hugo’s documented two-hour charge for two hours of use, though We-Vibe does not publish a standby figure to compare with LELO’s 90 days. We-Vibe documents a two-year warranty, and the shop page advertises free shipping, discreet delivery and a 100-day satisfaction guarantee; buyers should check the current terms of that guarantee on We-Vibe’s own page before relying on it.

In the Catalogue #

Vector+ vs. the LELO Hugo #

The LELO Hugo is the catalogue’s other prostate massager and the better-documented of the two: LELO publishes diameter, insertable length, weight, battery capacity, a 50 dB noise ceiling, a 100% waterproof claim and a two-plus-ten-year warranty. Vector+ answers with a lower list price, app control on top of the remote, a faster charge, and a flexible perineum arm rather than the Hugo’s fixed base. If the buyer wants a fully specified, waterproof device and does not need an app, the Hugo leads. If partner or long-distance control matters, or the budget is closer to 139 EUR than 189 EUR, Vector+ is the stronger fit, with the understanding that its published spec sheet is thinner.

Vector+ among the We-Vibe range #

Within We-Vibe, Vector+ is the only prostate product. The Chorus and Sync are the worn couples’ vibrators, the Melt is the app-controlled air-pulse stimulator, and the Tango X is the bullet. The whole line-up is on the We-Vibe reviews hub. Vector+ shares the brand’s app ecosystem with the Chorus and Melt, so a couple already using the We-Vibe app can add it without learning a second control system.

Value Position #

At a 139 EUR manufacturer list price, with no discount applied when we checked, Vector+ is the cheaper of the catalogue’s two prostate massagers by 50 EUR. It is not a value-first pick, and its official spec sheet leaves more unanswered than LELO’s. The reason to buy it is specific: dual prostate and perineum stimulation with app and remote control from a brand with a documented body-safe material and a two-year warranty. Buyers who want every number on paper, and in particular a waterproof rating, should weigh the Hugo first; buyers after something other than a prostate massager should start in the catalogue.

  • No documented weight, noise level, battery capacity or number of vibration modes
