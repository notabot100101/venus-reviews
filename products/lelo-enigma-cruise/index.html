LELO Enigma Cruise is the dual stimulator LELO’s product page introduces as its latest dual vibrator: the SenSonic sonic clitoral mouth and flexible G-spot arm of the LELO Enigma, which LELO’s copy calls its predecessor, plus patented Cruise Control technology that keeps the pulsations constant when the device is pressed hard against the body. The Enigma is the catalogue’s top-rated dual at 199 EUR; the Cruise lists for 20 EUR more.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO’s official Enigma Cruise product page and its published specification block, checked on 12 October 2026. Where LELO does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Enigma Cruise is the pick for someone who wants the Enigma’s sonic-plus-G-spot format and specifically wants Cruise Control, which LELO documents as keeping the pulsations constant during use so that extra power is released when the device is pressed hard against the body. The specification block is nearly as complete as the Enigma’s. The caveats are a 219 EUR list price, 20 EUR above the Enigma for identical documented figures; no charging time, noise figure, motor count or app control on the page; and a fit-sensitive dual format. LELO’s page does not say the Enigma Cruise replaces the Enigma.

Format and Who It Is For #

Enigma Cruise is a dual stimulator: an external mouth that uses LELO’s SenSonic technology to deliver sonic waves to the clitoris without direct contact, and an internal arm LELO describes as a fully flexible stimulator with ultra-powerful vibrations for the G-spot. The addition over the Enigma is Cruise Control, LELO’s patented technology that, in the page’s words, makes the pulsations constant during use so that extra power is unleashed when the device is pressed hard against the body.

This format suits people who already want blended internal and external stimulation, prefer sonic over vibrating external contact, and tend to press a stimulator firmly into the body, the situation Cruise Control is documented to address. It is not a first toy: a 140 mm insertable length, a 196 g body and two contact points to align make it a considered purchase. Buyers who do not press hard, or who want app control, get nothing documented over the Enigma for the extra 20 EUR.

Documented Specifications #

LELO’s specification block lists the material as body-safe silicone with ABS plastic, in Deep Rose and Black. The size is 100 x 50 x 185 mm (3.9 x 1.9 x 7.3 in), the insertable length is 140 mm (5.5 in), and the weight is 196 g (0.43 lb). The interface is three buttons and two LEDs, with automatic switch-off after 20 minutes. The frequency is given as 60 Hz / 100 Hz.

The battery is a Li-ion 850 mAh cell at 3.7 V, giving up to 2 hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby. LELO’s features tile states eight pleasure settings, recorded here as the patterns count with no separate intensity-level count. LELO does not publish a charging time, a maximum noise level, a motor count, an IP rating, a finish description, or any app or Bluetooth connectivity, so those remain not documented; the Enigma page, by contrast, does state a 2-hour charging time.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

LELO states the Enigma Cruise is 100% waterproof, suitable for the bath and shower, and describes the surface as extra-soft premium silicone that feels silky to the touch. Body-safe silicone is non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly: rinse under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner, then dry fully before storing it in the supplied pouch, paying attention to the junction between the two arms and the sonic mouth, where lubricant can collect. LELO states the product meets the ISO 3533 international safety standard and ships with a USB-charging cord, satin storage pouch, warranty registration card and instruction manual.

Battery and Warranty #

Enigma Cruise is charged over the supplied USB cord, with up to 2 hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby from the 850 mAh battery. The charging time is not stated on this page, so buyers should not assume the 2-hour figure LELO publishes for the Enigma. LELO documents a 2-year warranty from the purchase date, with a free replacement if a problem occurs in that period provided the receipt is kept, and separately states a 10-year quality guarantee once the base warranty expires.

In the Catalogue #

Enigma Cruise vs. the Enigma #

The LELO Enigma is the catalogue’s top-rated dual at a 199 EUR list price, and on documented values the two are almost identical: the same silicone and ABS build, dimensions, insertable length, weight, 850 mAh battery, 2-hour user time, 90-day standby, frequency, automatic switch-off, eight settings, waterproofing and two-tier warranty. What the Enigma Cruise adds is documented Cruise Control, for 20 EUR more; what it loses is a stated charging time. LELO’s page calls the Enigma its predecessor but does not say the Cruise replaces it, and LELO’s category feed still listed both on the day checked. The Enigma vs. Sona 2 comparison sets out the dual-versus-external split for the original.

Enigma Cruise vs. the rabbits and the other duals #

Against the catalogue’s other duals, the Enigma Cruise is the sonic option rather than a vibrating rabbit. The LELO Soraya Wave at 199 EUR is a conventional rabbit with LELO’s WaveMotion internal arm, eight settings, 185 g, a documented 50 dB noise ceiling and a 2-hour charge; it is 20 EUR cheaper and documents two figures the Cruise does not. The Womanizer Duo 2 at 209 EUR pairs air-pulse clitoral stimulation with a G-spot arm, documents 14 intensity levels, 10 patterns, an IPX7 rating and a 5-year warranty, and is 10 EUR cheaper. The Enigma Cruise’s distinctive claim is contact-free sonic stimulation plus Cruise Control; its weakest point is the thinnest noise and charging documentation of the three. The wider field is ranked on the best rabbit vibrators and best G-spot vibrators pages.

The full head-to-head with the original is Enigma vs Enigma Cruise.

Enigma Cruise among the LELO range #

Within LELO, the Enigma Cruise is the dual entry with Cruise Control. The external-only sonic devices are the LELO Sona 2 at 109 EUR and the app-connected LELO Sona 3 at 149 EUR, both with twelve settings and a documented under-60 dB noise ceiling; anyone who wants sonic stimulation without an internal arm should start there. The full LELO line-up is on the LELO reviews hub.

Value Position #

LELO’s own product page gives the Enigma Cruise a 219.00 EUR list price, in stock, with no discount applied at the time checked. That is the top of the catalogue’s dual band, above the Enigma, the Soraya Wave and the Womanizer Duo 2. The case for it is specific: the only LELO dual with documented Cruise Control, on the same documented body, battery and warranty as the Enigma. If constant intensity under pressure matters, the 20 EUR premium buys a documented feature; if not, the Enigma remains the better-value LELO dual. It sits in the premium picks band, and the full ranking is on the best-of 2026 catalogue.
