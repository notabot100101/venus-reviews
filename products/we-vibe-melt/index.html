We-Vibe Melt is the catalogue’s app-controlled Pleasure Air stimulator: a compact, curved clitoral device that uses pulsing waves and gentle suction rather than direct vibration, and that a partner can control through the We-Connect app. It sits in the contactless category alongside the Womanizer Premium 2 and LELO Sona 2, but it is the one built from the ground up for partner control.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on We-Vibe’s official Melt product page and its published specification block, checked on 6 October 2026. Where We-Vibe does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Melt is the pick for contactless clitoral stimulation with app and partner control. The specifications are strong and documented: body-safe silicone that is explicitly phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free, 100% waterproofing to IPX7, 12 Pleasure Air intensity levels, and up to two hours of use from a 90-minute charge. The We-Connect app adds remote and long-distance partner control on top of the on-device buttons. The caveats are real but modest: the warranty is two years where the Womanizer Premium 2 offers five, We-Vibe publishes no maximum noise figure, and air-pulse is a precise, concentrated sensation that does not suit everyone.

Format and Who It Is For #

Melt is a Pleasure Air device. Rather than vibrating against the clitoris, it seals a small mouth over it and pulses air, producing a suction-and-wave sensation that many people find more intense and more focused than vibration. Its compact, flexible body is designed to be held in place or tucked hands-free, and the We-Connect app lets a partner take over control from the next room or across a distance.

That makes it both a solo contactless stimulator and a genuine couples device, which is unusual in the category. If you want the air-pulse sensation and value app or partner control, it is squarely aimed at you. If you want broad, rumbly vibration or an insertable device, this is the wrong format.

Documented Specifications #

We-Vibe’s specification block lists Pleasure Air technology with 12 intensity levels. The body is body-safe silicone, explicitly free of phthalates, BPA and latex. It measures 126 x 24 x 10 mm, which is small and slim for the category, and it comes in Peach Pink and Midnight Blue.

Power is a rechargeable USB lithium-polymer battery giving up to two hours of use from a roughly 90-minute charge. Control is through on-device buttons plus the We-Connect app for remote and partner use. We-Vibe does not publish a maximum noise level, so anyone buying primarily for quiet operation is buying without a manufacturer figure to rely on.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

Melt uses body-safe silicone with an explicit phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free statement, which is a clearer materials disclosure than many devices give. It is rated 100% waterproof to IPX7, so it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and is safe for use in the shower or bath. The air-pulse mouth is the area to clean most carefully, since the opening can collect fluids; rinse it thoroughly and dry fully before storage. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone.

Battery and Warranty #

Melt is rechargeable over USB, with a roughly 90-minute charge giving up to two hours of use. We-Vibe documents a two-year warranty, plus its 100-day pleasure guarantee. That two-year term is shorter than the five years LELO-adjacent and Womanizer devices sometimes offer, so it is a point to weigh against the Womanizer Premium 2 if long warranty cover matters to you.

In the Catalogue #

Melt vs the other air-pulse and sonic devices #

The Womanizer Premium 2 is the category leader on quietness, runtime and warranty, and the LELO Sona 2 is the top sonic pick at a lower price. Melt’s distinct advantage over both is app and partner control built in. The whole contactless field is ranked on the best air-pulse & sonic stimulators page.

Melt among the We-Vibe range #

Within We-Vibe, Melt is the contactless clitoral device, where the Chorus and Sync are worn couples wearables and the Tango X is a compact bullet. The full line-up is on the We-Vibe reviews hub.

Value Position #

At a 149 EUR manufacturer list price, shown at 119 EUR when we checked, Melt sits in the mid tier of the contactless category: cheaper than the Womanizer Premium 2 at 179 EUR and dearer than the LELO Sona 2 at 109 EUR. The reason to pick it over either is specific: you want the air-pulse sensation with built-in app and partner control, from a brand with a clear body-safe materials statement. Buyers after the quietest, longest-warranty air-pulse device should look at the Womanizer; those after focused sonic at the best price, the Sona 2.
