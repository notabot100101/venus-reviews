LELO Tiani 3 is the catalogue’s wearable couples’ massager: a C-shaped silicone device worn by one partner during penetrative sex, sitting internally against the G-spot and externally against the clitoris, with a motion-sensitive remote for the other partner to control. It sits alongside the We-Vibe wearables as the richer, remote-driven alternative to the simpler Tor 3 ring.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO’s official Tiani 3 product page and its published specification block, checked on 6 October 2026. Where LELO does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Tiani 3 is the pick for couples who want a worn, remote-controlled massager rather than a simple ring. The specifications are strong and documented: FDA-approved body-safe silicone over ABS, ISO 3533 certification, a 125 Hz motor, a low 50 dB maximum noise figure, and roughly two hours of use from a two-hour charge. The standout is the SenseMotion remote, a motion-sensitive controller with a documented wireless range of up to 12 metres, so a partner can steer the intensity with a flick of the wrist from across the room. The caveats are inherent to the format: a worn device is fit-sensitive, it is strictly a partnered-use product, LELO does not document a waterproof rating for it, and at a 151.99 EUR manufacturer list price it is a specialist purchase.

Format and Who It Is For #

Tiani 3 is a worn couples’ device. One partner wears the C-shaped body during penetrative sex: the shorter arm sits internally toward the G-spot while the flatter external arm rests against the clitoris, so vibration reaches both partners at once. This is the same wearable category as the We-Vibe Chorus and We-Vibe Sync, rather than the simpler base-worn ring format of the Tor 3.

That design makes it squarely a partnered-use product, and a fit-sensitive one: the internal arm has to sit comfortably for the wearer while the external arm stays in contact during movement. It is not the device to buy as a first solo toy.

Documented Specifications #

LELO’s specification block lists FDA-approved body-safe silicone with ABS plastic. The body measures 78 x 44.5 x 32 mm with an insertable length of 78.2 mm, and it is light at 42 g. The motor runs at 125 Hz with a documented maximum noise level of 50 dB, and the device switches off automatically after 20 minutes.

Power is a Li-Ion 3.7 V cell rated at 70 mAh, charged in up to two hours at 5.0 V, giving up to two hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby. The main interface is a single button on the device, with the SenseMotion remote providing motion-controlled adjustment over a documented wireless range of up to 12 metres. The remote itself runs on two AAA batteries, included in the box.

The 50 dB maximum is low for the catalogue, quieter than the 60 dB LELO documents for the Tor 3 and Sona 2. The two-hour runtime is average rather than a standout.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

Tiani 3 uses FDA-approved body-safe silicone over ABS, and LELO states ISO 3533 body-safe certification. The silicone contact areas are non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone.

Unlike the Tor 3, LELO’s current Tiani 3 specification block does not state a waterproof rating. Until LELO documents one, treat the device as splash-clean only: wipe the silicone body with a damp cloth and mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner rather than submerging it, and keep the charging contacts dry. If full waterproofing matters to you, confirm the current rating with LELO before relying on it.

Battery and Warranty #

The Tiani 3 body is rechargeable, with the two-hour charge matching its two-hour runtime and a long 90-day standby that suits occasional use. The remote is not rechargeable: it takes two AAA batteries. LELO documents a two-year warranty from the purchase date; buyers who want LELO’s longer registration-based extension should confirm the current terms on the warranty page before relying on it.

In the Catalogue #

Tiani 3 vs. the We-Vibe wearables #

The We-Vibe Chorus and We-Vibe Sync are the direct rivals: worn C-shaped couples toys with app and remote control. Tiani 3’s advantage is the SenseMotion motion remote and its long 12 m range; the We-Vibe wearables lead on app ecosystem depth and, on the Chorus, a squeeze-sensitive remote. If you want the richest app control, the Chorus leads; if you want motion-driven control at range from a brand with a fully documented body-safe spec, Tiani 3 is the alternative.

Tiani 3 vs. the Tor 3 #

Within LELO, the Tor 3 is the simpler couples option: a vibrating ring worn at the base, mechanically simpler and cheaper to position, but with fewer features and no motion remote. Tiani 3 is the worn internal-external device with the richer control layer. The Tor 3 is also documented as 100% waterproof where the Tiani 3 is not, which is a point in the ring’s favour for shower use.

Tiani 3 among the LELO range #

Within LELO, Tiani 3 and Tor 3 are the two dedicated couples products. The Enigma is the dual solo device, the Sona 2 and Sila are external stimulators, and the Hugo is the prostate pick. The full line-up is on the LELO reviews hub.

Value Position #

At a 151.99 EUR manufacturer list price, shown discounted to 135.20 EUR when we checked, Tiani 3 sits in LELO’s mid tier. It is not a value-first pick, and it is not a do-everything device. The reason to buy it is specific: a couple who want a worn, motion-controlled massager with a long remote range, from a brand with a fully documented, body-safe specification. Buyers after a solo toy should start elsewhere in the catalogue.

Cons

  • LELO's current page does not document a waterproof rating, so treat it as splash-clean only
  • 151.99 EUR manufacturer list price for a single-purpose couples device
  • The remote runs on 2x AAA batteries rather than being rechargeable itself
