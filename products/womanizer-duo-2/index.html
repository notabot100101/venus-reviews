Womanizer Duo 2 is a genuine dual-stimulation rabbit toy: a Pleasure Air head delivers contactless, pulsing-air clitoral stimulation while a curved internal arm provides G-spot vibration, and the two are driven by independently controllable motors rather than one shared setting. It sits alongside the Womanizer Premium 2 in the catalogue as the brand’s answer for buyers who want internal sensation as well as Womanizer’s signature external technology.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Womanizer’s own Duo 2 product page, checked on 8 October 2026. Where the page does not document a figure clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Duo 2 documents a strong feature set for a dual-stimulation device: 14 intensity levels, 10 G-spot vibration patterns, Smart Silence that only activates the toy on skin contact, an Afterglow function for a gentler wind-down, IPX7 waterproofing, and a 5-year warranty backed by a 100-day pleasure guarantee. The gaps are real too - Womanizer does not publish battery capacity, noise level, app support or an auto-off timer for this model, and the runtime and charge-time figures are both listed as exactly 120 minutes, which is unusual and could not be checked against anything else on the page. At a 209 EUR manufacturer list price (shown at a 169 EUR flash-sale price when checked), it is a premium-tier purchase rather than a first toy.

Format and Who It Is For #

Duo 2 is a rabbit-style toy in the literal sense: one head curls around to stimulate the clitoris while a second arm is inserted for G-spot contact, both running at once if wanted. The difference from a conventional rabbit vibrator is the clitoral side - instead of direct vibration, it uses Womanizer’s Pleasure Air technology, pulsing air against the clitoris without direct contact, which the brand positions as gentler on sensitive tissue while still being intense at higher settings.

It is built as a solo toy first. The two stimulation types are independently adjustable, so a buyer who wants only the internal vibration or only the external air-pulse can run either side alone, which makes it a flexible choice for someone who already knows they like Pleasure Air and wants to add G-spot stimulation without buying and tracking two separate devices.

Documented Specifications #

Womanizer’s published specification table lists the Duo 2 at 88 x 203 x 58 mm and 263 g, available in five colors (Black, Bordeaux, Blueberry, Petrol, Lilac). The stimulation head is documented as body-safe silicone, and the overall materials line reads ABS and body-safe silicone, explicitly free of phthalates and BPA, with no latex.

The device offers 14 intensity levels and 10 vibration patterns for the internal motor, described by Womanizer as ranging from a wave pattern through “Cha-Cha” to a constant setting. Two independently controllable motors drive the Pleasure Air head and the G-spot vibrator separately, so intensity and pattern on one side do not have to match the other. Smart Silence is documented as starting stimulation only once the toy is close to the skin and dropping to standby when it is not in use, which both quiets the device and conserves battery between uses. An Afterglow function lets a short press of the power button drop the device to a lower intensity after climax rather than switching off outright.

Runtime is documented as 120 minutes, and charge time is also documented as 120 minutes - identical figures on the same specification table. That symmetry is unusual (most devices in this catalogue charge faster than they run), and Womanizer does not publish a battery capacity in milliamp-hours to check it against, so both numbers should be treated as manufacturer claims rather than independently verified figures. Noise level in decibels and app or Bluetooth support are not documented anywhere on the page; based on the described feature set (two physical buttons, no mention of an app), Duo 2 appears to be a button-controlled device only.

The box is documented as including a USB charging cable (no AC power adaptor), two extra stimulation heads, a quick guide, safety instructions, and a cotton storage bag. Warranty coverage is documented as 5 years, and the page also displays Womanizer’s standard 100-day pleasure guarantee alongside free shipping and discreet delivery.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

Duo 2 is rated IPX7 waterproof, which Womanizer markets specifically for bath, shower and pool use, and which also makes thorough cleaning straightforward: rinse under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner, then dry fully before storage. The documented materials - body-safe silicone over the contact surfaces, ABS for the body, no phthalates, no BPA, no latex - are consistent with what Womanizer documents for the Premium 2, and the page does not use the “CleanTech” branding for this product; it simply describes the head and shaft silicone as “body-safe.”

Because the insertable arm and the external head are both silicone, water-based lubricant is the conservative choice throughout. The two included stimulation heads (for the external side) should be removed, cleaned separately, and dried fully before refitting, since a damp head left sealed against the body is the most common hygiene mistake with this toy shape.

Battery and Warranty #

The device is USB rechargeable, with Womanizer’s own table listing both the runtime and the charge time at 120 minutes. Buyers should treat that charge time as a manufacturer figure rather than a confirmed fast-charge claim, since no separate charging specification (voltage, amperage, or mAh capacity) is published to corroborate it. Warranty coverage is 5 years, matching the Premium 2, and the 100-day pleasure guarantee shown on the page gives a separate, shorter return window for buyers who are unsure whether the format suits them.

In the Catalogue #

Duo 2 vs. the Premium 2 #

The Womanizer Premium 2 is the brand’s external-only Pleasure Air flagship, with a documented 240-minute runtime against a 120-minute charge - a notably better ratio than Duo 2’s matched 120/120 figures. Premium 2 is the better choice for someone who only wants clitoral stimulation and the longest runtime in the lineup; Duo 2 is the pick for anyone who also wants G-spot stimulation from the same device, at the cost of a shorter charge-to-runtime ratio and a higher list price.

Duo 2 among the insertable and dual options #

Against the LELO Enigma, both are dual air-pulse-plus-internal devices, but the mechanisms differ: Enigma’s internal side is designed to be felt alongside its own Pleasure Air-style technology within a single sealed shape, while Duo 2 keeps the clitoral head and G-spot arm as two visibly distinct, independently controllable components. For a purely internal G-spot pick, the Satisfyer Curvy is the simpler, single-purpose alternative without the external stimulation. Value Position #

At a 209 EUR manufacturer list price - shown at a 169 EUR flash-sale price (-20%) when we checked - Duo 2 sits at the premium end of the catalogue, priced above the Premium 2’s external-only 179 EUR. The case for it is specific: a buyer who wants Womanizer’s Pleasure Air technology and independently adjustable G-spot vibration in one device, and who can accept that Womanizer has not published battery, noise or app specifications for this model. Buyers who only want external stimulation should look at the Premium 2 first; buyers who are not sure dual stimulation suits them should start with a lower-cost single-purpose toy from the catalogue.

Pros

  • Genuinely dual mechanism: Pleasure Air clitoral stimulation and G-spot vibration, independently adjustable
  • Smart Silence starts stimulation only on skin contact, a real discretion advantage
  • 14 intensity levels and 10 vibration patterns, a long ladder for both stimulation types
  • IPX7 waterproof, with an explicit phthalate-free, BPA-free and latex-free materials statement
  • 5-year warranty plus a 100-day pleasure guarantee

Cons

  • No published battery capacity, noise level, or app/Bluetooth support for this model
  • Published charge time (120 min) exactly matches the published runtime (120 min), unusual and unverifiable from the page alone
  • No mains adaptor included - USB charging only
  • 209 EUR manufacturer list price puts it well above entry-level rabbit toys
