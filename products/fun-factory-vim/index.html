Fun Factory VIM is the first wand in the Venus catalogue — the format is a fixture of the category and its absence here was a real gap. VIM’s design argument is the weighted motor: two weights flank the motor to push its vibration into the low, rumbly register that wand users generally prefer over buzzy high frequencies.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This draft is based on Fun Factory’s own product page and its structured product data (funfactory.com/products/vim, checked 6 August 2026). Where a detail is not documented in that capture, this page says so.

Quick Verdict #

On documentation alone, VIM is an easy shortlist entry for anyone who wants deep external massage: body-safe silicone, fully waterproof, USB-C charging, and Fun Factory’s German-made construction, at a verified manufacturer price of 66.95 EUR — under half the price of the premium couples devices in this catalogue. The caveat is availability: it was out of stock at the manufacturer on the check date, so this page does not advertise a buyable offer.

Documented Specifications #

Verified on the manufacturer page on 6 August 2026:

  • Category: wand massager (Fun Factory’s own classification)
  • Weighted-motor design: two weights beside the motor, tuned for deep rumble over buzz
  • Body-safe silicone (“Körperfreundliches Silikon”)
  • Waterproof, IPX7 rated
  • USB-C rechargeable
  • Described by the manufacturer as very quiet for its power class
  • Price: 66.95 EUR — out of stock at time of check

What Is Not Documented Here #

The capture did not include verified figures for runtime, charge time, noise in dB, weight, or dimensions. Fun Factory publishes these; they should be confirmed before publication.

Who It Is For #

  • Shoppers who specifically want the wand format
  • Users who prefer deep rumble to high-frequency buzz
  • Value-focused buyers: it undercuts every premium product in the catalogue

Who Should Look Elsewhere #

Anyone who needs a compact or travel-friendly format — a wand is neither — and buyers who need it now, until stock returns.
