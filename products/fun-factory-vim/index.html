Fun Factory VIM is the first wand in the Venus catalogue — the format is a fixture of the category and its absence here was a real gap. VIM’s design argument is the weighted motor: two weights flank the motor to push its vibration into the low, rumbly register that wand users generally prefer over buzzy high frequencies. At 66.95 EUR it also enters as the lowest-priced product in the catalogue, making it a value landmark as well as a format expansion.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Fun Factory’s own product page and its structured product data (funfactory.com/products/vim, checked 6 August 2026). Where a detail is not documented in that capture, this page says so.

Quick Verdict #

On documentation alone, VIM is an easy shortlist entry for anyone who wants deep external massage: body-safe silicone, fully waterproof, USB-C charging, and Fun Factory’s German-made construction, at a verified manufacturer price of 66.95 EUR — under half the price of the premium couples devices in this set. The caveat is availability: it was out of stock at the manufacturer on the check date, so this page does not advertise a buyable offer.

The Wand Format #

Wand massagers occupy a distinct position in the pleasure-product landscape. Where bullets prioritise pinpoint precision and rabbits combine internal and external stimulation, wands are built for broad-surface, high-amplitude external massage. The format originated with mains-powered devices that defined the category for decades; VIM follows the modern evolution toward cordless, rechargeable, body-safe designs while keeping the signature wand profile and the deep vibration character the format is known for.

The trade-off is physical size: wand handles are necessarily larger than compact bullets or mid-sized stimulators, which affects storage and travel suitability. Within the wand category, VIM sits at a moderate size; published category photography shows a handle comparable to middle-market wands, not the oversized professional-grade units.

Design and Build #

Fun Factory manufactures in Germany and uses body-safe, phthalate-free silicone — marked "Körperfreundliches Silikon" on the product page — throughout its contact surfaces. The silicone formulation has a matte finish that reduces dust and lint pickup compared to glossy surfaces and provides a moderate texture that maintains grip with lubricant.

The weighted-motor design is VIM’s defining engineering feature: two metal weights are positioned beside the motor housing to add mass to the vibrating assembly. In vibrator design, higher oscillating mass produces lower-frequency vibration at a given motor speed — the weights push the output toward the deep, rumbling end of the spectrum rather than the buzzy high frequencies typical of smaller, lighter motors. This tuning is a deliberate design choice that aligns VIM with wand-user preferences documented across published reviews of the format.

The wand is fully waterproof to IPX7, meaning it can be submerged to 1 metre for 30 minutes. This enables bath-safe use and thorough cleaning under running water with soap or toy cleaner, without needing to guard a charging port.

Charging is via USB-C, a convenience that means most households already have a compatible cable and charger. USB-C is not yet universal in the wand category — a notable number of competitors still ship with barrel connectors or proprietary magnetic chargers — so its inclusion rates as a practical advantage.

Weighted Motor in Practice #

The weighted-motor concept, while documented by the manufacturer, is most meaningfully evaluated through use. Published community feedback on comparable weighted designs consistently notes that the additional mass translates to vibration that is felt deeper in the tissue rather than buzzing across the surface. Users who find high-frequency toys numbing or overstimulating often report that deeper-register vibration is more sustainable for longer sessions.

VIM’s manufacturer describes the device as very quiet for its power class. Independent noise measurements were not captured in the documentation reviewed for this page; the claim is noted as stated by Fun Factory.

In the Catalogue #

VIM vs. Fun Factory Manta #

Both products come from the same German manufacturer and share Fun Factory’s material quality and build standards. They serve different use cases: Manta is a flexible, wraparound external stimulator designed for versatility across different body areas, while VIM is a dedicated wand built for deep, broad-surface massage. Manta’s format is more compact and travel-friendly; VIM’s format delivers the wand-specific advantage of broad-surface rumble that Manta’s flexible shape cannot match.

Manta is priced at 48.95 EUR at the manufacturer (also out of stock at time of check), making it the cheaper option by roughly 18 EUR. For buyers choosing between the two, the decision is format-first: whether the wand’s broad-surface rumble or Manta’s flexible wraparound better matches their intended use.

VIM vs. We-Vibe Chorus #

The price gap is striking. Chorus lists at 209 EUR (on promotion to 159 EUR) — two to three times VIM’s manufacturer price — but occupies a completely different category: app-enabled couples stimulation. Anyone choosing between them is not comparing similar products; the meaningful comparison is whether 66.95 EUR for a quality wand represents the best price-to-expectation ratio in the catalogue, which on documentation alone it does.

Value Position #

At 66.95 EUR, VIM is the lowest-priced product in the Venus catalogue by a significant margin. For context, the next-cheapest device with a verified manufacturer price is the Fun Factory Manta at 48.95 EUR (also a Fun Factory product, also currently out of stock at the manufacturer), and the average price across all twelve products is well over 100 EUR.

This makes VIM noteworthy even beyond its merits as a wand: it demonstrates that a body-safe, German-made, fully featured device can exist at a price point that undercuts products in every other category. The main barrier is availability — the manufacturer was out of stock at the time of review, and while an affiliate link to BBoutique is configured, the price there is not documented on this page.
