We-Vibe Match is the remote-controlled member of We-Vibe’s worn couples’ family: a C-shaped silicone device held internally against the G-spot and externally against the clitoris during sex, steered by a handheld remote rather than an app. It sits below the Chorus in the range, and We-Vibe documents it fully.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on We-Vibe’s official Match product page, checked on 10 and 11 October 2026. Its specification table is loaded by script and invisible to a plain page fetch, so the values here were read from the rendered page and then independently re-opened and confirmed. Where We-Vibe does not document something, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Match is the pick for couples who want a worn vibrator with a physical remote and no app to pair. For a 139 EUR list price the documentation is unusually complete: exact dimensions of 76.5 x 29.7 x 46.4 mm, body-safe silicone free of phthalates, BPA and latex, 11 named vibration modes, 100% waterproofing, up to two hours of runtime from a 90-minute charge, and a 2-year warranty. The caveats are real: it is remote-only with no app, so no long-distance control, We-Vibe publishes no weight or noise figure, and it was out of stock on the manufacturer store when we checked, with a back-in-stock form in place of a buy button.

Format and Who It Is For #

Match is a worn couples’ device in the same C-shape family as the Chorus and Sync: the shorter arm sits internally toward the G-spot and the flatter arm rests against the clitoris, so vibration reaches both partners during penetrative sex. We-Vibe lists it under couples, G-spot and dual stimulation. Control is through the included remote, which runs the 11 modes without a phone.

That makes it the simplest of the We-Vibe wearables to set up, and the one for couples who find app pairing a chore. It is squarely a partnered-use, fit-sensitive product: the internal arm has to sit comfortably for the wearer while the external arm keeps contact during movement. Couples who want long-distance or phone control should look at the Chorus or the Jive instead.

Documented Specifications #

We-Vibe’s specification table lists an outer body of body-safe silicone, explicitly free of phthalates and BPA and made without latex. The device measures 76.5 x 29.7 x 46.4 mm and is offered in Periwinkle. Power is a rechargeable USB lithium-polymer battery giving up to two hours of runtime from a 90-minute charge.

The 11 modes are named rather than numbered: Low, Medium, High, Pulse, Wave, Ultra, Massage, Echo, Tide, Surf and Peak, selected from the remote. We-Vibe does not publish a weight or a maximum noise level; its marketing describes the Match as quiet as a whisper, which is a claim without a decibel figure behind it.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

The body-safe silicone outer, with its explicit phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free statement, is non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly. We-Vibe rates the Match 100% waterproof, so it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath or shower. The junction between the two arms is the area to clean most carefully; rinse thoroughly and dry fully before storage. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone. The remote is not stated as waterproof and should be kept dry.

Battery and Warranty #

A two-hour runtime from a 90-minute charge is solid for a worn device, in line with the Chorus and ahead of the shorter-running wearables in the catalogue. We-Vibe documents a two-year warranty plus its 100-day pleasure guarantee; confirm the current terms before relying on them.

In the Catalogue #

Match vs. the Chorus and Sync #

The Chorus at 209 EUR is the flagship, with a Squeeze Remote, touch-sense control and the We-Connect app. Match is cheaper and fully specified, but remote-only. If you want app or long-distance control, the Chorus leads; if you want the simplest worn couples’ device at the lowest We-Vibe price, Match is the pick, stock permitting.

Match among the worn couples’ devices #

Across brands, the LELO Tiani 3 is the worn massager with a motion-sensitive remote and a 12 m range, and the LELO Tor 3 is the simpler vibrating ring. All of them are ranked on the best couples’ toys page, and the full We-Vibe line is on the We-Vibe reviews hub.

Value Position #

At 139 EUR, Match is the lowest-priced worn couples’ vibrator in the We-Vibe range and one of the best documented in the catalogue, which is why it rates 4.0 despite lacking an app. The reasons to hesitate are stock and control: it was unbuyable on the manufacturer store when checked, so it must come through a retailer, and remote-only control rules out long-distance play. Couples who want the app ecosystem should spend up to the Chorus; for the whole catalogue, see the best-of 2026 ranking.

  • 11 named vibration modes on a remote, no phone or app pairing required
