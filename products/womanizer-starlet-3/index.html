Womanizer Starlet 3 is the entry point to the brand’s Pleasure Air range: the same contactless, air-pulse sensation as the Premium 2 and Next, in a smaller, simpler, cheaper body. It is the device Womanizer positions for people who want to find out whether air-pulse works for them without paying flagship money.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Womanizer’s official Starlet 3 product page and its published specification table, checked on 10 October 2026. Where Womanizer does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Starlet 3 is the pick for trying Pleasure Air at the lowest price Womanizer charges. For 69 EUR it is unusually well documented: 6 intensity levels, IPX7 waterproofing, a body-safe silicone stimulation head, exact dimensions of 117 x 46 x 47 mm, a weight of 80 g, and the same 5-year warranty as the flagships. The trade-offs are real, though. Its 30-minute runtime is the shortest of any device we have reviewed, it offers 6 levels where the Premium 2 and Next offer 14, the body is ABS plastic rather than silicone, and Smart Silence and Autopilot are not listed for it.

Format and Who It Is For #

Starlet 3 is an external-only air-pulse stimulator. Its mouth seals over the clitoris and pulses air rather than vibrating against it, the sensation Womanizer built the category on, with a 6-step intensity range controlled on the device. It is small and light, which suits travel and a first purchase, and its five colour options are the widest choice in the Womanizer line we have reviewed.

It suits people new to air-pulse who want the genuine Womanizer mechanism at a budget price, and people who value a compact device over a long session. Anyone who wants longer runtime, finer intensity control or Smart Silence should look at the Premium 2 or Next; anyone wanting internal stimulation should look at the Duo 2.

Documented Specifications #

Womanizer’s specification table lists a body of ABS plastic, free of phthalates, BPA and latex, with a stimulation head of body-safe silicone. The device measures 117 x 46 x 47 mm and weighs 80 g, and it comes in Turquoise, Indigo, Violet, Pink and Grey.

Power is rechargeable, with a documented runtime of 30 minutes from a 30-minute charge. It offers 6 intensity levels and is rated IPX7. Womanizer does not publish a noise level or a battery capacity for the Starlet 3, and the Smart Silence and Autopilot features of the pricier models are not listed for it.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

The stimulation head, the part that contacts the body, is body-safe silicone, while the body is ABS plastic with an explicit phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free statement. The IPX7 rating means it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath or shower. The mouth and the silicone head are the areas to clean most carefully, since fluids collect in an air-pulse opening; rinse thoroughly and dry fully before storage. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone.

Battery and Warranty #

The battery is the Starlet 3’s weak point: 30 minutes of runtime from a 30-minute charge is the shortest runtime in the catalogue, against 120 minutes on the Premium 2 and 240 on the Next. Charging is quick, which softens it for short sessions, but it is a real limitation for longer use. The warranty is the strong point: Womanizer documents 5 years plus its 100-day pleasure guarantee, the same cover as the flagships, which is rare at 69 EUR.

In the Catalogue #

Starlet 3 vs. the other Womanizers #

The Premium 2 at 179 EUR and Next at 209 EUR add Smart Silence, Autopilot, 14 levels and four to eight times the runtime. Starlet 3 is the same mechanism stripped to the essentials. If you already know you respond to air-pulse and want the full experience, the Premium 2 is the step up; if you want to find out cheaply, the Starlet 3 is the entry. The full line is on the Womanizer reviews hub.

Starlet 3 vs. the budget air-pulse devices #

Against the Satisfyer Pro 2 Generation 3 at a 79.95 EUR list price and the Satisfyer Curvy Trinity 3 at 59.95 EUR, Starlet 3 is the better-documented device on battery: Womanizer publishes a runtime where Satisfyer does not, and the Pro 2 page states dimensions and weight but no runtime, though Satisfyer counters with a 15-year warranty and, on the Curvy, an insertable arm. The whole field is ranked on the best air-pulse & sonic stimulators page, and Starlet 3 sits with the rest of the sub-90 EUR devices on the budget picks page.

Value Position #

At 69 EUR, Starlet 3 is the cheapest Womanizer and one of the cheapest air-pulse devices in the catalogue, with a spec sheet and a warranty that outclass its price. The reason to buy it is specific: you want the genuine Pleasure Air sensation from the brand that defined it, at the lowest cost, and you can live with 30-minute sessions. Buyers who want runtime and refinement should spend up to the Premium 2; for the whole catalogue, see the best-of 2026 ranking.
