Fun Factory Stronic Real is the second pulsator in the Venus catalogue and the realistic-shaped member of Fun Factory’s Stronic line. Like the Stronic G, it does not vibrate: an internal motor drives the body back and forth, so it thrusts on its own without a hand moving it. Where the Stronic G has a curved, G-spot-specific tip, the Stronic Real has a rounded head and a straighter, slimmer shaft, and Fun Factory lists it as a “Realistischer Pulsator”, a realistic pulsator.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Fun Factory’s official Stronic Real product page, its “Material & Mehr” specification block and its published variant data, checked on 12 October 2026. Where Fun Factory does not document something, this page says “not documented” rather than guessing.

Quick Verdict #

Stronic Real is the pick for someone who wants hands-free thrusting in a realistic shape rather than a sculpted G-spot curve. Fun Factory publishes a measured 202 x 40 x 35 mm body at 275 g, IPX7 waterproofing, USB-C charging, ten thrust programmes described as seven strengths and three rhythms, and a runtime of up to two hours per charge, at 67.95 EUR with both colours in stock when checked. The limits are the ones that apply to any pulsator: no vibration mode, a specific and polarising sensation, and no charge time, warranty term or noise level on the page.

Format and Who It Is For #

A pulsator works by motion, not vibration. Fun Factory’s question-and-answer copy is explicit: “Unser realistischer Pulsator vibriert nicht”, our realistic pulsator does not vibrate. A motor inside the shaft produces rhythmic, repeated thrusts, and the headline sums it up as “stößt von selbst”, it thrusts by itself. That makes it hands-free, and Fun Factory’s play ideas lean on that: propped on a pillow, or combined with oral stimulation. The page also suggests adding a lay-on vibrator for clitoral vibration, a tacit admission that the Stronic Real provides none of its own.

The shape is the point of difference within the line: a rounded, defined head, a shaft that tapers only slightly towards a three-button handle, and a 40 mm width that is noticeably slimmer than the 49 mm Stronic G. Fun Factory still claims it for G-spot stimulation (“Für die Stimulation deines G-Punkts”). It suits people whose internal response is to pressure and movement rather than buzz, and people who prefer a realistic silhouette over an anatomical curve. Anyone who wants a first all-rounder, a compact device, or who already prefers vibration internally should look elsewhere.

Documented Specifications #

Fun Factory’s “Größe, Material & Mehr” block, feature list and question-and-answer copy document the following, verified on 12 October 2026:

  • Weight: 275 g
  • Length: 202 mm
  • Width: 40 mm
  • Height: 35 mm
  • Material: soft, body-friendly silicone (“Weiches, körperfreundliches Silikon”, from the feature list; the spec block has no material row)
  • Colours: Petol (named “bottlegreen” in Fun Factory’s image files) and Black, article numbers FF000993BT01 and FF000993BK01
  • Waterproof: yes, IPX7 (“Wasserdicht (IPX7) verarbeitet”)
  • Battery: one rechargeable battery included (“Akku x 1 enthalten”), charged over USB-C
  • Thrust programmes: 10 (“10 Stoßprogramme”); the question-and-answer copy describes the controls as “7 Stärken und 3 Rhythmen”, seven strengths and three rhythms, which add up to the same ten
  • Runtime: up to two hours on one charge (“bis zu zwei Stunden am Stück”)

Not documented on the product page: charge time, warranty term, noise level, insertable length, thrust stroke length, a button lock, and the material of the handle section. The Stronic G page lists a button lock and names ABS plastic alongside silicone; the Stronic Real page does neither, so this review does not assume them.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

The contact surface is silicone, which Fun Factory describes as soft and body-friendly. Silicone is non-porous and appropriate for internal use when cleaned properly; water-based lubricant is the conservative choice, since silicone-based lubricants can damage silicone toys. The page does not name the handle material. The IPX7 rating means the device is rated for temporary submersion, so it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a toy cleaner and used in the bath. Dry it completely before storage.

Battery and Warranty #

Here the Stronic Real page is better than most of Fun Factory’s, which usually document USB-C charging and nothing else: it states that one charge gives up to two hours of continuous use, once in the headline copy and again in the question-and-answer section. Charge time is still not given, and neither is a warranty term, so buyers who want certainty on those should check the manual linked from the page or Fun Factory’s current terms before ordering.

In the Catalogue #

Stronic Real vs. the Stronic G #

Both are thrusting pulsators with ten programmes, IPX7 waterproofing and USB-C charging, priced within two euros of each other at 67.95 EUR and 69.95 EUR for the Stronic G. The differences are shape, size and documentation. The Stronic G is curved and tipped for the G-spot, 209 x 49 x 37 mm and 295 g; the Stronic Real is straighter with a realistic head, 202 x 40 x 35 mm and 275 g. The Stronic G page documents a button lock and ABS as a second material; the Stronic Real page documents a runtime and breaks its programmes into strengths and rhythms. Choose by shape first.

Stronic Real vs. the vibrating internal picks #

Every other internal toy on Venus vibrates. The LELO Mona at 139 EUR and LELO Gigi 2 at 109 EUR are far lighter, with stated runtimes and charge times, and the Mona has a two-year warranty on record; the Fun Factory Miss Bi adds a clitoral arm for 53.95 EUR. None of them thrusts. The Stronic Real is a different mechanism rather than a better or worse version of those; the best G-spot vibrators page lays out that choice.

Stronic Real among the Fun Factory range #

Within the brand, the Stronic Real and Stronic G are the internal pulsators; the Volta at 51.95 EUR, the VIM wand at 66.95 EUR and the Manta at 48.95 EUR are external vibrators, and the Miss Bi is the rabbit. Fun Factory’s own page suggests pairing a Stronic with an external vibrator, which makes the Volta or Manta a natural partner. The full line-up is on the Fun Factory reviews hub.

Value Position #

At 67.95 EUR from the manufacturer, with no discount and both colours in stock when checked, the Stronic Real is the cheaper of the two pulsators and sits in the sub-90 EUR band on the budget picks page. The price buys hands-free thrusting in a realistic shape with size, weight, waterproofing, programme count and runtime all documented; it does not buy a vibrator, a charge time or a warranty term. It rates 3.5, the same as the Stronic G, with the same caveat that thrusting is a niche sensation rather than a general upgrade. Buyers unsure whether they want motion or vibration should start with the catalogue overview.

Pros

  • Thrusts back and forth instead of vibrating, in a realistic shape that is 9 mm slimmer than the Stronic G
  • Ten documented thrust programmes, described as seven strengths and three rhythms, all hands-free
  • Runtime of up to two hours per charge is actually stated on the page, which the Stronic G page does not do
  • Documented 202 x 40 x 35 mm, 275 g, IPX7 waterproofing and USB-C, with both colours in stock at 67.95 EUR

Cons

  • No vibration mode at all; Fun Factory itself says the pulsator does not vibrate, and thrusting is polarising
  • Charge time, warranty term and noise level are not documented on the product page
  • The spec block has no material row: silicone is stated only in the feature copy, and the handle material is not named
  • Internal-only format with no clitoral component, and a 202 mm, 275 g body is not a compact device
