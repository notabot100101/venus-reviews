We-Vibe Wand 2 is a cordless wand vibrator: a large external massager with a flexible, ridged stimulation head on a compact handle, which We-Vibe calls its most powerful vibrator. Wands are the broad-contact end of the external category, built for deep vibration across a wide area rather than a pinpoint, and the Wand 2 brings the features We-Vibe uses across its range to that format: app control, Smart Silence and a remote control in the box.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on We-Vibe’s official Wand 2 product page and its published specification, feature and in-the-box tabs, checked on 12 October 2026. Where We-Vibe does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Wand 2 is the pick for someone who wants broad, deep external vibration and the convenience of a cordless, app-connected device. The documentation is solid on the points that matter for safety and ownership: body-safe silicone that is explicitly free of phthalates, BPA and latex, IPX7 waterproofing, a lithium-ion polymer battery with a documented runtime of up to 120 minutes from a charge of up to 150 minutes, a 2-year warranty and a 100-day pleasure guarantee. It offers more than ten intensity levels, Smart Silence that only starts the motor on skin contact, and both a bundled remote and the We-Vibe app. The gaps are physical: We-Vibe publishes no dimensions, weight or noise figure, so the page cannot tell you how big or how loud it is. The format caveat is inherent: a wand is a large, broad-contact device, not a discreet one.

Format and Who It Is For #

A wand vibrator puts the motor in a rounded head on the end of a handle, so the vibration spreads over a wide contact area and reaches deeper than a small bullet. Classic wands plug into the mains; the Wand 2 is cordless and rechargeable, which removes the cable at the cost of a finite runtime. We-Vibe describes the head as flexible, with curves and ridges intended to suit different areas, and the handle as ergonomic. Control is by plus and minus buttons for intensity and a We-Vibe button to cycle modes, with the remote control in the box and the free We-Vibe app adding partner control over any distance.

It suits people who prefer broad, rumbling stimulation over pinpoint contact, who find small vibrators too focused, or who want a handle long enough to reach comfortably. It is external-only and has no insertable part. It is not the device for someone who wants discretion, pocketability or precise placement; the We-Vibe Tango X covers that end of the range, and the We-Vibe Melt covers air-pulse.

Documented Specifications #

We-Vibe’s specification tab lists the Wand 2 as a body massager in the cordless wand format, app-enabled, in purple. The battery is a USB-rechargeable lithium-ion polymer cell with a runtime of up to 120 minutes and a charge time of up to 150 minutes. The materials are body-safe silicone, free of phthalates and BPA and made without latex, and the warranty is 2 years.

The feature tab adds more than ten intensity levels, We-Vibe app control, IPX7 waterproofing, a “whisper-quiet” description without a decibel figure, and Smart Silence, which can be switched on or off. The in-the-box tab lists the Wand 2 itself, a remote control, a USB cable for any USB port or adapter, and a quick-start guide. We-Vibe does not publish dimensions, a weight, a noise level in decibels, a battery capacity, or an exact count of levels or modes for the Wand 2.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

The contact surface is body-safe silicone with an explicit phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free statement, which is a clear materials disclosure. Wand 2 is rated IPX7, so it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath or shower within the limits of that rating. The ridged head has more surface than a smooth one, so rinse along the ridges and the neck where the head meets the handle, and dry fully before storage. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with a silicone toy.

Battery and Warranty #

The documented figures are up to 120 minutes of use from a charge of up to 150 minutes, which is a slower charge-to-use ratio than most devices in the catalogue; a wand motor draws more power than a bullet, so that is expected rather than a flaw. Smart Silence helps here by keeping the motor off until the head touches skin, which We-Vibe says conserves battery as well as reducing noise. The warranty is 2 years, and We-Vibe adds its 100-day pleasure guarantee; confirm the current terms before relying on either.

In the Catalogue #

Wand 2 vs. the Fun Factory VIM #

The Fun Factory VIM is the catalogue’s other wand, at 66.95 EUR against the Wand 2’s 159 EUR. Both pages list silicone and an IPX7 rating, though Fun Factory’s own care note calls the VIM water-resistant and not for the bath or shower. The Wand 2 adds what the VIM review does not list: a documented runtime and charge time, a stated 2-year warranty, Smart Silence, a bundled remote and app control. If the budget is the deciding factor, the VIM is the way into the wand format; if cordless convenience with remote and app control matters, the Wand 2 is the better-documented choice.

Wand 2 vs. the LELO Smart Wand 2 Medium #

The LELO Smart Wand 2 Medium is the catalogue’s third wand, at 169 EUR. On documented values, LELO publishes what We-Vibe does not: 210 x 85 x 45 mm, 155 g and 10 settings, with a 100% waterproof statement and a two-hour runtime from a two-hour charge. The Wand 2 answers with app control, a bundled remote and Smart Silence, which LELO does not list. All three wands are compared on the best wand vibrators page, and the two premium wands head-to-head in Smart Wand 2 Medium vs We-Vibe Wand 2.

Wand 2 vs. the small external vibrators #

Against the Tango X, the Wand 2 is the opposite answer to the same question: broad, deep contact from a large head rather than pinpoint intensity from a bullet. Against the Melt, it is direct vibration rather than contactless air-pulse. Someone who has found small vibrators too focused is the natural Wand 2 buyer; someone who wants a travel toy is not.

Wand 2 among the We-Vibe range #

Within We-Vibe, the Wand 2 is the solo external flagship. The Chorus and Match are the worn couples toys, the Nova 2 is the rabbit, the Vector+ is the prostate massager and the Jive is the wearable egg. All of them share the We-Vibe app, so a Wand 2 owner who adds another We-Vibe device controls both from the same place. The full line-up is on the We-Vibe reviews hub, and the Wand 2 sits among the top-tier devices on the premium picks page.

Value Position #

At 159 EUR, the Wand 2 is a premium pick, priced level with the LELO Tor 3 and above most of the external devices in the catalogue. The reason to buy it is specific: a cordless wand with a fully documented body-safe material, IPX7, Smart Silence, a remote and app control, from a brand with a 2-year warranty. The reasons to hesitate are the undocumented size and noise and the format itself. Buyers who want the wand format for less should weigh the Fun Factory VIM; for the whole catalogue, see the best-of 2026 ranking.

Pros

  • Body-safe silicone that is explicitly phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free, rated IPX7
  • Cordless with a documented 120-minute runtime, plus Smart Silence so it only runs on skin contact
  • Ships with a remote control and works with the We-Vibe app for partner or long-distance control
  • More than ten intensity levels on a flexible, ridged stimulation head, with a 2-year warranty

Cons

  • We-Vibe publishes no dimensions, weight or noise figure, so size and loudness cannot be judged from the page
  • A 150-minute charge for a 120-minute runtime is a slower ratio than most devices in the catalogue
  • A wand is a large, broad-contact format, not a discreet or travel toy
  • 159 EUR list price with no exact count of levels or modes documented
