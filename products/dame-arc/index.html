Dame Arc is a curved G-spot and clitoral vibrator from Dame, the US brand behind the catalogue’s Eva II. Where the Eva is a hands-free external stimulator, the Arc is an insertable, ergonomically curved vibrator designed to reach the G-spot internally and to work against the clitoris externally, with an angled handle meant to make the reach comfortable.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Dame’s official Arc product page and its published specification block, checked on 8 October 2026. Where Dame does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Arc is the pick for someone who wants a well-documented, ergonomic G-spot vibrator from a brand with a strong body-safe reputation. The specifications are good for the category: medical-grade, 100% body-safe silicone, 5 intensity levels and 5 pattern modes, USB-C charging, up to 1.5 hours of runtime at the highest setting from a two-hour charge, and a 3-year warranty, longer than the two-year terms common elsewhere. The main caveats are commercial rather than technical: Dame prices in US dollars (119.00 USD) rather than euros, so European buyers face conversion and possible import, and the page omits a weight, a noise figure and a specific waterproof rating.

Format and Who It Is For #

Arc is an insertable vibrator with a pronounced, ergonomic curve. The bulbous tip is angled to press against the front vaginal wall for G-spot stimulation, while the curved handle keeps the user’s wrist in a natural position, which is the problem the shape is built to solve, long or straight G-spot toys can be awkward to hold at the right angle. Dame also positions it as effective externally, so the same tip can be used against the clitoris.

It is a targeted device rather than a first all-rounder. People who already know they respond to internal G-spot stimulation, or who want one toy that covers both internal and external use, are the audience. Anyone after broad external-only stimulation or a contactless air-pulse sensation should look elsewhere in the catalogue.

Documented Specifications #

Dame’s specification block lists medical-grade silicone described as 100% body-safe. The device is 7.5 inches (about 190 mm) long overall, with a bulb 1.4 inches (about 36 mm) wide. Controls are split across two buttons, one cycling 5 intensity levels and one cycling 5 pattern modes, which Dame presents as a deliberately simple, independent layout.

Power is a USB-C rechargeable battery giving up to 1.5 hours of runtime at the highest setting from a 2-hour charge. Dame does not publish a weight, a battery capacity, or a maximum noise level, so buyers who want those figures will not find them on the official page.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

Arc uses medical-grade silicone that Dame states is 100% body-safe, which is non-porous and appropriate for internal use when cleaned correctly. Dame describes the device as waterproof but does not publish a specific IP rating, so the conservative reading is that it can be cleaned under running water but should not be assumed fully submersible until Dame documents the rating. Clean with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and dry fully, taking care around the USB-C port, and use water-based lubricant as the default with silicone. Dame notes recyclable packaging, consistent with the brand’s positioning.

Battery and Warranty #

Arc charges over USB-C, which is a practical advantage, any modern phone cable works, and Dame advises a 5V wall adapter. Runtime is up to 1.5 hours at the highest setting, which is modest but normal for a powerful single-motor insertable, and the charge time is two hours. The standout is the warranty: Dame documents a 3-year warranty, longer than most of the catalogue, which is a genuine point of confidence for a 119 USD device.

In the Catalogue #

Arc vs the other G-spot vibrators #

Within the insertable category, the LELO Gigi 2 and LELO Mona are the dedicated G-spot and firm-curve picks, both 100% waterproof with fuller published specs, and the We-Vibe Nova 2 is the app-controlled rabbit. Arc’s advantages are its ergonomic handle and a clean 5-plus-5 control layout; its disadvantages are USD pricing and a vaguer waterproof claim. All are ranked on the best G-spot & internal vibrators page.

Arc vs the Dame Eva II #

Within Dame, the Eva II is the hands-free external stimulator that tucks under the labia, while Arc is the insertable G-spot device. They cover opposite needs, external hands-free versus internal targeted, so a buyer choosing between them is really choosing which kind of stimulation they want rather than comparing like for like.

Value Position #

At 119 USD, Arc sits in the mid tier for a G-spot vibrator, with the important caveat that the price is in dollars, not euros, so European buyers should budget for conversion and check whether a local retailer stocks it before ordering from Dame directly. The reason to choose it is specific: an ergonomic, well-documented, medical-grade G-spot vibrator with a long warranty from a reputable brand. Buyers who want a fully specified, euro-priced, waterproof-rated insertable may prefer the LELO Gigi 2; those who want this exact ergonomic curve have a strong option here.
