We-Vibe Jive is the catalogue’s wearable egg vibrator: a small, flexible internal-external toy you insert and wear, controlled hands-free by a bundled remote or the We-Vibe app rather than by on-body buttons alone. It is built specifically for discreet use, including in public, with a partner holding the remote or driving the app from across the room or across a distance.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on We-Vibe’s official Jive product page and its published specification block, checked on 10 October 2026. We-Vibe’s own /jive link currently redirects to the Jive 2 generation, which is the version sold today and the one this review documents. Where We-Vibe does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Jive is the pick for wearable, remote-and-app-controlled play that stays discreet outside the bedroom. The core specifications are documented and solid: body-safe silicone that is explicitly phthalate- and BPA-free and made without latex, 100% waterproofing to IPX7, and up to four hours of runtime from a 90-minute charge, a longer window than most of the catalogue offers. A splashproof remote control is included in the box at no extra cost, and the We-Vibe app layers on partner and long-distance control. The caveats are gaps rather than flaws: We-Vibe does not publish dimensions, weight or a maximum noise figure for this device, and it currently ships in one color only.

Format and Who It Is For #

Jive is a worn internal-external egg: a soft silicone body with an external arm, inserted and worn during everyday activity rather than held or operated with on-device buttons in the moment. Control is handed off entirely to the included remote or the We-Vibe smartphone app, which is the point - it is designed to be put in, then controlled by a partner, making it a public-play and partner-control toy first and a solo device second.

That makes it a strong fit for couples who want hands-free, partner-directed stimulation and are comfortable with a wearable format, including discreet use while out together. If you want a device you hold and operate yourself, or a non-wearable external stimulator, this is the wrong shape; look at a Pleasure Air device like the We-Vibe Melt instead.

Documented Specifications #

We-Vibe’s specification block for Jive lists body-safe silicone, explicitly produced free of phthalates and BPA and made without latex. It is rated waterproof to IPX7, so it tolerates the bath or shower. Control is manual (on-device), remote-controlled via the bundled remote, and app-enabled through the We-Vibe app. The device currently ships in one color, Electric Pink.

Power is a USB-rechargeable lithium-ion polymer battery delivering up to four hours of runtime from roughly a 90-minute charge - a longer runtime window than We-Vibe documents for the Melt (2 hours) or LELO documents for the Tor 3 (2 hours). We-Vibe does not publish dimensions, weight or a maximum decibel figure anywhere on the product page’s text specification table. Separately, a marketing graphic in the product’s own image gallery states “10 Intensitätsstufen und Vibrationsmuster” (10 intensity levels and vibration patterns); because that figure appears only in image form rather than in We-Vibe’s text spec table, it is noted here as a secondary, lower-confidence source rather than folded into the verified block above.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

Jive uses body-safe silicone that We-Vibe explicitly states is phthalate- and BPA-free and made without latex. It is rated IPX7 waterproof, meaning it can be rinsed clean under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used safely in the bath or shower. The bundled remote is described as splashproof rather than fully waterproof, so it should be wiped down rather than submerged. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone toys.

Battery and Warranty #

Jive recharges over USB, with a roughly 90-minute charge giving up to four hours of use - enough for extended wear without a mid-session recharge. We-Vibe documents a two-year warranty, on top of its 100-day pleasure guarantee. That two-year term is in line with the rest of the We-Vibe range but shorter than the five years some Womanizer devices carry, worth weighing if long warranty cover matters to you.

In the Catalogue #

Jive vs the other We-Vibe wearables #

The We-Vibe Chorus is the more feature-dense wearable in the range, with an adjustable fit, Squeeze Remote and Touch-sense modes layered on top of app control. Jive is the simpler, more compact alternative: a smaller egg built around one job, discreet hands-free wear with remote or app control, rather than a full app/remote ecosystem. If you want maximum customization, the Chorus leads; if you want a compact, long-runtime wearable with a free remote in the box, Jive is the more focused pick. For a non-wearable, contactless alternative in the same app-controlled family, see the We-Vibe Melt. The full line-up is on the We-Vibe reviews hub.

Jive vs a worn couples massager #

Outside the We-Vibe range, the LELO Tiani 3 is a comparable worn internal-external couples massager with its own remote. The two differ mainly in control layer and brand ecosystem rather than in basic format - both are hands-free, partner-directable wearables rather than toys you hold and run yourself.

Value Position #

At a 129 EUR manufacturer list price, with no discount active when we checked, Jive sits at the accessible end of We-Vibe’s wearable line-up and below the Chorus. The reason to choose it over a feature-heavier wearable is specific: you want a compact, long-runtime egg with a remote control included free and app control available, without paying for Squeeze Remote or Touch-sense extras you may not use. Buyers who want the richest control ecosystem should look at the Chorus; those after contactless clitoral stimulation rather than a wearable should look at the Melt.
