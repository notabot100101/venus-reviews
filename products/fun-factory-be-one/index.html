Fun Factory Be One is a finger vibrator: a small two-motor body that sits between the index and middle finger, so that every touch during foreplay or sex carries vibration. Fun Factory files it as a couples’ vibrator, and at 33.95 EUR it is the cheapest powered device in the catalogue, a different kind of entry point from the bullets and lay-on minis on the best mini and bullet vibrators page.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Fun Factory’s official Be One product page, its “Material & Mehr” specification block and its published variant data, checked on 12 October 2026. Where Fun Factory does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Be One is the pick for a couple who want vibration added to manual touch rather than a toy held against the body, at a price that makes it an easy experiment. For such a cheap device the documentation is unusually complete: 70 x 20 mm, 28 g, two motors, 4 speeds plus a random rhythm, a button lock, IPX7 waterproofing, USB-C charging with a charge-level indicator, a 60-minute runtime and a charge time of one to two hours. The gaps are material and cover: Fun Factory describes the material only as body-friendly, hypoallergenic and non-porous without naming silicone, and publishes no warranty term or noise figure. The Purple colour was sold out when checked; Black and Magenta were in stock.

Format and Who It Is For #

A finger vibrator is worn, not held: Be One slips between two fingers and vibrates against whatever those fingers touch, so the hand does the positioning and the device adds intensity. Fun Factory frames it squarely for couples, as a way to make a partner’s touch vibrate, from gentle stroking to focused pressure, and it also works solo in the same way. Control is a button on the body with a lock to stop it switching on in a bag.

It suits couples who want a foreplay accessory rather than a dedicated vibrator, people who prefer the feel of fingers to a toy’s shape, and anyone who wants the cheapest possible way to try vibration. It is not the pick for someone who wants a vibrator to hold against the body on its own, which is what the We-Vibe Tango X and the lay-on minis do, nor for internal stimulation.

Documented Specifications #

Fun Factory’s specification block lists the Be One as a couples’ vibrator, rechargeable with a charge-level indicator, with 4 speeds and 1 random rhythm, a button lock, a size of 7 x 2 cm, recorded here as 70 x 20 mm, and a weight of 28 g. The battery runtime is 60 minutes and the charge time is given as one to two hours; because that is a range, this review does not record a single charge figure. The material is described as body-friendly, hypoallergenic and non-porous, without naming a polymer. Three colours are listed, Black, Purple and Magenta, each with its own article number.

The feature copy adds two powerful motors, five programmes in total, IPX7 waterproofing and USB-C charging. The box holds the toy, a charging cable without a USB adapter, and a booklet with the manual and play ideas. Fun Factory does not publish a warranty term, a noise level, an app or a remote for the Be One.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

Fun Factory states that the material is body-friendly, hypoallergenic and non-porous, which covers the safety basics, but it does not name silicone on this page, so this review does not claim it. Be One is rated IPX7, and Fun Factory’s own care note says to rinse it with lukewarm water and mild soap, with a toy cleaner for extra hygiene. A device worn between the fingers meets both partners’ skin and fluids, so clean it after every use and dry it fully. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default.

Battery and Warranty #

Be One charges over USB-C with a charge-level indicator, which is useful on a device this small. The documented runtime is 60 minutes, short against the catalogue’s two-hour norm but enough for its foreplay role, and the charge time is given as one to two hours. Fun Factory publishes no warranty term on the product page; buyers who want certainty should check Fun Factory’s current terms before purchase.

In the Catalogue #

Be One vs. the compact externals #

The We-Vibe Tango X, LELO Lily 3 and LELO Mia 3 are held against the body; Be One is worn on the hand, so it is a different use rather than a smaller version of the same thing. It is the only finger-worn device among the compact externals and by far the cheapest; all of them sit on the best mini and bullet vibrators page.

Be One among the couples’ devices #

Fun Factory files the Be One as a couples’ toy. Against the worn rings and C-shapes, the We-Vibe Pivot 2, Verge 2, Match and LELO Tor 3, it is the hand-held rather than body-worn way to share vibration, and the cheapest by a wide margin. The couples’ devices are ranked on the best couples’ toys page.

Be One among the Fun Factory range #

Within Fun Factory, the Volta is the precision external, the VIM the wand, the Miss Bi the rabbit, the Big Boss the large G-spot vibrator and the Stronic G and Stronic Real the pulsators. Be One is the brand’s smallest and cheapest powered toy. The full line-up is on the Fun Factory reviews hub.

Value Position #

At 33.95 EUR, Be One is the cheapest powered device in the catalogue and sits at the bottom of the budget picks page on price. The reason to buy it is specific: a documented, IPX7, two-motor finger vibrator for couples’ play, at a price that carries little risk. The reasons to hesitate are the unnamed material, the missing warranty term and the short runtime. For the whole catalogue, see the best-of 2026 ranking.
