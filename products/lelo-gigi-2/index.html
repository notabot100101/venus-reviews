LELO Gigi 2 is LELO’s G-spot vibrator built around a curved, flattened tip: LELO documents the flat face as channelling vibration during G-spot massage and as flippable for clitoral use. At a 109 EUR list price and 79 g it is the lightest and cheapest of the LELO G-spot and dual devices reviewed here, below the curved LELO Mona 2 at 139 EUR and the dual-format LELO Enigma at 199 EUR, and the only one of the three with a documented 4-hour user time.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO’s official Gigi 2 product page and its published specification block, checked on 13 October 2026. Where LELO does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Gigi 2 is the pick for someone who wants a compact, single-purpose G-spot vibrator with a flat rather than rounded tip and the longest documented runtime in LELO’s reviewed range. LELO’s documentation is complete: body-safe silicone over ABS, 163 x 35 x 42 mm, a 37.8 mm diameter, a 95 mm insertable length, 79 g, a 520 mAh battery giving up to 4 hours of use from a 2-hour charge, 90 days of standby, eight settings, 100% waterproofing, and a 2-year warranty followed by a 10-year quality guarantee. The caveats are that no noise figure, button count, colour options or app control are published, that eight settings is a short list, and that it has no external arm.

Format and Who It Is For #

Gigi 2 is an insertable G-spot vibrator with a curved shaft that ends in a broad, flattened tip. LELO’s highlights copy presents the flat tip as the design argument: ideal for channelling deep and powerful vibrations during G-spot massage, and designed to be flipped over so the same face works as a clitoral massager. LELO’s usage steps start with the flattened tip on the clitoris as a warm-up, then tilt the handle so the tip applies direct pressure or thrust on the G-spot.

This format suits people who want a dedicated internal G-spot device small enough to pack and prefer a broad, flat contact face to a rounded one. The 95 mm insertable length and 37.8 mm diameter are modest, part of the compact brief rather than a flaw. It is single-purpose: no external arm, no sonic element, no app layer. Buyers who want blended stimulation should look at the Enigma or a rabbit, and buyers who need a documented noise figure have one on the Mona 2 page but not here.

Documented Specifications #

LELO’s specification block lists the material as body-safe silicone with ABS plastic. The size is 163 x 35 x 42 mm (6.4 x 1.4 x 1.7 in), the diameter is 37.8 mm (1.5 in), the insertable length is 95 mm (3.7 in), and the weight is 79 g (2.8 oz). The frequency is 100 Hz and automatic switch-off is after 20 minutes.

The battery is a Li-ion 520 mAh cell at 3.7 V, charged in 2 hours at 5.0 V / 500 mA, giving up to 4 hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby. LELO’s features tile states eight settings, recorded here as the patterns count because LELO publishes no separate intensity-level figure. The page has no interface line, so the button count is not documented; LELO also publishes no maximum noise level, colour options, finish, travel lock, IP rating or app connectivity.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

LELO states the Gigi 2 is 100% waterproof, suitable for the bath and shower, and describes the surface as extra-soft premium silicone that feels silky to the touch. Body-safe silicone is non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly: rinse under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner, then dry fully before storing it in the supplied pouch. The edge where the flat tip meets the shaft is where lubricant collects. LELO states the product meets the ISO 3533 international safety standard and ships with a USB-charging cord, satin storage pouch, warranty registration card and instruction manual. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone; LELO claims no IP rating, only the 100% waterproof statement.

Battery and Warranty #

Gigi 2 is USB-rechargeable, with a 2-hour charge giving up to 4 hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby, and it switches itself off after 20 minutes. That is double the 2 hours LELO states for the Mona 2, Enigma and Sona 2, and the longest user time among the LELO devices reviewed here. LELO documents a 2-year warranty from the purchase date, with a free replacement if a problem occurs in that period provided the receipt is kept, and separately states a 10-year quality guarantee once the base warranty expires. Buyers relying on the extended guarantee should confirm the current registration terms on LELO’s warranty page first.

In the Catalogue #

Gigi 2 vs. the Mona 2 #

The LELO Mona 2 at 139 EUR is LELO’s pronounced-curve G-spot vibrator and the Gigi 2’s direct counterpart. On documented values the Mona 2 has six modes to the Gigi 2’s eight, weighs 128 g against 79 g, states a 2-hour user time against 4 hours, and publishes a 50 dB maximum noise level where the Gigi 2 page states none; both document a 2-hour charge, 100% waterproofing and the two-tier warranty. The Gigi 2 is 30 EUR cheaper, lighter and longer-running; the Mona 2 is the choice for a firm curve and a documented noise ceiling. Both are ranked on the best G-spot vibrators page.

Gigi 2 vs. the rabbits and the duals #

For internal and external stimulation at once, the Gigi 2 is the wrong shape. The LELO Enigma at 199 EUR pairs a sonic clitoral mouth with a flexible G-spot arm and documents a 2-hour user time and 196 g. The LELO Soraya Wave and LELO Ina Wave 2, both 199 EUR, are LELO’s WaveMotion rabbits with eight and twelve settings respectively. The We-Vibe Nova 2 at 149 EUR is the app-controlled rabbit with an IPX7 rating and a 90-minute runtime. All cost at least 40 EUR more; the Gigi 2 trades their second contact point for a 79 g body, a lower price and a documented runtime none of them matches. The rabbits are ranked on the best rabbit vibrators page.

Gigi 2 among the LELO range #

Within LELO, the Gigi 2 shares its 109 EUR list price with the LELO Sona 2, the external-only sonic stimulator; the two cover opposite jobs, the Sona 2 for focused external sonic stimulation with a documented 60 dB noise ceiling, the Gigi 2 for internal G-spot work with a documented 4-hour runtime. The full LELO line-up is on the LELO reviews hub.

Value Position #

LELO’s own product page gives the Gigi 2 a 109.00 EUR list price, in stock, with no discount applied at the time checked. That is level with the Sona 2, 30 EUR below the Mona 2, 40 EUR below the Nova 2 and 90 EUR below the Enigma. The case for it is specific: the lightest and longest-running of the LELO G-spot and dual devices reviewed here, with a documented flat, flippable tip, a complete body specification and a stated 2-year warranty plus 10-year guarantee. If a noise figure, a second contact point or app control matters more than compactness and runtime, the Mona 2 and the rabbits document those. For the full ranking, see the best-of 2026 catalogue.

  • Documented 4-hour user time from a 2-hour charge, double the 2 hours LELO states for the Mona 2, Enigma and Sona 2
  • Curved, flattened tip that LELO documents as channelling vibration during G-spot massage and as flippable for clitoral use
  • 100% waterproof with eight settings, a 2-year warranty plus a further 10-year quality guarantee, and ISO 3533 compliance stated

Cons

  • No button count, colour options, intensity-level count, travel lock or app control is published
  • Eight settings is a short feature list, and the 95 mm insertable length is modest for a dedicated G-spot device
