Fun Factory Stronic G is not a vibrator. It is a pulsator: the body of the toy moves back and forth on its own, thrusting rather than buzzing. That single distinction is the whole reason it exists, and it makes Stronic G the first thrusting device in the Venus catalogue. Every other internal toy reviewed here works by vibration; this one works by motion.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Fun Factory’s official Stronic G product page and its published “Größe, Material & Mehr” specification block, checked on 8 October 2026. Where Fun Factory does not document something, this page says “not documented” rather than guessing.

Quick Verdict #

Stronic G is the pick for anyone who has tried G-spot vibrators and found that vibration is not what their G-spot responds to. Fun Factory’s own framing is blunt: the clitoris responds to vibration and friction, the G-spot responds to pressure plus repeated movement, and Stronic G is built for the second. The published specification is unusually complete for size and build: 209 x 49 x 37 mm, 295 g, silicone over ABS, IPX7 waterproof, USB-C rechargeable, 10 thrust programmes and a button lock, at a 69.95 EUR manufacturer price. The caveats are real, though. Thrusting is a niche, polarising sensation with no vibration fallback, the toy is large and heavy by catalogue standards, and runtime, charge time, noise and warranty are simply not on the product page.

Format and Who It Is For #

A pulsator is mechanically unlike anything else on this site. In a vibrator, a small motor spins an off-centre weight and the whole toy buzzes. In a Stronic, an internal mass is driven back and forth along the toy’s axis, so the shaft itself pushes forward and pulls back in a rhythm, with no hand movement needed. Fun Factory calls this the “Stoßfunktion”, the thrusting function, and the product page describes it as “stößt von selbst”, thrusting by itself.

The practical consequence is hands-free use. Fun Factory’s own play ideas lean entirely on this: lying on your front or back with a pillow holding the toy in place while it does the work, or combining it with oral or clitoral stimulation from a partner or a second toy. There is no vibration mode at all, so if what you want is a buzzing G-spot toy, this is the wrong product and the best G-spot vibrators ranking is the right starting point.

Who it suits: people whose G-spot responds to pressure and rhythmic motion rather than vibration, people who want an internal toy that works hands-free, and people who already own a good external vibrator and want something different rather than a second buzz. Who should look elsewhere: anyone wanting a first, do-everything toy, anyone who needs a compact or quiet device, and anyone for whom a 49 mm wide, 209 mm long shaft is simply more than they want.

Documented Specifications #

Fun Factory’s specification block lists the following, verified on 8 October 2026:

  • Article number: FF000990PL01
  • Weight: 295 g
  • Length: 209 mm
  • Width: 49 mm
  • Height: 37 mm
  • Material: silicone, ABS plastic
  • Colour: lilac (“flieder”)
  • Waterproof: yes, IPX7
  • Battery: one rechargeable battery (“1x Akku”)
  • Charging: USB-C (stated in the feature list as “wiederaufladbar (USB-C)”)
  • Thrust programmes: 10 (“10 Stoßprogramme”)
  • Button lock (“Tastensperre”), so it cannot switch on in a bag

The following are not documented on the product page: runtime per charge, charge time, noise level, warranty term, and whether the 10 programmes include separate intensity levels or are 10 fixed rhythms. Fun Factory may publish some of these in the downloadable manual linked from the page, but they were not in the specification block or feature copy, so this review does not claim them.

On the numbers that are documented, Stronic G is substantial. At 295 g it is heavier than most internal vibrators in the catalogue, and 209 mm of overall length with a 49 mm maximum width is a filling size. Fun Factory describes the tip as shaped specifically for the G-spot with a gentle upward curve, which matches the product image.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

The contact surface is what Fun Factory calls soft, body-friendly silicone (“weiches, körperfreundliches Silikon”), over an ABS plastic handle section that carries the controls. Silicone is non-porous and appropriate for internal use when cleaned correctly; water-based lubricant is the conservative default, as silicone-based lubricants can degrade silicone toys.

The IPX7 rating means the toy is rated for submersion to one metre for 30 minutes, so it can be cleaned under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath. That matters more on a pulsator than on a vibrator: a moving shaft has more surface to clean, and a sealed, fully submersible body makes that straightforward. Dry fully before storage and engage the button lock.

Battery and Warranty #

Stronic G runs on a single built-in rechargeable battery charged over USB-C, which means a standard phone cable works and there is no proprietary charger to lose. That is the full extent of what the product page documents. Runtime per charge and charge time are not stated, and neither is a warranty period. Fun Factory’s other reviewed products on this site, the VIM and the Volta, had the same gap, so it appears to be how the manufacturer presents its product pages rather than a problem specific to this toy. Buyers who care about session length should check the manual or ask the retailer before purchase.

In the Catalogue #

Stronic G vs. the G-spot vibrators #

Every other G-spot product on Venus works by vibration. Stronic G is the only one that thrusts, so the comparison is less about which is better and more about which sensation you respond to. If vibration has worked for you internally, a G-spot vibrator is cheaper and more compact. If it has not, Stronic G is the only product in the best G-spot vibrators set that offers a fundamentally different mechanism.

Stronic G among the Fun Factory range #

Within the brand, Stronic G is the internal counterpart to Fun Factory’s external toys. The Volta is a twin-tip external stimulator at 51.95 EUR and the VIM is a weighted-motor wand at 66.95 EUR; both are vibrators, and both pair naturally with a hands-free pulsator for combined stimulation, which is exactly the pairing Fun Factory suggests on its own page. All three, plus the Manta, are on the Fun Factory reviews hub.

Value Position #

At 69.95 EUR from the manufacturer, Stronic G is priced just above the VIM and well below the premium app-driven devices in the catalogue, and it is the only product in the set that delivers thrusting at any price. The documentation is strong where it counts for fit, with exact size, weight and materials published, and weak on battery, which is the main reason it rates 3.5 rather than higher. The decision to buy it is a decision about sensation: a thrusting internal toy is a specific thing, not a general upgrade. Buyers who want a first toy or a vibrating all-rounder should start with the catalogue overview; buyers who know their G-spot wants motion rather than buzz have, for now, exactly one option here.

Pros

  • Thrusts back and forth instead of vibrating - a genuinely different sensation aimed at the G-spot
  • Hands-free by design: 10 documented thrust programmes run without you moving the toy
  • Fully documented dimensions and weight: 209 x 49 x 37 mm, 295 g, silicone over ABS
  • IPX7 waterproof, USB-C rechargeable, with a button lock for safe storage

Cons

  • Thrusting is a polarising sensation - many people prefer vibration, and there is no vibration mode here
  • Runtime, charge time, noise level and warranty are not documented on the product page
  • At 295 g and 209 mm it is a substantial device, not a compact or discreet one
  • Internal-only format: no external or clitoral component
