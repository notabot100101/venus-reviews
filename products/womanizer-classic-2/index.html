Womanizer Classic 2 is an external-only Pleasure Air clitoral stimulator that sits in the middle of Womanizer’s range: above the entry-level Starlet 3 and below the flagship Premium 2. It uses the same contactless, air-pulse mechanism Womanizer built the category on - the device’s mouth seals over the clitoris and pulses air rather than vibrating against it - packaged here with a longer documented runtime and more intensity levels than the Starlet 3, at roughly the midpoint of the brand’s pricing.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Womanizer’s official Classic 2 product page and its published specification table, checked on 12 October 2026. Where Womanizer does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Classic 2 is the pick for someone who has already tried Pleasure Air on the Starlet 3, or wants more than the entry model offers, without paying Premium 2 or Next prices. Documented figures are solid for the price: 10 intensity levels, IPX7 waterproofing, a 180-minute runtime from a 120-minute charge, three-quarters of the 240 minutes the flagships document, a body-safe silicone stimulation head on an ABS body free of phthalates, BPA and latex, and the same 5-year warranty the whole catalogue carries. Two stimulation head sizes, S and M, are included for a better individual fit. The trade-offs are the features Womanizer reserves for its higher models: no Smart Silence, no Autopilot, no app, and no published noise level, which makes it harder to judge how discreet it is in practice.

Format and Who It Is For #

Classic 2 is an external-only air-pulse stimulator with no insertable part. Its mouth seals over the clitoris and pulses air using Womanizer’s original Pleasure Air Technology, the mechanism the brand is known for, across 10 intensity levels. Afterglow is documented: a short press of the power button drops the device to its lowest setting, intended for a gentler finish rather than an abrupt stop.

It suits people who have tried Pleasure Air on the Starlet 3 and want a longer runtime and a finer intensity range without paying flagship prices, or anyone starting directly at the mid tier rather than the cheapest entry point. Anyone who wants Smart Silence, Autopilot or the longest runtime in the catalogue should look at the Premium 2 or Next; anyone wanting internal stimulation too should look at the Duo 2.

Documented Specifications #

Womanizer’s specification table lists a body of ABS plastic, stated as free of phthalates, BPA and latex, with a stimulation head of body-safe silicone. The device measures 148 x 48 x 35 mm and weighs 117.9 g, a plausible figure for a handheld device and in line with the Starlet 3’s 80 g and the Premium 2’s 141.4 g - this review has no reason to treat it as a packaging weight. It is offered in two colours, Black and Bordeaux.

Power is rechargeable, with a documented runtime of 180 minutes from a 120-minute charge, below the 240 minutes the Premium 2 and Next document but well above the Starlet 3’s 30. The device offers 10 intensity levels and is rated IPX7. The box is listed as including two stimulation head sizes (S and M), a USB charging cable with no mains adapter, a quick guide, safety instructions and a cotton pouch.

Womanizer does not publish a noise level, a battery capacity or app support for the Classic 2, and Smart Silence and Autopilot - both listed for the Premium 2 and Next - are not listed for this model.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

The stimulation head, the part that contacts the body, is body-safe silicone, while the body is ABS plastic with an explicit phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free statement. The IPX7 rating means it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath or shower. As with other air-pulse devices, the mouth and the silicone head are the areas to clean most carefully, since fluids collect in the opening; rinse thoroughly and dry fully before storage, and dry both included head sizes separately if both are used. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone.

Battery and Warranty #

The 180-minute runtime from a 120-minute charge is a genuine strength relative to its own price tier: it is six times the Starlet 3’s 30 minutes and ahead of the Liberty 2’s 120, though short of the 240 minutes the Premium 2 and Next document. Womanizer documents a 5-year warranty plus its 100-day pleasure guarantee, the same cover as the rest of the catalogue regardless of price, which is a meaningful point of confidence at 119 EUR. Battery capacity in milliamp-hours is not published, so the runtime figure cannot be independently checked against cell size and should be treated as a manufacturer claim.

In the Catalogue #

Classic 2 vs. the other Womanizers #

The Starlet 3 at 69 EUR is the cheaper, simpler entry: 6 levels against Classic 2’s 10, and a 30-minute runtime against 180. The Premium 2 at 179 EUR and Next at 209 EUR add Smart Silence, Autopilot and a 240-minute runtime, and on the Next 3D Pleasure Air, for 60 to 90 EUR more. Classic 2 sits between them: more levels and runtime than the Starlet 3, fewer features than the Premium 2, at roughly the midpoint of the brand’s pricing. If you want the full feature set, the Premium 2 is the step up; if 119 EUR and a long runtime are enough, Classic 2 covers the middle ground. The full line is on the Womanizer reviews hub.

Classic 2 among the air-pulse and sonic field #

Against the LELO Sona 2 at 109 EUR, the difference is mechanism: Sona 2 uses sonic waves rather than air-pulse, a broader and less concentrated sensation according to published descriptions of the category. Against the Satisfyer Pro 2, Classic 2 is the better-documented device on runtime and warranty, though Satisfyer answers with a lower price. The whole field is ranked on the best air-pulse & sonic stimulators page.

Value Position #

At 119 EUR, Classic 2 is a mid-tier buy: more capable than the cheapest Womanizer, considerably less than the flagships, with a 180-minute runtime that is not far behind the flagships’. The reason to buy it is specific: you already know - or are fairly confident - that Pleasure Air works for you, and you want more levels and longer sessions than the Starlet 3 without paying for Smart Silence or Autopilot you may not need. Buyers who want those features should spend up to the Premium 2; for the whole catalogue, see the best-of 2026 ranking.
