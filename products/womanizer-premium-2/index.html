Womanizer Premium 2 is the reference product for Pleasure Air clitoral stimulation, and it earns that position through two documented features rather than raw power. Smart Silence activates the device only when it is in contact with skin, and Autopilot 2.0 varies intensity automatically instead of holding a fixed level. Both address the practical complaints people actually have about air-pulse toys - that they are loud in the hand and monotonous at a constant setting - and neither is available on the budget alternatives in the catalogue.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Womanizer’s official Premium 2 product page and its published specification table, checked on 13 October 2026. Where Womanizer does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Premium 2 is the strongest external-stimulation pick for buyers who can meet the price. The documented 240-minute runtime against a 120-minute charge is an unusually good ratio in this category, the 5-year warranty is well above the two-year norm elsewhere in the market, and Smart Silence is a genuine discretion advantage rather than a marketing line. The caveat is straightforward: it is expensive, and it does one thing. If pressure-wave stimulation does not suit your anatomy, none of the refinement helps - and the cheaper Satisfyer Pro 2 Generation 3 will answer that question for a fraction of the outlay.

Format and Who It Is For #

Premium 2 is an external-only clitoral stimulator using Womanizer’s original Pleasure Air Technology: the stimulation head seals over the clitoris without direct contact, and pulsing air delivers the sensation. There is no insertable part and no vibration motor in the conventional sense - the entire design is built around this one contactless mechanism, refined with 14 intensity levels, Autopilot 2.0 and Smart Silence.

It suits buyers who already know Pleasure Air works for them and want the most refined, quietest version of it, or anyone who specifically wants automatic intensity variation without having to manage it by hand. It is not the right choice for anyone who has not yet established that air-pulse stimulation suits them - the Satisfyer Pro 2 Generation 3 or the Womanizer Starlet 3 are the lower-cost ways to find that out first - and it offers nothing for anyone who wants internal stimulation, app control, or partner-facing features.

Documented Specifications #

Womanizer’s published specifications give the device as 155 x 50 x 35 mm and 141.4 g. The material is documented as body-safe silicone made without phthalates, BPA or latex - a more explicit exclusion list than most manufacturers in this category publish, and one of the clearer material statements in the catalogue.

Womanizer documents 14 intensity levels, a longer ladder than most air-pulse devices offer, which matters because pressure-wave stimulation can go from imperceptible to overwhelming across a small range. Autopilot 2.0 is documented as alternating between intensity levels automatically rather than following a selectable program count, so it is recorded here as a feature rather than a “patterns” figure. Smart Silence is documented as activating the device only on skin contact and returning it to standby when lifted away, which both quiets it and conserves battery. The device is documented as IPX7 waterproof. Battery figures are given as a 240-minute runtime against a 120-minute charge; Womanizer does not publish a noise level in decibels or any app feature for Premium 2.

The box is listed as containing the Premium 2, a USB charging cable with no AC adaptor included, two stimulation heads in different sizes, a quick-start guide, safety instructions, and a cotton storage pouch. It is offered in five colours: Black, Bordeaux, Grey, Blueberry and Raspberry. The absence of a mains adaptor is a small but real omission at this price point, and the two head sizes matter more than they sound - head fit is the main variable in whether an air-pulse device works for a given person.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

The explicit phthalate-free, BPA-free and latex-free statement is the strongest part of Premium 2’s material documentation. Latex exclusion in particular is worth noting for anyone with a latex sensitivity, since it is rarely stated outright on toys of this type. Body-safe silicone over the contact surfaces means non-porous where it counts and compatible with water-based lubricant.

IPX7 rating means the device can be rinsed rather than only wiped, which matters because the stimulation head is a concave cup that sits directly against the body. Both included heads should be removed and cleaned separately, inside and out, and dried fully before being refitted - storing a damp head sealed onto the body is the one hygiene mistake this design invites. Keep the charging contacts dry, and inspect the silicone heads periodically, since a split or perished rim will break the seal the device depends on. A pressure-wave head needs a good seal against the skin, so a small amount of water-based lubricant around the rim is the usual way to achieve it; avoid silicone-based lubricant unless Womanizer’s instructions specifically permit it.

Battery and Warranty #

Womanizer documents a 240-minute runtime against a 120-minute charge - an unusually good ratio, since most air-pulse devices need longer to charge relative to what they return. Womanizer does not publish a milliamp-hour battery capacity, so the runtime figure should be treated as a manufacturer claim rather than independently checked. The warranty is documented as 5 years, well above the two-year norm elsewhere in the category, plus Womanizer’s standard 100-day pleasure guarantee. Save proof of purchase and register the product, since five years is long enough that a retailer record will be hard to reconstruct later.

In the Catalogue #

Premium 2 vs. the Satisfyer Pro 2 Generation 3 #

Premium 2 is the premium external-stimulation option, and the Satisfyer Pro 2 Generation 3 is its direct budget counterpart. The comparison is clean: Premium 2 documents 14 intensity levels, Smart Silence, Autopilot, a 240-minute runtime and a 120-minute charge, at 179 EUR; the Pro 2 documents 11 pressure-wave and 12 vibration programs across two motors, and answers with a far lower price, though its own page does not publish a runtime or charge time. A full comparison is on the Womanizer Premium 2 vs. Satisfyer Pro 2 page.

Premium 2 among the Womanizer range #

Within Womanizer’s own line-up, Premium 2 sits above the entry-level Starlet 3 and the mid-range Classic 2, both of which document fewer intensity levels and no Smart Silence or Autopilot. The full Womanizer line-up is on the Womanizer reviews hub, and Premium 2 is one of the options on the premium picks page.

Value Position #

At 179 EUR, Premium 2 is one of the more expensive external-stimulation devices in the catalogue, and the reason to pay it is specific: 14 intensity levels, Smart Silence, Autopilot 2.0, a documented 240-minute runtime against a 120-minute charge, and a 5-year warranty with an explicit phthalate/BPA/latex-free materials statement. Buyers who have not yet confirmed that pressure-wave stimulation suits them should start with the Satisfyer Pro 2 Generation 3 and upgrade only if it does; for the full ranked catalogue, see the best-of 2026 list.
