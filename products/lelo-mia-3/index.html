LELO Mia 3 is the catalogue’s lipstick-format mini vibrator: a 22 x 22 x 111 mm cylinder with a sculpted tip, which LELO titles a “mini lipstick vibrator ideal for travel.” It is the current generation of the Mia, with what the page calls an updated design, documented with 8 vibration settings, an ABS-and-silicone body in a matte finish, 100% waterproofing, a transport lock and a 36 g weight, at a 99 EUR list price.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO’s official Mia 3 product page and its published specification block, checked on 12 October 2026. Where LELO does not document a value clearly, this page records it as not documented rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Mia 3 is the pick for buyers who want the smallest and lightest fully documented external vibrator in the catalogue. LELO states materials, dimensions, weight, battery, charging, user time, standby, frequency, a noise ceiling, automatic switch-off, 100% waterproofing, a transport lock and a two-tier warranty, more than either We-Vibe mini publishes. The trade-offs are a user time of only up to 1.5 hours from a 70 mAh cell, 8 settings against the Lily 3’s 10 at the same 99 EUR, no stated app or remote control, and a box-contents list that names no charging cord.

Format and Who It Is For #

Mia 3 is a stick-shaped external vibrator, a 111 mm cylinder the width of a lipstick with a tip LELO describes as “specifically molded to target the clitoris.” Unlike the flat, palm-held LELO Lily 3 or We-Vibe Touch X, it is held like a pen and used at its tip. Two buttons cycle through the 8 settings, and LELO documents the ability to lock it during transport. No app, Bluetooth or remote-control feature is mentioned anywhere on the page.

It suits people who travel with a toy, people who prefer a focused tip to broad lay-on contact, and first-time buyers who want a low-complexity external device - see the first-time buyer guide for the format trade-offs. It is not the pick for internal or dual stimulation, app control, or long sessions.

Documented Specifications #

LELO’s specification block lists ABS and body-safe silicone with a matte finish, dimensions of 22 x 22 x 111 mm, and a weight of 36 g, lighter than the Lily 3’s 45 g; the two We-Vibe minis publish no weight. The motor runs at a stated 130 Hz, against 93 Hz for the Lily 3, and the maximum noise level is given as under 60 dB, recorded here as a 60 dB ceiling.

The battery is a Li-Ion cell rated 70 mAh at 3.7 V, charged in up to 2 hours at 5.0 V and 70 mA, for a user time of up to 1.5 hours and 90 days of standby. The device switches off automatically after 20 minutes. LELO states eight settings, described as “eight different vibration patterns, varying in intensity from a teasing murmur to a satisfying pulse”; this review records that as the patterns figure and leaves intensity levels undocumented, since LELO states no separate count. The interface is two buttons. The box contents are the Mia 3, a satin storage pouch, a warranty registration card and an instruction manual; no charging cord is named, and the page does not describe how the USB charge connects.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

LELO states Mia 3 is 100% waterproof, “perfect for the bath and shower,” with an ultra-smooth matte surface. No IP rating is given, so the waterproofing claim is LELO’s wording rather than a cited standard. Cleaning is the usual routine for a waterproof silicone-surfaced toy: rinse with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner, dry fully, and store in the supplied pouch. LELO states the product meets the ISO 3533 safety standard, which it describes as covering device safety and the body-safety of materials in contact with the genitalia. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default on silicone.

Battery and Warranty #

Mia 3 is USB-rechargeable, with a charge of up to 2 hours giving up to 1.5 hours of use and 90 days of standby. That runtime is the shortest among the catalogue’s small externals that publish one - the Lily 3 and Touch X both state 2 hours - and follows from a cell sized for a 36 g device. LELO documents a 2-year warranty from the purchase date, with a free replacement if a problem occurs in that period provided the receipt is kept, and a further 10-year quality guarantee once the base warranty expires.

In the Catalogue #

Mia 3 vs. the We-Vibe Tango X #

The We-Vibe Tango X is the catalogue’s other focused-tip external: an 89 EUR bullet, 10 EUR cheaper, with 8 intensity levels across 7 patterns, IPX7 waterproofing and a 2-year warranty. We-Vibe’s page leaves runtime, charge time, weight, dimensions and noise undocumented. Mia 3 documents all of them and adds a stated transport lock; the Tango X has the lower price and the level-and-pattern split.

Mia 3 vs. the lay-on minis #

The LELO Lily 3 and We-Vibe Touch X are both 99 EUR lay-on externals, the same price as the Mia 3 but a flat body pressed against the clitoris rather than a tip. Lily 3 documents 10 settings, 45 g, 2 hours of user time and an exact 60 dB noise level; Mia 3 answers with 8 settings, 36 g, 1.5 hours and an under-60 dB ceiling, plus a transport lock the Lily 3 review does not record. Touch X documents 8 levels and 7 patterns, 102 x 43 x 34 mm, 2 hours from a 90-minute charge, IPX7 and a lock function, but no weight or noise figure. Mia 3 is the lightest and the only stick format; Lily 3 has the most settings; Touch X has the fastest documented charge.

All six compact vibrators, including the Tango X, Lily 3 and Touch X, are ranked on the best mini and bullet vibrators page.

Mia 3 among the LELO range #

Within LELO, Mia 3 is the smallest device and the brand’s travel entry, beside the Lily 3 as the two conventional-vibration minis. It does not use LELO’s sonic-wave technology: the LELO Sona 2, at 109 EUR with 12 patterns, 125 g and a 60 dB noise figure, is the step across to sonic clitoral stimulation. The full line-up is on the LELO reviews hub.

Value Position #

LELO’s product page gives Mia 3 a 99.00 EUR list price, in stock, with the dataLayer’s discount_percentage field reading “0%,” so no discount was active at the time checked. That matches the Lily 3 and Touch X and sits 10 EUR above the Tango X, above the 90 EUR budget threshold and well below the premium tier, so this review places it on neither hub. It buys the most completely documented travel-sized vibrator in the catalogue, with a stated lock and the lightest published weight, but not a long runtime or a high setting count. Buyers who want the most capable mini at 99 EUR should compare it against the Lily 3 on the best vibrators 2026 ranking.
