Fun Factory Miss Bi is a rabbit vibrator: an insertable shaft aimed at the G-spot with a flexible external arm that vibrates against the clitoris at the same time. It is the German brand’s dual-stimulation entry, built around the same LOOP handle and USB-C charging as the Volta, and at 53.95 EUR it is the least expensive dual-stimulation device in the catalogue.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Fun Factory’s official Miss Bi product page, its “Material & Mehr” specification block and its published variant data, checked on 12 October 2026. Where Fun Factory does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Miss Bi is the pick for someone who wants internal and clitoral stimulation at once without paying flagship money. Fun Factory documents the essentials: silicone, ABS and polycarbonate construction, a measured 173 x 76 x 38 mm body at 230 g, IPX7 waterproofing, USB-C charging and 12 vibration programmes, plus a loop-shaped handle that is easier to hold at an angle than a plain shaft. The gaps are typical of Fun Factory’s product pages rather than unique to this model: no runtime, no charge time, no speed count and no stated warranty term. The format caveat is the one every rabbit carries. The distance between the shaft and the arm is fixed, so whether the arm lands where it should depends on anatomy, and the deliberately substantial girth will not suit everyone.

Format and Who It Is For #

A rabbit is a dual-stimulation vibrator. The shaft is inserted and curved toward the front vaginal wall, while the shorter external arm rests on the clitoris, so both areas are stimulated at once. Fun Factory describes the Miss Bi shaft as filling, with a substantial girth, and the flexible arm as vibrating against the external area. It is a solo-first toy that can also be used by a partner holding the handle.

Miss Bi suits people who already know they want internal and external stimulation together and prefer a handheld device with physical buttons over an app-driven one. It is a reasonable first dual toy because of the price, but a cautious first choice overall: someone unsure whether they want internal stimulation at all should start with an external device such as the Fun Factory Volta or the small vibrators on the budget picks page. Anyone who wants to adjust the arm position should look at designs with a flexible or adjustable arm, which this page does not claim for Miss Bi.

Documented Specifications #

Fun Factory’s specification block lists silicone, ABS plastic and polycarbonate as the materials, with the feature copy calling the silicone skin-friendly. The body measures 173 mm long, 76 mm wide and 38 mm high and weighs 230 g. Three colours are listed, Purple, Black and Grape, each with its own article number.

The device is rechargeable over USB-C from a single internal battery and offers 12 vibration programmes, with the feature copy also mentioning several speeds without giving a number. The page refers to a single “power motor” and does not state whether the arm has its own motor. Fun Factory does not publish a runtime, a charge time, an insertable length, a noise level or a warranty term for Miss Bi, and there is no app or remote control.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

The contact surface is silicone, which is non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly; the ABS and polycarbonate are the handle and internal structure rather than contact areas. Miss Bi is rated IPX7, so it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath or shower within the limits of that rating. A rabbit has more crevices than a plain shaft, especially where the arm meets the body, so rinse that junction carefully and dry fully before storage. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with a silicone toy.

Battery and Warranty #

Miss Bi charges over USB-C, which is a practical advantage over proprietary magnetic pins: any standard cable works. Fun Factory does not publish how long a charge lasts or how long it takes, so plan on charging before a session rather than relying on a figure. No warranty term is stated on the product page; buyers who want certainty should check Fun Factory’s current terms before purchase.

In the Catalogue #

Miss Bi vs. the other rabbits #

The We-Vibe Nova 2 is the catalogue’s app-controlled rabbit, with an arm designed to flex and stay in contact during use, and the Lovehoney Desire is the conventional firm-shaft rabbit at 99.95 EUR. Miss Bi’s case against both is price and documentation: it is the cheapest dual device here, with a measured size and weight and an IPX7 rating on the page. The LELO Soraya Wave is the premium rabbit, with a WaveMotion tip that moves rather than only vibrates and a fully documented spec sheet at 199 EUR. If app control or an adaptive arm matters, the Nova 2 is the richer choice; if a moving tip and complete documentation matter, the Soraya Wave; if the budget is the deciding factor, Miss Bi is the one to shortlist. All of them are compared on the best rabbit vibrators page.

Miss Bi vs. the dual devices that are not rabbits #

The LELO Enigma pairs sonic clitoral stimulation with a G-spot arm, and the Womanizer Duo 2 pairs air-pulse with a G-spot vibrator. Both cost several times what Miss Bi does and both document runtime and warranty where Fun Factory does not. Miss Bi is the conventional, vibration-only approach to the same goal at a fraction of the price.

Miss Bi among the Fun Factory range #

Within Fun Factory, the Volta is the precision external pick, the VIM is the wand, the Stronic G is the thrusting pulsator and the Manta is the flexible external stimulator. Miss Bi is the brand’s dual-stimulation entry and shares the LOOP handle and USB-C charging with the Volta. The full line-up is on the Fun Factory reviews hub, and the internal options across brands are ranked on the best G-spot and internal vibrators page.

Value Position #

At 53.95 EUR, Miss Bi is a value pick, and it sits with the other sub-90 EUR devices on the budget picks page. The reason to buy it is specific: dual G-spot and clitoral stimulation from a brand that documents materials, size, weight and waterproofing, at the lowest price for that format in the catalogue. The reasons to hesitate are the undocumented runtime and warranty and the fixed rabbit geometry. If this is a first purchase, the first-time buyer guide covers how to think about internal versus external stimulation, and the whole catalogue is ranked on the best-of 2026 page.

Pros

  • Documented 173 x 76 x 38 mm, 230 g, silicone construction and IPX7 waterproofing
  • 12 vibration programmes with USB-C charging and Fun Factory's LOOP handle for grip
  • Dual G-spot shaft and flexible clitoral arm at 53.95 EUR, the cheapest dual pick in the catalogue
  • Three colours, with Purple and Black in stock at the manufacturer when checked

Cons

  • Runtime, charge time, number of speeds and warranty term are not documented on the product page
  • Rabbit fit depends on anatomy: the shaft-to-arm distance is fixed, so the arm may not land where needed
  • Fun Factory advertises a substantial girth, which will not suit everyone, and publishes no insertable length
  • No app or remote, and the Grape colour was sold out when checked
