Womanizer Vibe is the brand’s first vibrator: not an air-pulse device, but an ergonomic clitoral vibrator built around what Womanizer calls UltraWave vibration, which it markets as the world’s first hum-free clitoral vibrator. It sits apart from the Pleasure Air models that define the rest of the Womanizer range, and competes instead with the catalogue’s lay-on and handheld external vibrators.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Womanizer’s official Vibe product page and its published specification table, checked on 12 October 2026. Where Womanizer does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Vibe is the pick for someone who wants direct clitoral vibration from a brand with long warranty cover, and who does not want air-pulse. The documentation is complete on the physical side: ABS and body-safe silicone, exact dimensions of 142 x 47 x 52 mm, a 143 g weight, IPX7 waterproofing, 10 intensity levels and 3 Autopilot preset sequences, a 90-minute runtime from a 90-minute charge with magnetic charging pins, and a 5-year warranty. The gaps are the hum-free claim without a decibel figure, no app or remote, and no Smart Silence. At 129 EUR it is priced between the catalogue’s small externals and its flagships.

Format and Who It Is For #

Vibe is a handheld external vibrator with an ergonomic body that Womanizer says can be used two ways: the flat side for broad contact or the tip for a focused point. The 10 intensity levels are controlled by buttons, and the 3 Autopilot modes run preset vibration sequences hands-off, each with its own rhythm. There is no insertable part.

It suits people who respond to direct vibration rather than contactless stimulation, who want a precise, published size and weight, and who value a long warranty. Anyone who wants the Pleasure Air sensation should look at the Starlet 3, Classic 2 or Premium 2 instead.

Documented Specifications #

Womanizer’s specification table lists ABS and body-safe silicone as the materials, a weight of 143 g and dimensions of 142 x 47 x 52 mm. The device is rated IPX7, offered in sage green, dusky pink and dark blue, with a 5-year warranty.

Power is rechargeable through magnetic pins on a USB cable, with no mains adapter included; the documented runtime is 90 minutes from a 90-minute charge. The feature copy lists 10 intensity levels, 3 Autopilot modes, the UltraWave vibration technology and a very quiet motor without a decibel figure. Womanizer does not publish a noise level, a battery capacity or any app support for the Vibe, and Smart Silence is not listed for it.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

The contact surface is body-safe silicone over an ABS body. Vibe is rated IPX7, so it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath or shower within the limits of that rating. Keep the magnetic charging contacts dry before plugging in, and dry the device fully before storage. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with a silicone surface.

Battery and Warranty #

The documented figures are 90 minutes of use from a 90-minute charge, an average ratio for the catalogue and below the 180 to 240 minutes Womanizer documents for its Classic 2, Premium 2 and Next. The magnetic pins make charging simple but mean the cable is proprietary. Womanizer documents a 5-year warranty plus its 100-day pleasure guarantee, among the longest cover in the catalogue; confirm the current terms before relying on it.

In the Catalogue #

Vibe vs. the lay-on and handheld externals #

The We-Vibe Touch X and LELO Lily 3 are the catalogue’s compact externals at 99 EUR, and the Dame Pom the palm-held pick in USD. Vibe is larger and heavier than all three, with a longer warranty than any of them; the Lily 3 publishes a 60 dB maximum where Vibe publishes only a hum-free claim. For broad, deep contact the wands are the next step up in size.

Vibe among the Womanizer range #

Within Womanizer, Vibe is the only vibrator; every other model we review uses Pleasure Air. The Starlet 3 is the air-pulse entry, the Classic 2 the mid-range, the Premium 2 and Next the flagships, and the Duo 2 the dual device. The full line-up is on the Womanizer reviews hub.

Value Position #

At 129 EUR, Vibe is mid-priced: above the compact externals, below the air-pulse flagships. The reason to buy it is specific: a fully documented clitoral vibrator with a 5-year warranty from a brand that otherwise sells air-pulse. The reasons to hesitate are the missing decibel figure behind the quiet claim, the absence of app control and the average runtime. For the whole catalogue, see the best-of 2026 ranking.

Pros

  • Exact dimensions and weight published: 142 x 47 x 52 mm and 143 g, in ABS and body-safe silicone
  • 10 intensity levels plus 3 Autopilot preset sequences, with an ergonomic shape for flat or tip contact
  • IPX7 waterproof, magnetic charging pins and a 90-minute runtime from a 90-minute charge
  • Womanizer's 5-year warranty and 100-day guarantee at 129 EUR, well above the catalogue's 2-year norm

Cons

  • The hum-free claim is not backed by a published decibel figure
  • No app or remote control, and no Smart Silence on this model
  • 90-minute runtime is average for the catalogue, below the brand's air-pulse flagships
  • A clitoral vibrator from a brand known for air-pulse: buyers wanting the Pleasure Air sensation should look elsewhere in the range
