Satisfyer Curvy Trinity 3 is the catalogue’s first 3-in-1: a single curved device that combines an insertable G-spot vibrator with an air-pulse clitoral stimulator, so it covers internal and external stimulation at once. It is Satisfyer’s attempt to fold the air-pulse sensation that made the brand famous into a fuller-featured, insertable body, at a budget price.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on Satisfyer’s official product page and its published specification block, checked on 8 October 2026. Where Satisfyer does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Curvy Trinity 3 is the pick for someone who wants to try both air-pulse and insertable G-spot stimulation in one budget device. The documented basics are good for the price: body-safe silicone over ABS, 100% waterproofing to IPX7, a whisper mode, USB magnetic charging, and an unusually long 15-year warranty, all at a 59.95 EUR list price. The caveats are the spec sheet and the stock. Satisfyer does not publish a runtime, a charge time, a mode count or a noise figure, and the device was out of stock on Satisfyer’s own store when we checked, so it has to be bought through a retailer.

Format and Who It Is For #

Curvy Trinity 3 is a combination device. The curved, insertable shaft delivers G-spot vibration internally, while a small air-pulse mouth near the top sits over the clitoris and stimulates it with pulsing air rather than contact. The “3-in-1” name refers to internal vibration, external air-pulse, and the two working together. It is designed to be used solo and, per Satisfyer, is aimed at women.

That combined shape is its appeal and its limitation: it does two jobs at once, but a single body that must align an internal shaft and an external air-pulse mouth is inherently more fit-sensitive than a single-purpose toy. If you want only air-pulse, a dedicated stimulator is simpler; if you want only internal vibration, a dedicated G-spot vibrator is easier to position.

Documented Specifications #

Satisfyer’s specification block lists body-safe silicone over ABS plastic, with the silicone described as super-soft and skin-friendly. The device measures 177.5 mm high, 53.5 mm long and 38.1 mm wide, and weighs 138.55 g. It is offered in grey and wine-red.

It combines air-pulse (Druckwellen) clitoral stimulation with G-spot vibration, and includes a whisper mode for quieter operation. Power is a lithium-ion battery charged over an included USB magnetic cable. Satisfyer does not publish a runtime, a charge time, a number of intensity levels or modes, or a maximum noise level, so buyers who want those numbers will not find them on the official page.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

The documented material is body-safe silicone over ABS, which is non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly. Satisfyer rates the device 100% waterproof to IPX7, so it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath or shower. The air-pulse mouth and the junction between the shaft and the external arm are the areas to clean most carefully, since fluids can collect there; rinse thoroughly and dry fully before storage. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone.

Battery and Warranty #

Curvy Trinity 3 is rechargeable over an included USB magnetic cable, though Satisfyer does not document the runtime or charge time. The standout is the warranty: Satisfyer documents a 15-year guarantee, far longer than the two-year terms common elsewhere in the catalogue, which is a genuine point in a budget device’s favour. As always, confirm the warranty’s current terms before relying on them.

In the Catalogue #

Curvy Trinity 3 vs the other Satisfyer #

The Satisfyer Pro 2 is the brand’s single-purpose air-pulse stimulator at 69.95 EUR, also with a 15-year warranty. Curvy Trinity 3 is the more versatile device for less: it adds insertable G-spot vibration to the air-pulse, where the Pro 2 does air-pulse only. Both share the same honest caveat, a thin published spec sheet. If you want only the air-pulse sensation, the Pro 2 is simpler; if you want both sensations in one, Curvy Trinity 3 is the fuller device.

Curvy Trinity 3 among the air-pulse field #

Against the premium contactless devices, the LELO Sona 2 and Womanizer Premium 2 lead on documented quietness, runtime and build, and the We-Vibe Melt adds app control. Curvy Trinity 3 does not compete on documentation; it competes on versatility and price. The whole field is ranked on the best air-pulse & sonic stimulators page, and for internal options see the best G-spot & internal vibrators.

Value Position #

At a 59.95 EUR manufacturer list price, Curvy Trinity 3 is one of the cheapest devices in the catalogue and the only budget 3-in-1. It is not a documentation-first pick, and it was out of stock at the manufacturer when we checked, so it must be bought through a retailer. The reason to choose it is specific: you want to try both air-pulse and insertable G-spot stimulation without spending much, from a brand with a documented body-safe material and a long warranty. Buyers who want a fully specified device should look at the premium picks; those on a budget can compare it with the rest of the budget picks under 90 EUR.
