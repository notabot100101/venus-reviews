We-Vibe Nova 2 is the catalogue’s app-controlled rabbit: a dual-stimulation device with an internal G-spot shaft and a flexible external arm that is designed to keep contact with the clitoris as you move, controlled on the body or through the We-Connect app. It is the better-documented alternative to the catalogue’s only other rabbit, the Lovehoney Desire.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on We-Vibe’s official Nova 2 product page and its published specification block, checked on 6 October 2026. Where We-Vibe does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Nova 2 is the pick for dual G-spot and clitoral stimulation in a rabbit format with app control. The specifications are strong and documented: body-safe silicone that is explicitly phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free, 100% waterproofing to IPX7, up to two hours of use from a 90-minute charge, and the We-Connect app for remote and long-distance control. Its standout feature is the flexible external arm, which is built to maintain clitoral contact as the device moves, the common failure point of rabbits. The caveats are the format itself: it is large at 217 mm, rabbits are fit-sensitive, the warranty is two years, and We-Vibe publishes neither a noise figure nor a vibration-mode count.

Format and Who It Is For #

Nova 2 is a rabbit: an insertable G-spot shaft paired with an external arm that sits against the clitoris for simultaneous internal and external stimulation. What distinguishes it from a basic rabbit is that the external arm is engineered to flex and follow, so it keeps contact during movement rather than slipping off, which is the usual complaint about the format. The We-Connect app adds on-phone control and lets a partner take over remotely or over distance.

It is aimed at people who specifically want both sensations at once and like a larger, filling internal shape. If you want a focused external-only device, an air-pulse stimulator, or something compact, this is the wrong format.

Documented Specifications #

We-Vibe’s specification block describes a dual stimulator: a flexible external arm for constant clitoral contact and an internal G-spot shaft that adjusts to the body. The body is body-safe silicone, explicitly free of phthalates, BPA and latex. It measures 217 x 85 x 39 mm, which is large for an insertable, and the documented colour is Fuchsia, though it is also sold in a blue finish.

Power is a USB-rechargeable lithium-ion battery giving up to 120 minutes of use from a charge of up to 90 minutes. Control is on-device plus the We-Connect app. We-Vibe does not publish a maximum noise level or a specific number of vibration modes in the specification block, so both are items to confirm if they matter to you.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

Nova 2 uses body-safe silicone with an explicit phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free statement, which is a clearer materials disclosure than many rabbits give. It is rated 100% waterproof to IPX7, so it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and is safe for bath or shower use. As with any rabbit, the seam where the external arm meets the shaft and the area around the base controls need the most attention, since lubricant can collect there; rinse thoroughly and dry fully before storage. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone.

Battery and Warranty #

Nova 2 is rechargeable over USB, with a charge of up to 90 minutes giving up to two hours of use. We-Vibe documents a two-year warranty plus its 100-day pleasure guarantee. The two-year term is modest for a 149 EUR device, so weigh it if long warranty cover matters to you.

In the Catalogue #

Nova 2 vs the other rabbit #

The catalogue’s only other rabbit is the Lovehoney Desire at 69.99 EUR, which is cheaper but whose materials, waterproofing and warranty were not documented by the manufacturer when we reviewed it. Nova 2 costs more than twice as much, but it is fully documented and adds app control and the adaptive external arm, which is why it rates higher.

Nova 2 among the internal options #

For internal stimulation without the external arm, the LELO Gigi 2 and LELO Mona are the dedicated G-spot and firm-curve picks, and the LELO Enigma is the dual sonic-and-G-spot device. All are ranked on the best G-spot & internal vibrators page.

Nova 2 among the We-Vibe range #

Within We-Vibe, Nova 2 is the rabbit, where the Chorus and Sync are worn couples wearables, the Melt is the contactless air-pulse device and the Tango X is a compact bullet. The full line-up is on the We-Vibe reviews hub.

Value Position #

At a 149 EUR manufacturer list price, shown at 119 EUR when we checked, Nova 2 sits in the mid-to-upper tier for a rabbit. It is not a value-first pick against the Lovehoney Desire, but it is a far better-documented one, with app control and an external arm built to solve the rabbit’s usual contact problem. The reason to buy it is specific: you want dual G-spot and clitoral stimulation in one device, from a brand with a clear body-safe specification.
