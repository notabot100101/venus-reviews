Gentle, slower-build stimulation

LELO Sila is the gentle entry in LELO’s sonic range: the same non-contact sonic-wave category as Sona 2, tuned for a slower, softer build rather than maximum intensity. In the Venus catalogue it answers a request the existing pages do not: an air-pulse-family product specifically positioned for users who find the standard intensity curves too aggressive.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This draft is based on LELO’s own product page (lelo.com/sila, checked 6 August 2026) and the product’s relationship to the already-reviewed Sona 2. Where a detail is not documented in that capture, this page says so.

Quick Verdict #

Sila is the considered recommendation for sensitivity-first shoppers: LELO’s own positioning is “the gentle clitoral vibrator”, with a wide mouth that spreads its sonic waves over a larger area than Sona 2’s focused opening. At a verified 169 EUR it costs more than Sona 2 (109 EUR) — the premium buys the gentler tuning and larger contact area, not more power. Buyers chasing intensity should buy Sona 2 and save 60 EUR.

Who It Is For #

Who Should Look Elsewhere #

Sona 2 delivers LELO’s standard sonic intensity at 109 EUR; if gentleness is not the specific requirement, it is the better value in the same family.
