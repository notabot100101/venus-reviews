LELO Tor 3 is the catalogue’s first vibrating couples’ ring: a stretchy silicone ring worn by one partner during sex, with a vibrating control body sitting against both partners. It fills a gap that the wearable couples toys do not, a simpler, single-piece ring rather than an app-heavy worn device.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO’s official Tor 3 product page and its published specification block, checked on 5 October 2026. Where LELO does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Tor 3 is the pick for couples who want shared vibration in the simplest wearable format. The specifications are strong and fully documented: body-safe silicone over ABS, 100% waterproofing, a 130 Hz motor, a 60 dB maximum noise figure, and roughly two hours of use from a two-hour charge. The LELO app adds customizable patterns on top of the two physical buttons. The caveats are inherent to the format rather than flaws: a ring is fit-sensitive, it is a partnered-use device rather than a solo all-rounder, and at a 159 EUR manufacturer list price it is a specialist purchase.

Format and Who It Is For #

Most of the couples products people consider are worn internally-and-externally, like the We-Vibe wearables. Tor 3 is different: it is a ring, worn at the base, with a single vibrating node positioned to contact both partners during sex. That makes it mechanically simpler and less dependent on precise internal placement, but it is still fit-sensitive, the node has to sit where both partners benefit.

It is squarely a partnered-use product. Unlike an external stimulator or an internal vibrator, its whole design assumes two people, so it is not the device to buy as a first solo toy.

Documented Specifications #

LELO’s specification block lists body-safe silicone with ABS plastic and a smooth finish. The ring measures 46.7 x 22.2 x 60 mm with a 29 mm internal diameter. The motor runs at 130 Hz with a documented maximum noise level of 60 dB, and the device switches off automatically after 20 minutes.

Power is a Li-Ion 3.7 V cell rated at 70 mAh, charged in up to two hours at 5.0 V, giving up to two hours of user time and up to 90 days of standby. The interface is two physical buttons, with the LELO app layering on additional customizable vibration settings.

The 60 dB maximum is honest but audible in a quiet room, the same figure LELO documents for the Sona 2. The two-hour runtime is average for the catalogue rather than a standout.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

LELO marks Tor 3 as 100% waterproof, which makes cleaning straightforward: rinse under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner, then dry fully before storage. The body-safe silicone over the contact areas is non-porous and appropriate for intimate use when cleaned correctly, and LELO states ISO 3533 body-safe certification. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with silicone.

Battery and Warranty #

Tor 3 is rechargeable over USB, with the two-hour charge matching its two-hour runtime and a long 90-day standby that suits occasional use. LELO documents a two-year warranty from the purchase date; buyers who want LELO’s longer registration-based extension should confirm the current terms on the warranty page before relying on it.

In the Catalogue #

Tor 3 vs. the We-Vibe wearables #

The We-Vibe Chorus and We-Vibe Sync are worn couples toys with a C-shape and a heavier app-and-remote layer. Tor 3 is the simpler alternative: a ring rather than a worn internal-external device, with fewer moving parts to position. If you want the richest control ecosystem, the Chorus leads; if you want the simplest shared-vibration format, Tor 3 is the cleaner choice.

Tor 3 among the LELO range #

Within LELO, Tor 3 is the dedicated couples product. The Enigma is the dual solo device, the Sona 2 and Sila are external stimulators, and the Hugo is the prostate pick. The full line-up is on the LELO reviews hub.

Value Position #

At a 159 EUR manufacturer list price, shown discounted to 124.02 EUR when we checked, Tor 3 sits in LELO’s mid tier. It is not a value-first pick, and it is not a do-everything device. The reason to buy it is specific: a couple who want shared vibration in the simplest wearable ring, from a brand with a fully documented, body-safe specification. Buyers after a solo toy should start elsewhere in the catalogue.

  • 60 dB maximum is audible in a quiet room, the same figure as the Sona 2
  • 159 EUR manufacturer list price for a single-purpose couples device
  • Documented user time of ~2 hours is average rather than a standout
