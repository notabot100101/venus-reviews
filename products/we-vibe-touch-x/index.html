We-Vibe Touch X is a compact lay-on clitoral vibrator: a palm-sized, slightly curved body with a soft, angled tip that rests against the external anatomy rather than being inserted. We-Vibe calls it a mini vibrator and a multitasker, and it is the brand’s simplest solo device, with physical controls, a lock function and no app.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on We-Vibe’s official Touch X product page and its published specification, feature and in-the-box tabs, checked on 12 October 2026. Where We-Vibe does not document something clearly, this page says so rather than filling the gap.

Quick Verdict #

Touch X is the pick for someone who wants a small, direct external vibrator with real documentation and no app to set up. The specification covers what matters: body-safe silicone that is explicitly free of phthalates, BPA and latex, IPX7 waterproofing, exact dimensions of 102 x 43 x 34 mm, 8 intensity levels and 7 vibration patterns, a runtime of up to two hours from a 90-minute charge, a lock function for travel, and a 2-year warranty. The gaps are a missing weight and no noise figure behind the whisper-quiet claim. At 99 EUR it is priced just above the budget tier and above the We-Vibe Tango X, the brand’s bullet.

Format and Who It Is For #

A lay-on vibrator is held in the palm and pressed against the clitoris and surrounding area, so the contact is broader than a bullet’s tip but far smaller than a wand’s head. Touch X has a curved body with a tapered, flexible tip, so it can be used flat for broad contact or on the tip for a more focused point. Controls are buttons on the body, cycling through the 8 levels and 7 patterns; a lock function keeps it from switching on in a bag, and the lock releases automatically while charging.

It suits people who want simple, direct external stimulation without an app, and people who travel with a toy and want the lock. It is also a natural partner toy held by hand during sex, which is how We-Vibe frames its multitasker label. It is not the pick for someone who wants internal stimulation, app or remote control, or the deep, broad massage of a wand such as the We-Vibe Wand 2.

Documented Specifications #

We-Vibe’s specification tab lists the Touch X in Crave Coral and Green Velvet, with a USB-charged lithium-ion battery, a runtime of up to two hours and a charge time of 90 minutes. The materials are body-safe silicone, free of phthalates and BPA and made without latex. The body measures 10.22 x 4.33 x 3.43 cm, recorded here as 102 x 43 x 34 mm, and the warranty is 2 years.

The feature tab adds 8 intensity levels with 7 vibration patterns, IPX7 waterproofing, the lock function and a whisper-quiet description without a decibel figure. The in-the-box tab lists the Touch X, a USB cable with no adapter, an illustrated multilingual manual and a silky storage pouch. We-Vibe does not publish a weight or a noise level for the Touch X, and lists no app or remote control for it.

Materials, Waterproofing and Cleaning #

The contact surface is body-safe silicone with an explicit phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free statement. Touch X is rated IPX7, so it can be rinsed under running water with mild soap or a compatible toy cleaner and used in the bath or shower within the limits of that rating. A smooth lay-on body is among the easiest formats to clean; dry it fully before it goes back into the pouch. Water-based lubricant is the conservative default with a silicone toy.

Battery and Warranty #

The documented figures are up to two hours of use from a 90-minute charge, charged over a USB cable from any USB port or adapter. The lock function is a practical detail for travel and is released automatically when the device is plugged in to charge. The warranty is 2 years, and We-Vibe shows its 100-day pleasure guarantee site-wide; confirm the current terms before relying on either.

In the Catalogue #

Touch X vs. the Tango X #

The We-Vibe Tango X is the brand’s bullet at 89 EUR, with 8 intensity levels and 7 vibration modes of its own and IPX7. The difference is shape: the Tango X is a slim pinpoint bullet, the Touch X a wider lay-on body with a flexible tip for broader contact. Both document the same level and mode counts; Touch X adds exact dimensions and the lock function, the Tango X costs 10 EUR less and is the more pocketable of the two.

Touch X vs. the other small external vibrators #

The LELO Lily 3 is the closest rival in format and price: a 45 g lay-on vibrator with 10 settings, a documented 60 dB maximum and 100% waterproofing at 99 EUR. LELO publishes a weight and a noise figure where We-Vibe does not; We-Vibe publishes a lock function and a split of levels and patterns where LELO does not. The Fun Factory Volta and Dame Pom are the other handheld externals, with different shapes rather than different specs.

All six compact vibrators are ranked on the best mini and bullet vibrators page.

Touch X among the We-Vibe range #

Touch X is the entry to We-Vibe’s solo external line. The Tango X is the bullet, the Melt the air-pulse device, the Wand 2 the wand and the Moxie+ the wearable; unlike those, the Touch X has no app. The full line-up is on the We-Vibe reviews hub.

Value Position #

At 99 EUR, Touch X sits just above the budget tier and 10 EUR above the Tango X. The reason to buy it is specific: a documented, IPX7, body-safe lay-on vibrator with a lock function and a 2-year warranty, for someone who wants broader contact than a bullet without an app. The reasons to hesitate are the undocumented weight and noise and the lack of any remote or app control.
