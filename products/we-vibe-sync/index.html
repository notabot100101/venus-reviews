We-Vibe Sync is the simpler adjustable couples option next to Chorus. It is still a connected wearable vibrator, but the live manufacturer page presents it more plainly: adjustable fit, remote control, waterproof IPX7 construction, USB charging, app control, and a compact C-shaped body for shared stimulation.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on We-Vibe’s live Sync product page, visible manufacturer specification and feature tabs, the current Venus catalogue, and patterns in published user feedback. Where a detail is not documented clearly by the manufacturer, the review says so.

Quick Verdict #

Sync is best for couples who want an adjustable wearable vibrator with remote and app control but do not need Chorus’s richer Squeeze Remote and Touch-sense system. We-Vibe documents the core practical specs: waterproof IPX7, eco-friendly USB rechargeable battery, up to 90 minutes runtime, 2-hour charge time, body-safe silicone exterior, 74.8 x 31.4 x 41.7 mm dimensions, and a two-year warranty. The caution is that Sync appears out of stock on the live manufacturer page during this review pass, so retailer availability should be checked carefully.

Documented Specifications #

Per We-Vibe’s live Sync product page at https://www.we-vibe.com/us/sync, Sync is described as fitting snugly to the body for clitoral and G-spot stimulation, with use during sex and control via remote or app. The feature section documents adjustable fit and says Sync can be adjusted to the body so vibrations land where wanted. It also documents remote control and Waterproof IPX7.

The in-the-box section lists the Sync couples vibrator, USB cable, quick-start user guide, wireless remote control, and a 2 ml We-Vibe Lube made by pjur sample. The USB cable is described as charging through any USB port or widely available USB power adapter, with the adapter not included.

The visible specification tab lists color as Purple, battery as eco-friendly USB rechargeable, runtime as up to 90 minutes, charge time as 2 hours, materials as body-safe silicone exterior, dimensions as 74.8 mm x 31.4 mm x 41.7 mm, and warranty as two year. The page also shows vibration modes including Low, Medium, High, Pulse, Wave, Ultra, Echo, Tide, and Surf.

Exact hard internal materials beyond the silicone exterior, battery chemistry, battery capacity, decibel level, remote range, app platform requirements, and precise IPX7 limitations were not documented clearly in the visible manufacturer specification table.

Materials and Cleaning Analysis #

Sync’s manufacturer materials statement is narrower than Chorus’s. We-Vibe documents a body-safe silicone exterior, but the visible Sync spec table does not clearly identify the internal hard plastic or whether it is produced without phthalates, BPA, or latex. That does not mean the product lacks those qualities; it means Venus should not claim them for Sync unless a current manufacturer source states them.

The waterproof IPX7 claim is useful for care. A wearable C-shaped couples vibrator has two contact arms and a bend area, so cleaning should be careful around transitions and any seams. The remote should be treated separately unless its own water-resistance rating is documented. For Sync, the live product feature text documents the toy as waterproof, not a remote waterproof rating.

As with other silicone-contact products, water-based lubricant is the conservative recommendation unless We-Vibe’s instructions for Sync and the chosen lubricant say otherwise. Dry the toy fully before charging or storing, and avoid storing silicone surfaces loose against lint-heavy fabric.

Published Feedback Synthesis #

Published feedback around Sync-type couples vibrators usually centers on adjustability. Buyers who like the product often value being able to bend or position it more naturally than older fixed wearable shapes. Remote and app control are also meaningful positives when partners want shared control or long-distance experimentation.

The negative pattern is fit and stability. Even adjustable couples toys can move, press in the wrong place, or feel intrusive depending on anatomy and position. Some users also find that app control adds complexity without improving the experience; for those buyers, a simpler remote toy or external vibrator may feel more practical.

The live manufacturer page’s out-of-stock state is also a buying signal. If Sync is being phased out or replaced in some markets, support, accessories, and retailer availability may vary. That does not make the product unusable, but it raises the importance of buying through a retailer with clear return and warranty handling.

Catalogue Comparisons #

Within the Venus catalogue, Sync is the more straightforward We-Vibe couples pick, while Chorus is the higher-feature option. Chorus documents ABS plus body-safe silicone, phthalate/BPA/latex-free language, Squeeze Remote, Touch-sense, AnkorLink, and a charging case. Sync documents the simpler adjustable form, app/remote control, IPX7, and a smaller dimensions table.

Compared with LELO Enigma, Sync is more partnered and wearable, while Enigma is more of a premium solo dual-stimulation product. Compared with LELO Sona 2 or Womanizer Premium 2, Sync is not the best choice for focused solo external stimulation. Compared with LELO Hugo, Sync is a couples vaginal/clitoral product, not a prostate massager.

Compared with Dame Eva II, Sync is more feature-rich because it adds remote/app control and a C-shaped insertable design. Eva-style products are better when the buyer wants a smaller external wearable; Sync is better when internal positioning and partner control are part of the brief.

Who Should Buy It #

Choose Sync if partnered use is the priority, adjustable fit matters, and remote/app control sounds useful rather than distracting. It is a reasonable shortlist product for couples who want a less elaborate We-Vibe option than Chorus and are comfortable checking current availability through a traceable retailer.

Skip it if you want a simple solo device, dislike app-controlled products, need detailed material disclosures beyond body-safe silicone exterior, or want published noise and remote-range specs before buying. Also skip it if the product’s out-of-stock state on the manufacturer page makes warranty or replacement confidence feel uncertain.

Ownership Notes #

Keep the USB cable and remote with the toy; losing either can make a connected couples device much less useful. Save proof of purchase for the two-year warranty, especially if buying from a third-party retailer while the manufacturer page is out of stock. Before travel, check the user manual for lock behavior and app setup steps, because a travel lock was not documented clearly in the visible specification table.
