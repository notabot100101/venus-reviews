Womanizer Premium 2 vs Satisfyer Pro 2 #

Both of these are pressure-wave stimulators, often called air-pulse: they pulse air against the clitoris rather than vibrating against it. The question is almost never which is “better” in the abstract, but whether you want the premium version or the value one. Here is how our two reviews line up.

Womanizer Premium 2 Satisfyer Pro 2
Our rating 4.5 3.5
Manufacturer price 179.00 EUR 69.95 EUR
Intensity 14 levels, gradual ladder 11 pressure-wave + 12 vibration programs
Noise Smart Silence, quieter in reports Louder in published reports
Runtime ~240 min per 120 min charge ~60 min, slower ~3h charge
Warranty 5 years 15 years (documented)

Where the money goes #

The Premium 2 earns its price on the things you feel over time, not on the core sensation. Smart Silence activates the motor only when it is against skin, which is a real discretion advantage. The 240-minute runtime is the best ratio in the catalogue, the intensity ladder is long and gradual, and the materials carry an explicit phthalate-free, BPA-free and latex-free statement. Its weaknesses are that it does one thing only, no internal or app features, and the price is simply high.

The Satisfyer Pro 2 is the best-value way into pressure-wave at under 70 EUR. It ships with an IPX7 waterproof body and two attachments, and its documented 15-year warranty is the longest in our catalogue. The trade-offs are an honest modest runtime, a louder motor, and a fiddlier interface.

Which to buy #

Buy the Satisfyer Pro 2 if you have never tried pressure-wave and want to find out whether it works for you without spending much. Buy the Womanizer Premium 2 if you already know you like air-pulse and want the quiet operation, long runtime and warranty that justify the step up.

Both are external-only. If you want air-pulse in a different format, the LELO Sona 2 uses sonic waves instead, and our best-of 2026 list ranks every pick. New to this entirely? Start with the first-time buyer guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the main difference between them?

Both use pressure-wave (air-pulse) stimulation, but the Womanizer Premium 2 is the premium device at 179 EUR with Smart Silence, a 240-minute runtime and a five-year warranty, while the Satisfyer Pro 2 is the value entry at 69.95 EUR with a shorter runtime and a louder motor.

Which is quieter?

The Womanizer Premium 2. Its Smart Silence feature only activates the motor on skin contact, and published reports rate it quieter than the Satisfyer Pro 2.

Is the Satisfyer Pro 2 good enough on its own?

Yes, as a first pressure-wave device. It is the inexpensive way to find out whether air-pulse stimulation suits you at all, with a documented 15-year warranty. We rate it 3.5 against the Premium 2's 4.5.
