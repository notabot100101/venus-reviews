LELO Hugo 2 vs LELO Loki Wave 2 #

These are LELO’s two current prostate massagers, both in stock, both with two motors, both 100% waterproof with a documented noise ceiling under 60 dB. The Hugo 2 is the connected, compact one: app control, a smaller body, a one-hour runtime. The Loki Wave 2 is the mechanical one: a WaveMotion tip that moves against the prostate rather than only vibrating, more settings, a longer insertable length and a two-hour runtime, for 40 EUR more.

What each one does better #

The Hugo 2 is the connected pick. It pairs with the LELO app over Bluetooth, which is where six of its ten settings live, and it is the smaller and lighter of the two at 104 x 107 x 41 mm and 121 g with a 72 mm insertable length. It is also 40 EUR cheaper. Its weaknesses are a documented runtime of only one hour from a two-hour charge, and the fact that most of its settings need a phone.

The Loki Wave 2 is the mechanical pick. Its WaveMotion tip rises and falls in a finger-like motion while the external arm vibrates, each driven by its own motor, and LELO documents twelve settings on the device itself with five buttons, no phone required. It runs twice as long, two hours from a two-hour charge, and inserts deeper at 100 mm. It is larger and heavier at 196 g, costs 219 EUR, and has no app.

Which to buy #

Choose the Hugo 2 if you want app control, a compact body and the lower price, and a one-hour session is enough. Choose the Loki Wave 2 if you want the moving tip, all settings on the device, the longer runtime and the deeper reach, and the extra 40 EUR is acceptable.

The original LELO Hugo was out of stock at the manufacturer when last checked, and the We-Vibe Vector+ is the cheaper adjustable alternative; all of them, plus the We-Vibe Ditto+ plug, are ranked with the safety basics on the best prostate massagers page.
