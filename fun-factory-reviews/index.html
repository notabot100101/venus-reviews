How to choose a Fun Factory #

For broad, deep massage, the VIM is the weighted-motor wand: its heavier motor delivers the rumbly, low-frequency sensation wand users look for, over a broad contact head rather than a point.

For precise contact, the Volta uses two flexible tips that can cradle or pinpoint, a more exploratory feel than a single flat head, at the lowest 4.0-rated price in the range.

For a flexible wraparound shape, the Manta bends around contours and is the most affordable of the four, though its 3.5 rating reflects a more niche shape that suits some users better than others.

For hands-free internal thrusting, the Stronic G is the odd one out: a pulsator that thrusts by itself rather than vibrating, built for people whose G-spot responds to motion rather than buzz. It is the only thrusting device in the catalogue, ranked among the insertables on the best G-spot & internal vibrators page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Fun Factory toy is the best overall?

Two share our top Fun Factory rating of 4.0: the VIM weighted-motor wand at 66.95 EUR for deep, rumbly external massage, and the Volta twin-tip stimulator at 51.95 EUR for precise external contact. The right pick depends on whether you want broad wand coverage or pinpoint tips.

What is the cheapest Fun Factory you recommend?

The Manta at 48.95 EUR is the least expensive, a flexible wraparound external stimulator. We rate it 3.5. The Volta at 51.95 EUR is only slightly more and rates 4.0.

Has Venus tested these Fun Factory products?

No. Every Fun Factory review is based on Fun Factory's official product pages and structured data, with the date we checked each one stated on the page. We do not claim to have physically tested them.
