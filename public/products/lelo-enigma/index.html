Lelo Enigma is the premium dual-stimulation pick in this Venus set: a luxury sonic-and-internal vibrator for shoppers who already know they want a shaped, fit-sensitive device rather than a simple beginner toy. Its strongest case is not that it is universal. Its strongest case is that LELO documents the materials, dimensions, battery, charging, warranty, and sonic/internal design clearly enough for careful comparison.

Venus has not physically tested this product. This review is based on LELO’s live Enigma product page, LELO’s published warranty and ISO 3533 language, the current Venus catalogue, and patterns in published user feedback. Where a detail is not documented clearly by the manufacturer, the review says so.

Quick Verdict #

Enigma is best for experienced shoppers who want simultaneous external sonic stimulation and insertable G-spot vibration in a polished rechargeable body. The product is easier to justify when dual stimulation is already the goal and the buyer values a documented silicone/ABS build, waterproof construction, and LELO’s warranty path. It is harder to recommend as a first toy, a value buy, or a casual experiment, because the shape, price tier, and sensation style all ask for confidence.

Documented Specifications #

Per LELO’s live Enigma product page at https://www.lelo.com/enigma, Enigma is described as a dual-action sonic massager for clitoral and G-spot stimulation. LELO says it uses SenSonic technology on the exterior portion and internal vibration for the G-spot. The documented materials are body-safe silicone and ABS plastic, with listed colors of Deep Rose and Black.

LELO lists the size as 100 x 50 x 185 mm, or 3.9 x 1.9 x 7.3 in. Insertable length is documented as 140 mm, or 5.5 in. Weight is documented as 196 g, or 0.43 lb. The interface is listed as a 3-button interface, and automatic switch-off is listed after 20 minutes.

The battery is documented as a Li-ion 850 mAh, 3.7 V battery. LELO lists charging as 2 hours at 5.0 V / 750 mA, user time as up to 2 hours, standby as up to 90 days, and frequency as 60 Hz / 100 Hz. The page also lists Enigma as 100% waterproof and extra-soft silicone, and says the box includes the Enigma, a USB-charging cord, satin storage pouch, warranty registration card, and instruction manual.

LELO’s warranty language is unusually explicit: a 2-year warranty from purchase date for problems that can lead to replacement, plus a 10-year warranty period after the 2-year warranty expires. Noise level, exact motor count, app compatibility, and phthalate-free status were not documented in the visible LELO Enigma specification table.

Materials and Cleaning Analysis #

The material story is one of Enigma’s clearer advantages. LELO documents body-safe silicone and ABS plastic, and the same page presents the silicone as extra-soft. For buyers comparing premium devices, that is a stronger signal than generic “premium feel” language because it identifies the main surface material and the structural plastic.

LELO also states that its pleasure devices meet ISO 3533, the international safety standard for sex toys. That does not settle whether Enigma will fit a particular body, but it gives the materials claim more substance than a retailer listing alone.

Cleaning should be simpler than with splashproof devices because LELO presents Enigma as 100% waterproof. The shape still deserves patience: dual-stimulation toys have curves, contact points, and transitions between sections that can trap lubricant or lint if rushed. A conservative care routine is to use water-based lubricant, clean the silicone surface according to LELO’s instructions, let the toy dry fully before storage, and keep it away from sharp accessories. Silicone lubricant compatibility should not be assumed unless LELO’s instructions for the exact product and lubricant confirm it.

Published Feedback Synthesis #

Published feedback around Enigma-style LELO products tends to be enthusiastic when the buyer already wants focused dual stimulation. Positive themes usually center on premium finish, strong sensation contrast between external sonic stimulation and internal vibration, and the feeling that the device is more refined than a basic rabbit shape. The documented 140 mm insertable length and 196 g weight also help explain why some buyers perceive it as substantial rather than compact.

The negative pattern is fit sensitivity. A dual-stimulation product has to line up with two target areas at once, so even a well-designed shape will not suit every anatomy. Some published buyer comments for this category praise the alignment; others say the external arm, insertable curve, or firmness misses the mark. That is not a defect by itself, but it matters more at this price tier.

Another recurring caution is intensity style. Sonic stimulation can feel gentler, deeper, or simply different from direct vibration depending on the user. Buyers who want broad external pressure may prefer a wand or a larger external vibrator, while buyers who want suction-style stimulation may be better served by a Womanizer or LELO Sona-style product. Enigma should be framed as a specific premium dual-stimulation device, not as a universal upgrade.

Catalogue Comparisons #

Within the Venus catalogue, Enigma is the more advanced LELO option compared with Mona 2. Mona 2 is simpler: a curved internal G-spot vibrator with a more traditional purpose. Enigma adds external sonic stimulation and a dual-contact shape, making it more ambitious but also more dependent on fit.

Compared with LELO Sona 2, Enigma is larger, insertable, and dual-purpose. Sona 2 is the cleaner choice for someone who only wants external sonic stimulation. Compared with LELO Hugo, Enigma is in a different category: Hugo is prostate-focused and remote-controlled, while Enigma is designed for clitoral and G-spot stimulation.

Compared with We-Vibe Chorus or We-Vibe Sync, Enigma is less couples-positioning focused and does not have the same app/remote ecosystem. We-Vibe is stronger for partnered control and wearability; Enigma is stronger for a premium solo dual-stimulation shortlist. Compared with Womanizer Premium 2, Enigma is more physically shaped and insertable, while Womanizer is more focused on external air stimulation.

Who Should Buy It #

Choose Enigma if you already like dual-stimulation designs, want a premium silicone/ABS product, value a clear warranty path, and prefer a rechargeable waterproof device with documented dimensions and battery details. It is also a sensible upgrade for someone who tried a lower-cost rabbit-style toy and wants a more refined, less generic version.

Skip it if you are new to insertable toys, unsure about dual stimulation, need app control, want the smallest travel option, or prefer broad external pressure. Also skip it if exact noise data is central to the purchase decision; LELO documents many specs for Enigma, but not a max noise level in the visible specification table.

Ownership Notes #

Buy through a traceable retailer and keep the receipt. LELO’s 2-year and later 10-year warranty language is only useful if the purchase date and seller can be documented. Charge before storage, avoid leaving the battery depleted for long periods, and use the satin pouch so the silicone surface does not sit loose against lint, dust, or sharp items in a drawer.
