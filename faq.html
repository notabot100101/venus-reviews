FAQ

Is this investment advice?

No. We publish what our software did on testnet and on three small real-money bots. We do not recommend trades or pairs to you.

Which exchange?

Binance, testnet and live. Other exchanges are not supported.

Can you run the bot on my account?

No. Running a bot on someone else's account is a regulated financial service in Germany. You run the software yourself, with your own keys.

Why publish the losers?

Because a gate only means something if you can see what failed it. Overfit warnings and zero-fill weeks stay in the archive.

Why no live return figure?

Because we do not have a production-validated one yet. Testnet has no slippage and no real liquidity. When we have live numbers, they will be published as fills and realised PnL, not as a percentage promise.

How do I cancel?

Newsletter (once it exists): the link in every mail. Paid tiers: the customer portal of the payment provider, any time.

Where does my email go?

Right now, nowhere: the signup is offline until the privacy notice covers it. Once it is live, your address is stored only after you confirm it by double opt-in, and we do not sell or share it. The provider will be named in the privacy notice before signup opens. Details in the privacy notice.

This site documents software we build and test. It is not investment advice, we do not manage anyone's money, and testnet results do not predict live performance. Crypto trading can lose your whole stake.
