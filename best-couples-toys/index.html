Best Couples’ Toys #

These are the devices built for two people at once: worn by one partner during sex so both feel the stimulation, or held in place to free up hands. It is a format where fit and anatomy matter more than with a solo toy, so the right pick depends as much on how it is worn as on the spec sheet. Here are the five we have reviewed, ranked by our rating with the verified manufacturer price.

How to pick within the category #

If you want the most complete, best-controlled wearable: the We-Vibe Chorus. Its Squeeze Remote responds to grip pressure, the touch-sense feature adjusts to movement, and it carries an explicit phthalate-, BPA- and latex-free statement. It earns the top couples rating despite being the priciest at 209 EUR.

If you want the simplest shared-vibration format: the LELO Tor 3. A vibrating ring worn at the base is mechanically simpler than a worn internal-external device and easier to position, and the Tor 3 is documented as 100% waterproof with app control on top of its two buttons.

If you want a worn device with control at range: the LELO Tiani 3. Its SenseMotion remote is motion-sensitive with a documented 12 m wireless range, and at a documented 50 dB it is the quietest here. Note LELO does not document a waterproof rating for it.

If you want the same wearable format for less: the We-Vibe Sync. It does the core couples job for 50 EUR under the Chorus, with a narrower materials disclosure, and it was out of stock on the manufacturer page when we checked, so confirm availability first.

If you want hands-free rather than worn-internally: the Dame Eva II. It tucks its flexible wings under the labia to sit against the clitoris, so nothing is inserted, which suits couples who find worn wearables uncomfortable.

The head-to-heads worth reading: We-Vibe Chorus vs We-Vibe Sync for the two We-Vibe wearables, and LELO Tor 3 vs LELO Tiani 3 for LELO’s ring against its worn massager. If this is a first purchase, start with the first-time buyer guide; for the whole catalogue, see the best-of 2026 ranking.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a couples' toy?

A device designed to be used by two people together, usually worn by one partner during penetrative sex so both feel it. The main formats are worn wearables (a C-shape held internally and externally), vibrating rings worn at the base, and hands-free external stimulators held in place during sex.

Which couples' toy is the best overall?

The We-Vibe Chorus holds our top couples rating at 4.5 for its Squeeze Remote, touch-sense control and clear materials disclosure, though at 209 EUR it is the most expensive. The LELO Tor 3 and LELO Tiani 3 both rate 4.0 at around 150 to 160 EUR.

What is the cheapest couples' toy you recommend?

The Dame Eva II at 129 EUR is the least expensive here, a hands-free clitoral stimulator that tucks under the labia rather than being worn internally. We rate it 3.5.
