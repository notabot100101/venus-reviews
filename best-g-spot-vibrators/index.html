This is the insertable category: devices designed for internal stimulation, usually with a firm curve or angled tip to hold pressure against the G-spot. It is where shape and firmness matter more than raw power, so the right pick depends on how targeted you want the sensation. How to pick within the category #

If you want external and internal stimulation at once: the LELO Enigma. It pairs SenSonic external stimulation with internal G-spot vibration in one 100% waterproof body, which is what earns it the top rating. It is a dual device rather than a pure G-spot toy, so it is the pick when you want both.

If you want a dedicated, travel-friendly G-spot device: the LELO Gigi 2. Its flattened tip is a genuine design difference for broad front-wall contact, it is light at 79 g with a documented insertable 95 mm and 100% waterproofing, and it runs up to 4 hours per charge. LELO does not publish a noise figure for it.

If you want a firmer, more pronounced curve: the LELO Mona. A longer, firmer body with a purposeful curve for targeted internal pressure, 100% waterproof with a documented 50 dB maximum. It is aimed at people who already like targeted G-spot toys rather than absolute beginners.

If you want a well-documented, app-controlled rabbit: the We-Vibe Nova 2. Its flexible external arm is built to keep clitoral contact as the device moves, it is 100% waterproof with a clear body-safe materials statement, and the We-Connect app adds remote control. It is the rabbit to pick when documentation and features matter.

If vibration is not what your G-spot responds to: the Fun Factory Stronic G. It is a pulsator, not a vibrator: it thrusts by itself, hands-free, for people whose G-spot wants pressure and motion rather than buzz. It is the only thrusting device in the catalogue, so it is a sensation choice rather than a spec comparison.

If you want a value rabbit: the Lovehoney Desire. A mainstream dual-motor rabbit at the lowest price here, but several specifications were not documented by the manufacturer when we reviewed it, so treat materials, waterproofing and warranty as items to confirm on the retailer page before buying.

What makes a vibrator good for the G-spot?

The LELO Enigma at 199 EUR holds the top rating of 4.5 because it adds external sonic stimulation to internal G-spot vibration. For a dedicated single-purpose G-spot device, the LELO Gigi 2 at 109 EUR is the focused pick at 4.0.

What is the cheapest internal vibrator you recommend?

The Lovehoney Desire rabbit at 69.99 EUR is the cheapest, rated 3.0, but several of its specifications were not documented by the manufacturer when reviewed, so confirm materials, waterproofing and warranty on the retailer page first.
